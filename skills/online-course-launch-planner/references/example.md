# Online Course Launch Planner — Worked Example

A complete walkthrough of planning a first-time course launch using this skill, from intake through post-launch debrief template.

---

## Example: Freelance Designer Launching a Figma Course

### Creator Context

- **Creator:** Priya Mehta, senior product designer with 8 years of experience
- **Audience:** Junior designers (0–3 years), mostly on LinkedIn and Twitter/X (~14,000 combined followers)
- **Course topic:** "Figma for Product Designers: Systems, Not Screens"
- **Launch goal:** $15,000 in first-cohort revenue, 60 students
- **Current stage:** Outline drafted, 2 of 8 lessons recorded, sales page not started
- **Target timeline:** 21-day launch ending with a 5-day live Q&A week

### Input Given

```
Course: Figma for Product Designers: Systems, Not Screens
Audience: Junior product designers, LinkedIn/Twitter, ~14K followers
Goal: $15K revenue, 60 students, first cohort
Stage: Outline done, 2/8 lessons recorded, no sales page yet
Timeline: 21 days, ending with 5-day live Q&A
Previous launches: None
Email list: 1,800 subscribers, ~32% open rate
```

### Full Output

#### 1. Pre-Launch Readiness Audit

**Ready:**
- Core curriculum outline is locked
- Audience concentration on LinkedIn is strong — one-channel launches are easier to execute well
- Email list of 1,800 at 32% open is a healthy anchor (~575 engaged opens per send)

**Missing — must fix before launch week:**
- Sales page copy and checkout (blocks every acquisition channel)
- 6 of 8 lessons unrecorded — do not try to record during launch week
- No testimonials or social proof — run a free 3-person beta this week for quote capture
- Refund policy not written
- Welcome/onboarding email sequence not written

**Risks that could derail launch:**
- Recording lessons during launch week splits attention between marketing and production — highest single risk
- No bonus offer defined — tier pricing will feel flat without one
- No cart-close urgency mechanic — 21 days is long, conversions cluster at the deadline

#### 2. Pricing Recommendation

**Recommended structure: Tiered with early-bird window**

| Tier | Price | Window | Contents |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early-bird (first 72h) | $249 | Days 1–3 | Full course + live Q&A week + bonus component library |
| Standard | $349 | Days 4–18 | Full course + live Q&A week |
| Last-call | $349 + expiring bonus | Days 19–21 | Standard + "Portfolio review" 1:1 bonus expires at cart close |

**Reasoning:**
- $349 anchors at the low end of the "premium self-serve" band for design courses (typical: $297–$497)
- 60-student target × blended $310 ≈ $18,600, giving ~25% headroom on the $15K goal
- Early-bird $100 discount rewards the warm list without training them to wait for discounts later
- Expiring 1:1 bonus at close is the urgency mechanic that replaces a true price jump

#### 3. 21-Day Launch Timeline

**Week 1 — Seeding (Days 1–7):**
- Day 1: Announcement email + LinkedIn post ("I'm building a course. Here's why, here's who it's for.")
- Day 3: LinkedIn carousel with 1 key concept from Lesson 3 (highest-value teaching moment)
- Day 5: Email — "Here's what I've been building" with behind-the-scenes photo of recording setup
- Day 7: Twitter/X thread — 10-tweet summary of course thesis, link to waitlist signup

**Week 2 — Launch (Days 8–14):**
- Day 8: Cart opens + announcement email + LinkedIn post, early-bird is live
- Day 8–10: 1 LinkedIn post per day, each anchored on a specific lesson outcome
- Day 10 (72h mark): Early-bird closes — send email at 9am and 6pm
- Day 11: Case study post — pick 1 beta tester, share their before/after
- Day 13: Objection-handler email ("Is this right for someone who already uses Figma daily?")
- Day 14: Live Q&A week kickoff — 60-min live on LinkedIn

**Week 3 — Close (Days 15–21):**
- Day 15: Live Q&A session 2, record and distribute
- Day 17: Cart-closing email #1 — "4 days left, bonus expires"
- Day 19: Final live Q&A session, tell attendees cart closes in 48h
- Day 20: Cart-close email — 9am and 6pm
- Day 21: Cart is planned to close at 8pm in the confirmed launch timezone. Draft a "one hour left" reminder for 7pm. Send a "doors closed" notice only after confirming closure; scheduling and sending still require authorization and an available service.

#### 4. Asset Checklist

- Sales page (hero, curriculum, instructor bio, testimonials, pricing, FAQ, guarantee)
- Checkout page with order bump ($29 Figma audit template)
- 3 testimonial quotes from beta cohort (run beta this week)
- Bonus component library (1 deliverable, not 3 — scope tightly)
- 12 emails: announcement, 2 early-bird, 4 mid-launch, 3 cart-close, welcome, onboarding
- 14 LinkedIn posts (one per day of launch, plus 7 seeding-week)
- Refund policy (recommend: 14-day no-questions)
- Welcome email with Lesson 1 access + Discord/Circle invite
- Onboarding checklist PDF

- **Blended ACV:** $______

- **Top 3 objections raised in DMs/email:** ______
- **What I'd keep for next cohort:** ______
- **What I'd cut:** ______

## How to Use This Output

1. Paste the pre-launch audit into your project doc — treat "Missing" items as blockers, not polish
2. Lock pricing tiers in your checkout before writing any sales page copy
3. Calendar-block the 21-day timeline with real dates, including the 9am/6pm send windows
4. Run the beta cohort during the 7-day seeding week — you need quotes by Day 8
5. Schedule the debrief review for Day 22. Do not skip it; the second cohort is where the money is.
