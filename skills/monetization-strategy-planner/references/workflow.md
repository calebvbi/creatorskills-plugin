# Monetization Strategy Planner

Build a practical revenue experiment plan that fits the creator's audience, available time and existing commitments. Separate observed business results from proposed offers and hypothetical scenarios. Do not promise income, forecast from follower count alone or recommend charging for this plugin through an assumed marketplace exception.

## 1. Map the current business

Collect platforms, audience definitions, current offers and prices, recent receipts, fees/refunds/costs, fulfillment hours, capacity, customer promises and goals. Ask for redacted totals. Reconcile gross sales versus payouts and recurring versus one-time receipts. Record the date range and accounting basis. Do not treat views, subscribers or email opens as guaranteed buyers.

## 2. Compare suitable streams

Consider sponsorships, affiliates, digital products, courses, memberships, services, licensing and platform revenue only when they fit the creator's material and capacity. For each, describe the customer need, offer, work required, dependencies, evidence of demand, costs, risks and smallest useful test.

A sponsor rate is a negotiated asking price, not a valuation proved by a CPM table. Affiliate income depends on actual terms and conversions. Courses and products need a support and rights plan. Memberships require ongoing fulfillment. Services have capacity constraints. Verify current official platform eligibility, fees and terms before using them in a calculation or recommending a provider.

Respect existing customers' entitlements, price promises and contracts. Do not change pricing, issue refunds, publish offers or migrate subscriptions from a strategy request. Any external service or marketplace monetization must comply with its verified current terms; no indirect fee permission is assumed.

## 3. Prioritize experiments

Score fit, observed demand, time to learn, cost and operational burden using an explicit qualitative rationale. Avoid unsupported numerical certainty. Start with one or two experiments the creator can fulfill. Define the customer segment, offer, proposed channel, required assets, budget ceiling, observation window and decision criterion.

A launch calendar is a proposed task list, not a record of executed activity. Outreach is drafted, not sent. Price and volume targets are choices to test, not expected market outcomes.

## 4. Model optional scenarios

Ask which assumptions the creator wants to explore. For each stream show units × unit price, gross receipts, known fees/refunds/variable costs and contribution if calculable. Use the correct per-thousand denominator for RPM/CPM. Distinguish billable downloads, subscribers, delivered messages and actual views. Do not subtract an unspecified platform fee and call the result net.

Mark every input as observed, user-selected assumption or unknown. Show sensitivity to unsold inventory, zero new sales, lower demand and fulfillment cost. A base scenario is not an expected forecast. If an input lacks support, show a variable or leave the result unknown rather than filling it to meet a revenue target.

Calculate concentration from actual or explicitly assumed totals, with no universal danger threshold. Discuss what would happen if the largest stream stops and what obligations remain. Email lists, products and affiliates are not guaranteed passive income.

## 5. Build a staged plan

- First stage: validate a specific audience need and assemble one useful offer draft.
- Next stage: run a separately authorized small test with bounded spending and clear measurement.
- Review stage: compare observed demand, costs and time against the assumptions; keep, revise or stop.

Use dates and milestones that match the creator's capacity. Do not assume future earnings will fund reduced employment or hired help. Track gross/net distinctions and actual fulfillment capacity as the business changes.

## 6. Output

Return current-state summary, ranked options, one selected experiment, a cost/capacity checklist, optional scenario table, risk/commitment review and the next action. Preserve uncertainties and avoid invented industry conversion multipliers. No purchase, deployment, billing change or financial forecast is completed by this skill.
