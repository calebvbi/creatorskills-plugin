# Worked Example: Search-Led Drafts With Evidence Limits

## Synthetic input

A creator makes beginner home-recording videos. A budget-microphone comparison has 3,400 views, 7.2% impression CTR and 4:12 average duration on a 7:30 video. A setup walkthrough has 310 views, 2.4% CTR and 1:18 average duration on a 10:05 video. Publication ages, traffic-source-specific metrics, transcripts and search query data are not supplied. The creator asks what to try next.

## Readout

Average percentage viewed is 252/450 = 56% for the comparison and 78/605 ≈ 12.9% for the walkthrough. The comparison has higher aggregate CTR and viewing percentage in this fixture. We cannot tell whether topic, packaging, source mix, video age or content structure explains the difference. Do not declare walkthroughs a failed format from two unequal observations.

## Research and content plan

Candidate questions to verify include how to choose a budget microphone, what changes microphone placement makes and how to connect the creator's actual equipment. No search-volume claim is available. Check accessible search results and real audience questions, recording dates and sources.

Draft title structures, pending the actual video material:

- How to choose a budget microphone for [confirmed use case].
- [Two tested microphones]: what changed in [documented test].
- Set up [actual interface] with [actual microphone].

The second title cannot be used unless the creator really performed the comparison. No brands, prices or test results are invented.

## Description outline

Explain the confirmed setup and intended user in two sentences. List only verified steps and supplied resources. Add a relevant call to action and required disclosures after checking the actual context. With no timed transcript, chapter entries remain [TIMESTAMP NEEDED].

## Next experiment

Obtain comparable video ages/source metrics and the walkthrough retention curve. If the source shows a mismatch between title promise and opening, propose one title or opening change and log its date. Evaluate compatible CTR and viewing measures after a preselected observation window. No expected traffic lift, ranking, completed edit or publication is claimed.
