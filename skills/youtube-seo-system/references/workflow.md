# YouTube Search and Content Review

Build a practical search, packaging and measurement plan from the creator's actual material. Produce keyword research notes, title candidates, a description, retention hypotheses and a monthly review. Do not invent search volume, competitor observations or ranking outcomes.

## 1. Gather the evidence

Ask for niche, intended viewer, video content/transcript, current title/description, publishing constraints and the goal. For a channel review, request a comparable sample with video age, format, length, traffic sources, eligible impressions, CTR and viewing metrics. Read only accessible supplied material and state coverage. A title or URL does not prove the video was watched.

## 2. Research search intent

Use current official platform information for feature availability and actual accessible search observations for topic research. Record query, date, locale, observed result and source URL. Autocomplete suggests phrases, not exact search volume. Competitor coverage is a signal to investigate, not proof of unmet demand.

Create a table: query / viewer question / supporting observation / relevance to supplied video / confidence. If research access is unavailable, generate clearly labelled candidate queries and a manual verification checklist. Leave volume and competition unknown rather than estimating them from memory.

## 3. Draft titles

Offer titles that accurately describe the supplied content and fit the creator's voice. Distinguish search-led and curiosity-led variants. Use a number, personal result, comparison or product test only when the source supports it. Explain the tradeoff in specificity and clarity without predicting CTR. Verify current platform constraints if giving an exact character limit.

Choose one candidate for testing based on the intended viewer and content match. No title is guaranteed to rank or improve views. Do not change a published title without authorization and an available tool.

## 4. Draft the description and navigation

Write a useful first paragraph explaining the actual topic and benefit. Add a concise summary, verified links supplied by the creator, relevant disclosures and a call to action aligned to the request. Avoid keyword stuffing or fabricated resources. Do not claim a fixed percentage of SEO weight belongs to the title and description.

Use chapters only with verified cue times. Without them, provide headings with [TIMESTAMP NEEDED]. If explicitly asked for a planning estimate, label every estimated cue and require checking against the final edit before upload. Consult current official rules before claiming a chapter format meets publication requirements.

## 5. Review packaging and retention

Compare similar video ages, formats, traffic sources and audiences. The creator's relevant baseline is preferable to a generic industry range. External benchmarks need a dated source and compatible definitions. Impression CTR and total views cover different events; do not assume they reconcile by multiplication.

Inspect the retention curve and transcript together. Averages alone cannot locate a drop, establish a cause or prove viewers reached an ad. Present candidate causes and a test, not an algorithm diagnosis. Keep content quality, packaging, distribution and upload volume distinct.

## 6. Plan a manageable content mix

Build a schedule from the creator's capacity and the strongest observed questions. Mark ideas as grounded in supplied material or as hypotheses. Do not declare a universal search/browse split. Each planned piece should have an audience question, content evidence needed, draft title and outcome measure.

For existing videos, propose an exact candidate list and change rationale. Preserve a copy of the prior title/description. Make one interpretable change where feasible; record when it occurred. A plan does not authorize publication or deletion.

## 7. Monthly review

Use a fixed observation window and compatible metrics. Record changes, dates, audience/source mix and concurrent interventions. Report absolute counts, rates, percentage points and uncertainty. Overlapping windows are not independent replications. Before/after movement cannot establish causality by itself.

Return the evidence table, research notes, titles, description, any chapter placeholders, prioritized experiments and next review date proposed by the creator. Do not forecast a 10–20% traffic lift or specific subscriber growth. Update the recommendation when observations contradict the hypothesis.
