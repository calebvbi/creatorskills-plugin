# Worked Example: First Outreach Without Invented Proof

## Creator input

Write a pitch to HyperX for its gaming headset line. The creator reports 5,000 YouTube subscribers and 20,000 average views per video, focused on tech unboxings and first impressions. No prior brand campaign, audience demographics, podcast, product experience or availability is supplied.

## Draft email

Subject: Headset first-impressions idea for HyperX

Hi HyperX team,

I make tech unboxing and first-impressions videos on YouTube. My channel has 5,000 subscribers and averages 20,000 views per video over [confirm reporting period].

I'd like to propose a headset first-impressions video that explains the features viewers can assess from a real hands-on demonstration. The specific product, test plan, deliverables and disclosure would be agreed before filming. I would only describe product experience after actually testing it.

Would you be open to discussing whether that fits an upcoming campaign?

[Creator name]
[Verified channel link and contact]

## Why these fields remain open

The input does not support a claim about prior campaign lifts, tracked traffic, audience ages or cross-platform reach. No success story or attached analytics package is invented. Before sending, confirm the current product and contact, the reporting period and the proposed scope. This is an unsent draft.

## Optional social-proof line

If the creator supplies a verified prior result, add: "For [approved prior campaign], [defined result] during [period], measured by [source]." Otherwise omit the section. Having prior brand work does not by itself mean a campaign exceeded its KPIs.
