# Worked Example: Report Only Confirmed Campaign Facts

## Synthetic creator input

A travel creator supplied a campaign fixture for a fictional VPN brand, HarborVPN. The goal was trial sign-ups. A 14-minute video has 38,400 views after 28 days, 9.4 minutes average view duration, 538 tracked promo-link clicks, 87 brand-confirmed attributed sign-ups, 312 comments and 1,840 likes. The agreed deliverables were a 60-second mid-roll and a pinned link comment. The creator confirms a 75-second integration and a pinned comment live within two hours. Three supplied comments say the use-case explanation was useful. Deal cost, impressions, projections, channel baseline, exact sponsor cue and overall sentiment coding are absent.

## Draft report

HarborVPN campaign report, reflecting 28-day observations. Report date: [confirm date]. This draft has not been sent.

The video recorded 38,400 views and 87 attributed trial sign-ups confirmed by the brand. There were 538 tracked link clicks, giving 87/538 ≈ 16.2% observed sign-ups per click under the supplied attribution method. This is not an incrementality estimate. Cost per acquisition is unavailable until the relevant campaign cost is supplied.

| Measure | Supplied or computed result |
|---|---|
| Views | 38,400 |
| Likes | 1,840; about 4.8% of views |
| Comments | 312 |
| Average duration | 9.4 minutes of 14, about 67.1% |
| Promo clicks per view | About 1.4%; not thumbnail impression CTR |
| Platform impressions | Not supplied |
| Comparison to projection/baseline | Not supplied |

Average duration does not show what proportion watched the sponsor segment. The exact cue and retention at that cue are needed. The three supplied positive comments illustrate those respondents' reactions; they do not establish sentiment across all 312 comments.

Deliverables: the creator confirms the integration length and pinned comment. Description-link position, exact segment cue and other deliverables remain unverified. No explanation for the additional 15 seconds is invented.

For the next campaign, propose testing one alternative use-case framing and agree on tracking and reporting windows beforehand. Potential future topics and dates need the creator's approval. No upcoming shoot, second booking or campaign performance target is assumed.

## Follow-up checklist

Request the deal cost, original projections if any, exact sponsor cue and retention, platform impressions and a broader comment sample if those sections matter to the brand. Keep missing fields explicit in the report rather than deriving impressions from a fixed multiple of views.
