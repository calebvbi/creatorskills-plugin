# Worked Example: Profile and Content Drafts

## Creator input

A personal-finance YouTube creator reports 18,000 subscribers, 890 X followers, six months on X and an inconsistent posting cadence of about three tips per week. They want newsletter growth. No credentials, years of study, net worth, personal mistakes or financial product experience are supplied.

## Profile options

1. Personal-finance content for millennials. 18K on YouTube. Newsletter: [verified link].
2. Making personal-finance questions easier to understand. Videos on YouTube, notes in [confirmed newsletter].

No six-year experience claim or subscriber wealth outcome is invented.

## Pinned-post draft

I make personal-finance content for millennials. If there is one topic you'd like explained more clearly, tell me what it is. You can find my videos at [verified link] and newsletter at [verified link].

A value thread can follow once the creator supplies a researched topic and sources. Do not write a fictitious story of growing from zero net worth or claim personal mistakes. Financial claims require current, appropriate sources and context; this example does not give investment advice.

## One-week plan

Draft a sourced explainer, a question drawn from real audience feedback and a summary of an existing video. Reply substantively to relevant conversations the creator selects. Do not fabricate research, personal familiarity or endorsement. All posts and replies remain drafts until authorized.

## Newsletter path

Use a confirmed signup page and a truthful description of the newsletter. Track clicks and signups with defined denominators and dates. No conversion target is assumed. If exploring reply-to-DM automation, verify that the chosen provider actually supports the relevant X feature and the intended recipient flow, then review permissions and message scope. No automation is installed here.
