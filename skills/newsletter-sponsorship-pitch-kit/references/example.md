# Worked Example: A First Newsletter Sponsor Pitch

## Synthetic creator input

The Freelance Layer publishes weekly for freelance product designers. The creator reports 4,200 subscribers, 38% measured open rate and 5.1% click rate, with definitions and dates to confirm. They have not sold a sponsorship. A prior free mention of a friend's plugin is not a paid campaign result. The creator wants a proposed media kit and outreach draft.

## Media-kit draft

Audience: freelance product designers, as described by the creator. List size: 4,200 on [confirm date]. Frequency: weekly. Open/click metrics: supplied figures, pending period and denominator confirmation. Paid campaign history: none. Sample issue: [actual approved link]. Contact: [creator-confirmed address].

Do not invent an email address, audience income, buying intent, industry open-rate average or sponsor history. Privacy-related open tracking limits should be stated where relevant.

## Proposed placement

For illustration, the creator may choose a $180 asking price for an agreed placement. This is an editable negotiation assumption, not a verified market rate. Specify word count, location, issue date, approval process, disclosure and link reporting. Subscriber count is not guaranteed delivery or readership.

## Unsent email

Hi [verified contact],

I write The Freelance Layer, a weekly newsletter for freelance product designers. It has 4,200 subscribers as of [confirmed date]. I'd like to discuss a [proposed placement] about [verified relevant product angle].

The proposed scope is [agreed draft terms], at an asking price of $180 if approved. I can share an actual issue and the definitions behind the available readership metrics.

Would this be relevant to your current plans?

[Creator name and confirmed contact]

## Follow-up and sponsored copy

Draft at most the follow-up the creator requests; sending requires recipient and message authorization. For sponsored copy, use a verified product brief and the creator's actual experience. Do not imply a tool worked for the creator unless they say so. Offer terms, trial duration, code and disclosures must match the real approved campaign.
