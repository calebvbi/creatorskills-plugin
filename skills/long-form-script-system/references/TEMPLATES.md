# Long-Form Script System — Script Templates

Use these fill-in-the-blank templates as starting frameworks. Each one follows the narrative structure and retention mechanics from the core system. Replace anything in `[BRACKETS]` with your specific content.

## 1. Tutorial / How-To Template

```
[ENERGY: HIGH]

HOOK (0:00 - 0:30)
[TEASER — Show the end result or bold claim]
"By the end of this video, you'll know exactly how to [desired outcome].
And it's way simpler than you think — [surprising detail about the method]."

[B-ROLL: finished result / end state]

INTRO (0:30 - 1:30)
[ENERGY: MEDIUM]
"Here's the problem. [Describe the pain point your audience has.]
Most people try [common wrong approach], and it doesn't work because [reason].
Today I'm going to show you [your method] — step by step, nothing skipped.
We'll cover [Step 1 topic], [Step 2 topic], and [Step 3 topic].
[Open loop: And in the last step, I'll show you the one trick that
makes the biggest difference.]"

[GRAPHIC: roadmap showing the steps]

STEP 1: [STEP NAME] (1:30 - [timestamp])
[ENERGY: MEDIUM]
"First thing you need to do is [action].
The reason we start here is [brief explanation]."
[B-ROLL: demonstrating the step]
[GRAPHIC: key settings / numbers / details on screen]
"The most common mistake here is [mistake]. Instead, [correct approach]."

TRANSITION → STEP 2
"Now that [Step 1 result], here's where it starts to get interesting."

STEP 2: [STEP NAME] ([timestamp] - [timestamp])
[ENERGY: BUILD]
"This is the step most people skip, and it's why their [thing] looks amateur.
[Walk through the step with specific details.]"
[CUT TO: screen recording / close-up of the process]
[B-ROLL: showing the difference between doing this step and skipping it]

[RETENTION CHECK]
"Quick pro tip before we move on — [unexpected insight related to this step].
Trust me, this saves you [time/money/frustration] later."

STEP 3: [STEP NAME] ([timestamp] - [timestamp])
[ENERGY: HIGH]
"Okay, this is the one I told you about at the beginning.
[Deliver the step with enthusiasm — this is the payoff.]"
[B-ROLL: dramatic reveal of the technique in action]
[GRAPHIC: before vs after comparison]

RESULT REVEAL ([timestamp] - [timestamp])
[ENERGY: HIGH]
"And here's the finished [result].
[Describe what they're looking at and why it works.]
Compare that to where we started — [reference the problem from the intro]."
[B-ROLL: side by side of starting point vs finished result]

OUTRO
"If you want to take this further, I made a video about [related topic]
— it picks up right where this one leaves off. Link is right here.
If this saved you time, hit subscribe. I post [schedule]. See you next time."
[CUT TO: end screen]
```

## 2. Product Review Template

```
[ENERGY: HIGH]

HOOK (0:00 - 0:30)
"[Bold opinion — either strongly positive or honestly critical.]
I've been using the [product] for [time period] and [strongest takeaway].
But there's one thing about it that [teases a flaw or surprise]."

[B-ROLL: product reveal — clean, cinematic shot]

INTRO (0:30 - 1:30)
[ENERGY: MEDIUM]
"The [product] costs [$price]. The [competitor 1] costs [$price].
The [competitor 2] costs [$price]. You're trying to decide between them,
and I'm going to make that easy for you.
I'll cover [design], [performance], [the key feature], and [the dealbreaker].
By the end, you'll know exactly which one is worth your money."

[GRAPHIC: price comparison of the products being discussed]

FIRST IMPRESSIONS ([timestamp] - [timestamp])
[ENERGY: MEDIUM]
"[What you notice first — design, build, feel, unboxing experience.]"
[B-ROLL: hands-on footage, different angles]
[CUT TO: side-by-side with competitors]
"Compared to [competitor], [specific observation]."

DEEP DIVE: [CATEGORY 1] ([timestamp] - [timestamp])
[ENERGY: MEDIUM]
"[Detailed analysis of this aspect — specs, real-world performance, tests.]"
[GRAPHIC: specs comparison table]
[B-ROLL: demonstrating the feature in real use]

[RETENTION CHECK]
"Now, remember I mentioned [teased flaw/surprise from the hook]?
We're getting to that. But first — [next category]."

DEEP DIVE: [CATEGORY 2] ([timestamp] - [timestamp])
[ENERGY: BUILD]
"This is where the [product] really [shines/struggles].
[Detailed analysis with specific examples and comparisons.]"
[CUT TO: direct comparison footage with competitor]
[B-ROLL: specific test or demonstration]

DEEP DIVE: [CATEGORY 3 — the key feature or dealbreaker] ([timestamp])
[ENERGY: HIGH]
"Okay. [Pay off the open loop from the hook.]
[Detailed breakdown of the feature/flaw, with evidence.]"
[GRAPHIC: visual evidence — charts, photos, test results]
"For some of you, this won't matter. For others, this changes everything.
Here's how to know which camp you're in: [specific criteria]."

VERDICT ([timestamp] - end)
[ENERGY: HIGH]
[GRAPHIC: scorecard or summary ratings]
"Here's the bottom line.
Buy the [product] if: [specific use case].
Buy [competitor 1] if: [specific use case].
Buy [competitor 2] if: [specific use case].
[One-line definitive recommendation.]"

"Subscribe if you want honest reviews — I never sugarcoat it.
See you in the next one."
[CUT TO: end screen]
```

## 3. Storytelling / Personal Story Template

```
[ENERGY: LOW]
[MUSIC: reflective, intimate]

HOOK — IN MEDIAS RES (0:00 - 0:45)
"[Drop into the most dramatic, emotional, or pivotal moment of the story.
No setup. No context. Just the moment.]"
[B-ROLL: visual that grounds the scene — a location, an object, a screenshot]
"[One or two more sentences that deepen the tension.]
[Tease: What I didn't know yet was that (hint at the resolution).]"

INTRO (0:45 - 2:00)
[ENERGY: MEDIUM]
"[Step back and frame the story for the viewer.]
This is the story of [one-sentence summary].
I want to tell you this because [why it matters to the viewer].
[Set expectations: what they'll learn, feel, or take away.]"

[CUT TO: talking head]

BACKSTORY ([timestamp] - [timestamp])
[ENERGY: MEDIUM]
"[Rewind. Give the necessary context — where you were, what led up to this.
Keep it tight. Only include details that make the turning point hit harder.]"
[B-ROLL: relevant visuals from that time period]
[GRAPHIC: timeline or key data if relevant]

RISING ACTION ([timestamp] - [timestamp])
[ENERGY: BUILD]
"[Escalate. Things get harder, more complicated, more emotional.
Each paragraph should raise the stakes.]"
[B-ROLL: visuals that show the progression]

[RETENTION CHECK]
"[Acknowledge the viewer's investment:]
And this is where it gets [worse/weirder/more complicated].
[Tease the turning point without giving it away.]"

BREAKING POINT ([timestamp] - [timestamp])
[ENERGY: LOW]
"[The lowest moment. The point of almost giving up.
Be specific and honest — vague emotion doesn't land.]"
[MUSIC: shift to quieter, more vulnerable tone]
[CUT TO: tight talking head shot, direct eye contact]

THE TURNING POINT ([timestamp] - [timestamp])
[ENERGY: BUILD]
"[What changed. The decision, the event, the realization.
Close the biggest open loop from the hook.]"
[B-ROLL: evidence of the change — screenshots, footage, results]
[GRAPHIC: data or visual proof that things shifted]

RESULTS & RESOLUTION ([timestamp] - [timestamp])
[ENERGY: MEDIUM]
"[Show what happened after the turning point.
Be specific with numbers, timelines, outcomes.
Acknowledge luck, privilege, or caveats honestly.]"
[GRAPHIC: before/after data or timeline]

THE LESSON ([timestamp] - [timestamp])
[ENERGY: BUILD]
"[What you learned. What the viewer can take from this.
Avoid generic motivational platitudes — be specific to your experience.]"
[CUT TO: direct to camera, centered]

OUTRO
[ENERGY: MEDIUM]
"[Tie back to the hook. Reference where the story started.
Close with forward-looking hope or a direct challenge to the viewer.]"
"Subscribe if [reason connected to your channel's ongoing story].
See you in the next one."
[MUSIC: warm, hopeful]
[CUT TO: end screen]
```

## 4. Listicle (Top 5/10) Template

```
[ENERGY: HIGH]

HOOK (0:00 - 0:30)
"[Tease the #1 pick or the most surprising entry on the list.]
I tested/tried/used [number] different [things] and these [X] are the ones
that actually [delivered result]. Number [X] on this list is the one that
[specific surprising claim]."

[B-ROLL: quick montage of all items on the list]

INTRO (0:30 - 1:15)
[ENERGY: MEDIUM]
"[Brief context: why you made this list, how you evaluated, what criteria.]
We're going from [least to most impactful/cheapest to most expensive/etc].
Let's get into it."

ITEM [lowest number]: [NAME] ([timestamp])
[ENERGY: MEDIUM]
"[What it is, one sentence.]
[Why it's on the list — the key benefit.]
[Who it's best for.]
[One honest limitation.]"
[B-ROLL: showing the item in use]
[GRAPHIC: key specs or price]

TRANSITION
"[Micro-hook for the next item:]
Good, but the next one does something [surprising/better/completely different]."

ITEM [next number]: [NAME] ([timestamp])
[ENERGY: MEDIUM]
[Same structure as above, slightly more detail as you approach the top]
[CUT TO: comparison with previous item if relevant]

[RETENTION CHECK — mid-list]
"Now we're getting into the ones that genuinely surprised me."

ITEM [middle number]: [NAME] ([timestamp])
[ENERGY: BUILD]
[This is usually the "sleeper pick" — the one viewers didn't expect]
[B-ROLL: extended demonstration]
[GRAPHIC: data or results that prove the value]

TRANSITION
"[Tease the top pick:] Okay, these last [2/3] are on a different level."

ITEM [second to last]: [NAME] ([timestamp])
[ENERGY: HIGH]
[Extended coverage — more detail, more demonstration, direct comparison]
[B-ROLL: side by side with the top pick]

ITEM #1: [NAME] ([timestamp])
[ENERGY: HIGH]
"And the number one pick is — [build just a beat of suspense].
[Extended section — why it's #1, detailed walkthrough, evidence.]"
[B-ROLL: the best footage you have of this item]
[GRAPHIC: summary of why it won]

RECAP & OUTRO ([timestamp])
[ENERGY: HIGH]
[GRAPHIC: all items listed with one-line verdict each]
"Quick recap: [rapid fire through the list].
If I could only pick one, it's [#1] — [one sentence why].
Links to everything are in the description.
If this saved you from buying the wrong [thing], hit subscribe.
See you in the next one."
[CUT TO: end screen]
```

## 5. Commentary / Opinion Template

```
[ENERGY: HIGH]

HOOK (0:00 - 0:30)
"[Your hottest take — the opinion that will make some people agree
immediately and others want to argue.]
[One sentence of supporting evidence or credibility.]
Let me explain why."

[B-ROLL: relevant footage or screenshots that ground the topic]

INTRO (0:30 - 1:30)
[ENERGY: MEDIUM]
"[Frame the topic — what happened, what's being debated, why it matters now.]
I've been thinking about this for a while, and I think [thesis statement].
I know a lot of people disagree, so I'm going to lay out my case,
address the best counterargument, and let you decide."

[GRAPHIC: the key question or debate framed visually]

YOUR ARGUMENT — Part 1 ([timestamp] - [timestamp])
[ENERGY: BUILD]
"[First piece of evidence or reasoning. Be specific.]"
[B-ROLL: supporting footage, examples, data]
[GRAPHIC: data visualization, quote, or screenshot]
"[Connect it back to your thesis.]"

YOUR ARGUMENT — Part 2 ([timestamp] - [timestamp])
[ENERGY: MEDIUM]
"[Second piece of evidence. Different angle or type of proof.]"
[CUT TO: example or case study]
"[Build the case. Stack the evidence.]"

[RETENTION CHECK]
"Now, I know what some of you are thinking. And honestly?
You might have a point. Let me address it."

THE COUNTERARGUMENT ([timestamp] - [timestamp])
[ENERGY: MEDIUM]
"The strongest argument against what I just said is [steelman the other side].
And I genuinely think [acknowledge what's valid about it].
[But here's where it breaks down / But here's what that misses:]"
[GRAPHIC: the counterpoint, fairly presented]
[B-ROLL: relevant examples]

THE NUANCE ([timestamp] - [timestamp])
[ENERGY: BUILD]
"Here's what I think both sides miss: [your unique insight].
[This is where your commentary adds something new to the conversation.
Not just agreeing with one side — offering a perspective nobody else has.]"
[CUT TO: talking head, direct and confident]

CONCLUSION ([timestamp] - end)
[ENERGY: HIGH]
"So here's where I land.
[Restate your thesis, refined by everything you've discussed.]
[One-sentence definitive position.]"

"I want to hear where you land on this — drop your take in the comments.
If you want more videos where I actually think about stuff instead of
just reacting to it, subscribe. See you in the next one."
[CUT TO: end screen]
```
