# Advanced Guide — Video-to-Everything Repurposer

You've used the system on a single video. Now here's how to get 10x the value out of it.

---

## Multi-Video Batching

Instead of repurposing one video at a time, batch four videos together and generate a full month of content in one session.

**How to do it:**

1. Gather transcripts or scripts from your last 4 videos
2. Paste all four into one conversation with this prompt:

```
Here are transcripts from my last 4 videos. My channel is about
[niche] with [X] subscribers.

Repurpose all 4 into a full month of content. For each video, give me:
- 1 Twitter thread
- 1 LinkedIn post (mix formats across the 4)
- 1 Instagram carousel outline
- 1 Shorts script
- 1 quote graphic

Then create a 4-week posting calendar that spaces the content
so I'm not posting about the same video topic twice in one week.
```

This gives you 20+ content pieces mapped to a full month. No more staring at a blank screen wondering what to post.

**Pro tip:** Pick videos that cover different subtopics. If all four are about the same thing, the repurposed content will feel repetitive across platforms. Variety in input = variety in output.

## Platform-Specific Customization

The default system generates content for all platforms. But if you have specific needs, you can tune the output per platform.

### Adjusting Tone

```
Repurpose this transcript, but adjust the tone:
- Twitter: more casual, slightly edgy
- LinkedIn: professional but personal (this is my main B2B channel)
- Instagram: upbeat, use shorter sentences than normal
- Blog: authoritative, more detailed than usual
```

### Adjusting Format

```
For this video, I want:
- Twitter: a single viral tweet instead of a thread
- LinkedIn: story format only (skip listicle and hot take)
- Instagram: 6-slide carousel instead of 10 (I want it punchy)
- Shorts: 3 scripts instead of 2 (I post Shorts daily)
```

### Skipping Platforms

```
Repurpose this for Twitter, LinkedIn, and Shorts only.
Skip Instagram, blog, and newsletter.
```

The system adapts to whatever you specify. If you don't specify, you get the full package for every platform.

## Content Calendar Integration

Once you have repurposed content from a video, map it to a weekly posting schedule to avoid dumping everything on the same day.

**Recommended posting cadence:**

| Day | Platform | Content Type |
|-----|----------|-------------|
| Monday | Twitter/X | Thread from this week's video |
| Tuesday | LinkedIn | Story or listicle post |
| Wednesday | Instagram | Carousel from this week's video |
| Thursday | YouTube Shorts | Short #1 |
| Friday | LinkedIn | Hot take post |
| Saturday | YouTube Shorts | Short #2 |
| Sunday | Newsletter | Weekly email with snippet |

**Ask for a custom schedule:**

```
Here's my repurposed content. Create a 2-week posting calendar
that accounts for:
- I post on Twitter daily (mix thread content into daily tweets)
- LinkedIn 3x per week
- Instagram 2x per week
- Shorts every other day
- Newsletter goes out on Fridays
```

The system will space content across your platforms and suggest which pieces to post when, based on platform best practices and your publishing frequency.

## Building Content Pillars

If you publish regularly, your videos naturally cluster around recurring themes. Use the Repurposer to turn those clusters into content pillars — consistent topic categories that your audience recognizes across platforms.

**How to identify your pillars:**

```
Here are the titles and brief descriptions of my last 20 videos.
Group them into 3-5 content pillars — recurring themes that tie
multiple videos together.

Then suggest how I should label/brand each pillar on social media
(e.g., a series name, a recurring hashtag, a visual style).
```

**How to use pillars with repurposing:**

Once you know your pillars, you can repurpose with pillar awareness:

```
This video falls under my "[Pillar Name]" content pillar.
Repurpose it and make sure all social content ties back
to this pillar theme. Use my pillar hashtag [#hashtag]
where appropriate.
```

This turns random social posts into a cohesive brand story. Followers start to recognize your content categories, which builds loyalty and makes your feed look intentional instead of scattered.

## Repurposing Your Back Catalog

You don't need to only repurpose new videos. Your best-performing older videos are gold mines for social content.

```
Here's the transcript from my most popular video (published 6 months ago,
[X] views). It still gets traffic from search.

Repurpose it for my current platforms. My audience has grown from
[old number] to [new number] since I published it, so most of my
followers haven't seen the original.
```

A video with proven engagement likely has the strongest insights and stories. Repurposing it for new platforms and a larger audience is one of the highest-value things you can do — and it takes 10 minutes instead of creating something from scratch.
