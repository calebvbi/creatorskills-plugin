## LinkedIn Post Templates

### Story Format
```
[Opening line — a specific, personal moment that hooks the reader.]
[Make it vivid. "Last Tuesday I..." not "I've always believed..."]

[Line break]

[2-3 short paragraphs telling the story. Include:
 - What happened
 - What you learned
 - Why it matters for the reader]

[Line break]

[One clear, actionable takeaway in 1-2 sentences.]

[Engagement prompt — question or invitation to share their experience.]
```

### Listicle Format
```
[Bold claim + number that sets expectations.]
(e.g., "After [experience], here are the [X] things I'd tell myself:")

1. [Point] — [Brief explanation in 1 sentence]
2. [Point] — [Brief explanation in 1 sentence]
3. [Point] — [Brief explanation in 1 sentence]
4. [Point] — [Brief explanation in 1 sentence]
5. [Point] — [Brief explanation in 1 sentence]

[Closing line that restates the core value.]

[Question to drive comments.]
```

### Hot Take Format
```
[Contrarian statement — something that challenges conventional wisdom.]

[Line break]

[2-3 paragraphs explaining your position.]
[Use evidence, personal experience, or data to support the take.]
[Don't hedge — commit to the opinion.]

[Line break]

[Closing challenge: "Am I wrong?" or "What's your take?"]
```

Text: [The pain point or common mistake — under 30 words.]
Design: [Simple icon or visual that represents the problem.]

      [Explanation in 1-2 sentences. Under 50 words.]
Design: [Consistent layout. Icon or small visual per slide.]

Text: [Recap all key points as a short bulleted list.]

Text: "Save this for later."
      "Follow @[handle] for more [topic]."
Design: [Brand colors, handle prominent, clean.]

## YouTube Shorts / Reels Script Template

```
[HOOK — 2 seconds]
[Bold statement, surprising fact, or direct question.]
[This is the only thing that matters. If the hook doesn't grab
 attention, the rest doesn't exist.]

[SETUP — seconds 3-10]
[Quick context. What's the problem or situation?]
[1-3 sentences. No preamble, no throat-clearing.]

[PAYOFF — seconds 10-45]
[Deliver the value. One idea, explained clearly.]
[Use short sentences. Conversational. Like telling a friend.]
[Include 1-2 staging notes: [look at camera], [show screen], etc.]

[CTA — final 5 seconds]
[One line: "Follow for more [topic]" or "Full video on my channel."]
```

**Word count target:** 80-150 words total (30-60 seconds when spoken naturally).

              open this email from a friend?]

Cheers,
[Creator name]

## Quote Graphic Template

```
Quote Graphic [#]:
Text: "[Exact quote or paraphrased insight — under 15 words]"
Attribution: "— [Creator name or channel name]"
Visual: [Background color/style, font suggestion, any accent elements]
Best for: [Which platforms — Instagram story, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Pinterest]
```

**Selection criteria for choosing quotes:**
- Can someone understand it without any context? If not, skip it.
- Does it make you feel something — surprise, agreement, motivation? If not, skip it.
- Is it under 15 words? If not, trim it until it is.
- Would you screenshot this if you saw it in a feed? That's the bar.
