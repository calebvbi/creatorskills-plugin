# Example Output: Channel Art & Banner Kit

Two complete walkthroughs: a solo backpacking YouTube channel building its first full kit, and a cozy indie-game Twitch streamer redoing a mismatched panel set.

## Example 1: Solo Backpacking YouTube Channel — Full Kit From Scratch

### Input

```
I'm starting a YouTube channel called "Offline Miles" — solo backpacking trips, mostly remote trails, minimal gear talk, more about the actual experience of being alone in the backcountry for days at a time. No brand colors yet.

Vibe: quiet, rugged, a little melancholy in a good way. Think early morning light, not golden-hour Instagram vibes.

I'm faceless — I don't want my face in the art, just the landscapes and the feeling.

Run the Brand Mood & Style Guide Builder, then the YouTube Banner tool.
```

---

### Output: Brand Mood & Style Guide

**Brand Mood & Style Guide: Offline Miles**

**Niche:** Solo backpacking and remote trail experiences | **Platforms:** YouTube

---

**Color Palette**

- **Primary:** #2B3A3D (deep slate green) — dominant background tone, evokes forest shade and early-morning fog
- **Secondary:** #E8DCC8 (warm bone/fog cream) — used for typography and negative space, keeps the palette from feeling heavy
- **Accent/Pop:** #C4622D (burnt clay orange) — used sparingly, one element per asset — think a single sunrise-lit ridge or a tent flap catching light
- **Neutral/Background:** #14181A (near-black charcoal) — base tone for darker compositions, night/pre-dawn scenes

This palette reads as "quiet and rugged" without leaning into the oversaturated golden-hour look most travel channels use. The slate and charcoal keep it grounded and a little somber; the single orange accent gives the eye somewhere to land without turning the whole thing into a postcard.

**Mood & Tone**

Every asset should feel like the five minutes after you wake up in a tent and unzip the door — quiet, a little cold, worth the effort. Lighting should be soft and directional (low sun, fog, overcast), never harsh midday light. Composition should be spacious and a little empty — negative space is doing emotional work here, not wasted space to fill.

**Typography Treatment**

**Primary treatment:** Thin-weight serif or high-contrast sans, wide letter-spacing, lowercase — feels considered, not shouty
**Where AI-generated text is safe:** None — skip AI text entirely for this brand, even background texture text tends to look wrong against this mood
**Where to add text manually instead:** Channel name, tagline, all overlay text — add in a clean editor after generation using the treatment above

**Imagery Style**

Photographic, not illustrated — this brand lives or dies on feeling real. Slightly desaturated, film-grain texture, wide-angle landscape framing with a small human-scale element (tent, pack, boots) rather than a person's face. Matte, not glossy — avoid anything that reads as a stock travel photo.

**Consistency Rules**

- Every asset uses slate green + bone cream as the dominant pairing; burnt clay orange appears once, small, never as a large background field
- No faces or identifiable figures — silhouettes, gear, or landscape only
- Always leave the lower-center third relatively simple/low-detail — that's where text gets added

Give me the full Twitch Banner + Panel Set (About, Schedule, Rules, Donate, Socials).
