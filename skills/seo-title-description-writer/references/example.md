# SEO Title & Description Writer: grounded copy

## Example 1: camera comparison

**Supplied brief:** A video comparing five cameras under $1,000 for people starting a YouTube channel. Keyword: best camera for YouTube beginners. No hands-on testing, purchases, camera model names, winner, date, footage or search-volume research supplied.

### Option 1
Title (49 characters): Best Camera for YouTube Beginners: 5 Under $1,000
Description (120 characters): Compare five cameras under $1,000 for people starting a YouTube channel. See the video for the comparison and its scope.

### Option 2
Title (48 characters): Starting YouTube? Compare 5 Cameras Under $1,000
Description (123 characters): A camera comparison for new YouTube creators, covering five options below $1,000. Confirm current prices before purchasing.

### Option 3
Title (51 characters): 5 Cameras Under $1,000 for a Beginner YouTube Setup
Description (135 characters): Looking for a beginner YouTube camera? This video compares five options under $1,000 without assuming every creator has the same needs.

**Editorial pick:** Option 1 directly names the requested topic and supplied scope. This is a clarity judgment, not a ranking or CTR prediction. Preview truncation varies by surface and device; verify current hard limits rather than promising a fixed visible character count.

**Research status:** No keyword-volume or competition research was performed. Related keyword ideas may be proposed for validation, never called high-volume without data. Add model names, timestamps or sample-footage claims only when the video supports them.

## Example 2: meal-prep article

**Supplied brief:** A food blogger plans five dinners on Sunday, each taking under 30 minutes, for busy parents. Keyword: easy weeknight meal prep. No grocery list, storage tests or personal family routine supplied.

### Option 1
Title (52 characters): Easy Weeknight Meal Prep: 5 Dinners for Busy Parents
Description (108 characters): Plan five dinners on Sunday, with each dinner taking under 30 minutes. A meal-prep article for busy parents.

### Option 2
Title (34 characters): Plan 5 Weeknight Dinners on Sunday
Description (117 characters): An easy weeknight meal-prep plan built around five dinners. Each recipe in the supplied brief takes under 30 minutes.

### Option 3
Title (42 characters): A Sunday Plan for Easy Weeknight Meal Prep
Description (117 characters): Five dinner ideas for a weeknight meal-prep plan. Confirm the recipe timings and preparation steps before publishing.

**Editorial pick:** Option 1 makes the audience and scope clear. Clarify whether the 30-minute figure includes prep before promising a combined session length. Do not add reheating quality, safe storage duration, taste-test results or a grocery list that was not supplied.
