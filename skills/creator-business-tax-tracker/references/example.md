# Worked Example: Prepare Records Before Estimating Tax

## Synthetic creator input

A US creator in Colorado wants to organize one month's records for tax-year review. They report $4,000 gross platform sales, $200 refunds and $120 platform fees. The platform paid out $3,680. They also received $600 for freelance work, paid $80 for editing software and bought a $1,200 camera used for both personal and business projects. They have a day job but have not supplied wages, withholding, filing status, prior-year tax or documented camera usage. No payment or filing is requested.

## Reconciliation

Platform gross sales $4,000 minus refunds $200 minus fees $120 equals the $3,680 payout. Do not count the payout again as sales. Net receipts before fees are $3,800 plus $600 freelance receipts = $4,400. Separately recorded fees and software total $200. The bookkeeping surplus before camera treatment is $4,200. This is not a taxable-income determination.

## Expense worksheet

| Item | Recorded amount | Evidence needed | Treatment |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Platform fees | $120 | Platform statement | Recorded expense, tax classification to review |
| Editing software | $80 | Receipt and business purpose | Candidate business expense |
| Camera | $1,200 | Receipt, purchase date, actual business use | Allocation and expensing/depreciation unresolved |

Do not assume the camera is fully deductible or assign a typical usage percentage. Keep its purchase in the records while marking tax treatment pending.

## Estimated-tax preparation

The payment amount is undetermined. Required inputs include the exact tax year, filing status, wages and withholding, other income, prior-year tax/AGI, relevant deductions and credits, payments already made and income timing. Check the applicable IRS Form 1040-ES, Schedule SE and state instructions. The day job may affect the calculation, so multiplying this month's surplus by a generic tax percentage is not sufficient.

## Business-structure questions

The revenue figure alone does not establish a need for an LLC or S-corporation election. Ask an adviser about contracts, liability exposure, administrative costs, payroll requirements and the creator's actual situation. No entity recommendation or legal filing is made.

## Handoff

The creator can gather the platform statement, software and camera receipts, and redacted wage/withholding totals. The adviser packet contains the reconciliation, unresolved camera allocation and missing tax inputs. No tax bill, guaranteed reserve rate or payment deadline has been invented.
