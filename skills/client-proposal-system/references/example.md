## Example 1: Video Editor — Monthly Retainer Proposal

### Creator Input

```
I'm a freelance video editor specializing in YouTube long-form. A SaaS company
(project management tool, ~50K subscribers on YouTube) wants me to edit 8 videos
per month — 4 long-form tutorials (15-20 min) and 4 Shorts. They've been editing
in-house but quality is inconsistent and their editor just left.

My rate: $4,000/month. I want a 3-month minimum commitment. I have 2 years of
experience editing for tech YouTubers. Turnaround is 5 business days for long-form,
2 days for Shorts. Two revision rounds included.
```

### Generated Output

---

**PROPOSAL: YouTube Video Production Partnership**

**Prepared for:** [SaaS Company Name]
**Prepared by:** [Your Name]
**Date:** [Current Date]

---

### The Problem You're Solving

Your YouTube channel has 50K subscribers and a proven content strategy — but inconsistent editing quality is holding back growth. In-house editing worked when output was lower, but at 8 videos per month, you need a dedicated editor who understands tech content and can maintain a consistent visual standard across every video.

With your previous editor's departure, you also have an institutional knowledge gap: brand-specific motion graphics, pacing preferences, and audience expectations that take weeks to rebuild.

### What I'll Deliver

**Monthly output:**

| Deliverable | Quantity | Turnaround | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long-form tutorials | 4/month | 5 business days | 15–20 min, full editing with motion graphics, b-roll integration, and branded intro/outro |
| YouTube Shorts | 4/month | 2 business days | Repurposed from long-form or standalone, optimized for vertical with captions and hooks |

**What's included in each video:**
- Color correction and audio cleanup
- Custom motion graphics for key concepts and UI demos
- Branded lower-thirds, transitions, and end screens
- Thumbnail-ready frame exports
- Two rounds of revisions per video

### Why Me

I've spent the last two years editing exclusively for tech YouTubers — SaaS demos, developer tutorials, and product walkthroughs. I already know how to make screen recordings feel dynamic, pace tutorials so viewers don't drop off, and highlight UI features without over-producing.

Relevant experience:
- [Creator A]: Edited 60+ tech tutorials, channel grew from 20K to 85K during our engagement
- [Creator B]: Produced a 12-part SaaS onboarding series that averaged 45% retention on 20-min videos

### Investment

**$4,000/month** — 3-month minimum commitment.

This covers all 8 videos, revisions, and a dedicated Slack channel for async feedback. No per-video invoicing, no surprise charges.

For context, hiring a comparable in-house editor (salary + benefits + software licenses) runs $5,500–7,000/month. Freelance rates for this volume from editors without tech niche experience typically start at $3,500 with longer turnaround times.

### Timeline

- **Week 1:** Onboarding — review your last 10 videos, document brand standards, align on style
- **Week 2–4:** Full production begins, first 8 videos delivered
- **Ongoing:** Steady monthly cadence with async feedback via Slack

### Next Step

I have availability starting [date]. If you'd like to move forward, I'll send over a simple contract and onboarding questionnaire this week. Want to lock in the start date?

## Example 2: Social Media Manager — Quick Email Proposal

### Creator Input

```
A local restaurant chain (3 locations) wants me to manage their Instagram. They
post sporadically right now, maybe 2-3 times a week with phone photos. They want
more professional content and consistent posting. Budget is around $1,500/month.

I'd do 4 posts/week + stories, handle engagement for 30 min/day, and provide a
monthly report. I'd shoot content on-site once a month (half day). Send me a quick
email proposal, not a full formal one.
```

### Generated Output

---

**Subject: Instagram management proposal — [Restaurant Name]**

Hi [Client Name],

Thanks for the conversation yesterday. Here's what I'd put together for your Instagram.

**The core issue:** You're posting 2–3 times a week with phone photos, which means your 3 locations aren't getting consistent visual representation and you're leaving engagement on the table. A professional, steady presence will build the kind of local following that actually drives foot traffic.

**What I'd handle:**

- **4 posts/week** across feed — mix of food photography, behind-the-scenes, staff spotlights, and seasonal promotions
- **Daily Stories** (3–5 per day on rotation across locations)
- **Community engagement** — 30 min/day responding to comments, DMs, and engaging with local accounts
- **Monthly on-site shoot** (half day) at rotating locations for fresh content
- **Monthly report** — follower growth, engagement rate, top-performing posts, and recommendations

**Investment:** $1,500/month, 2-month minimum so we have enough runway to see what's working.

**What I need from you to start:** Menu updates, event schedules, and a quick brand chat so I nail your voice from day one.

I could start as early as [date]. Want to set up a 15-minute call this week to finalize details?

Best,
[Your Name]

## What These Examples Show

1. **Problem-first framing** — both proposals open with the client's pain point, not the freelancer's credentials
2. **Specific deliverables with quantities** — no vague "I'll manage your content," every output is countable
3. **Pricing context** — the full proposal anchors against in-house costs; the quick proposal states the number cleanly
4. **Clear next step** — both end with a specific action, not "let me know what you think"
5. **Format matches context** — the SaaS company gets a formal proposal; the restaurant gets a conversational email
