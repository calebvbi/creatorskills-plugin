# Worked Examples — Sponsor Pitch Email in Action

## Example 1: Fitness Creator Pitching a Recovery-Gear Brand

### Creator Input

```
Brand: Restora (recovery/mobility gear)
What they're currently running: They just launched a new percussion
massage gun and are running an Instagram campaign with the hashtag
#RecoverLikeAnAthlete, mostly targeting amateur endurance athletes.

My audience fit: 62% of my subscribers are training for their first
half marathon or marathon — the exact "amateur endurance" audience
their launch hashtag is targeting.

Integration idea: A dedicated 8-minute video testing the new massage
gun during my actual post-long-run recovery routine, not a scripted
unboxing.

Write the pitch email and subject lines.
```

### Output

```
SUBJECT LINE OPTIONS:
1. "Idea for your #RecoverLikeAnAthlete launch" — Campaign-named
2. "62% of my audience is training for their first marathon" — Result-led
3. "Recovery gun idea 🏃" — Curiosity/short

RECOMMENDED: Option 1 — the #RecoverLikeAnAthlete hashtag is specific
enough that it signals real research before the brand even opens the
email, and it's still under 40 characters.

EMAIL:

Subject: Idea for your #RecoverLikeAnAthlete launch

Hi [Name],

Saw Restora's #RecoverLikeAnAthlete push for the new percussion gun —
smart timing, since my audience is exactly who that campaign is built
for.

62% of my subscribers are currently training for their first half or
full marathon. I'd love to feature the gun in a real 8-minute recovery
video — not a scripted unboxing, but the actual routine I run after
long training days, gun included.

I can send over my channel stats and a shot list for the segment this
week if that's useful — worth a quick call?

Best,
[Name]
[Channel link]

WORD COUNT: 97
```

## Example 2: Newsletter Writer With No Campaign Detail Yet

### Creator Input

```
Brand: Notion
Write me a sponsor pitch email.
```

### Output

```
I don't have enough to write a specific pitch yet — a pitch built on
just "Notion" with nothing else reads as mass-blasted, and it'll get
skipped.

Before I write this, can you get me:

1. What is Notion actually running right now? Check their Instagram,
   blog, or product page from the last 2 weeks — a new feature launch,
   a template gallery push, a pricing change, anything specific.
2. What's the one number that proves your newsletter audience fits
   that specific push? (Subscriber count, open rate, or a demographic
   detail — pick whichever is strongest for what they're currently
   promoting.)
3. What would the actual integration look like? A dedicated newsletter
   section, a sponsored send, a template you'd build and share?

Once I have those three things, I can write you a tight email in one
pass — the campaign detail is what makes the difference between a
pitch that gets read and one that gets deleted.
```

### Follow-Up (Creator Provides Detail)

```
Brand: Notion
What they're currently running: They just launched a new "AI meeting
notes" feature and are running case studies on productivity newsletters.

My audience fit: My newsletter has 14,000 subscribers, 91% of whom
identify as remote or hybrid knowledge workers in the "how I work"
survey I ran last month.

Integration idea: A dedicated "how I use it" section in one issue,
walking through how I'd use the AI meeting notes feature in my actual
workflow, with a template readers can copy.

Write the pitch email and subject lines.
```

### Output

```
SUBJECT LINE OPTIONS:
1. "Idea for your AI meeting notes launch" — Campaign-named
2. "14K remote workers, 91% hybrid — [Newsletter Name]" — Result-led
3. "Meeting notes idea for Notion" — Curiosity/short

RECOMMENDED: Option 1 — naming the specific feature launch signals
you're not sending a form email, and it's the detail most likely to
get forwarded to the right person on their team.

EMAIL:

Subject: Idea for your AI meeting notes launch

Hi [Name],

Saw Notion's push behind the new AI meeting notes feature, alongside
the productivity case studies you're running — my newsletter audience
is a close match for that story.

[Newsletter Name] reaches 14,000 subscribers, 91% of whom are remote
or hybrid knowledge workers, based on a reader survey I ran last month.

I'd love to build a dedicated "how I use it" section for one issue —
walking through the AI meeting notes feature in my real workflow, with
a copyable template for readers. Practical, not promotional.

Happy to send over open-rate stats and a draft outline this week if
useful — worth a quick call?

Best,
[Name]
[Newsletter link]

WORD COUNT: 108
```

## Why These Examples Work

Both finished emails lead with something the brand actually did, not a compliment about the brand in general — that's the difference between an email that reads as researched and one that reads as copy-pasted to fifty brands in the same week. The second example shows the skill refusing to write a generic pitch when the creator gave it nothing to work with, then producing a sharp, specific email as soon as the campaign detail, audience number, and integration idea were on the table. Every subject line tests a genuinely different angle rather than three versions of the same phrasing, and every email lands well under the 150-word target while still naming a real number and a real integration idea — nothing generic, nothing invented.
