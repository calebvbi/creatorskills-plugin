# Worked Example: A Membership Sales Page With Confirmed Terms

## Synthetic offer input

A fictional community, The Newsletter Lab, proposes $49/month membership, a weekly Tuesday workshop, a library of 200 newsletter examples, a database of 80 sponsor prospects and peer feedback groups. The creator offers a monthly AMA and open DMs. The creator reports 340 members, 67 reaching 1,000 subscribers within their first 60 days and 23 obtaining a first sponsor. No individual testimonials, causal evidence, member demographics, daily response commitment or confirmed provider trial/cancellation behavior is supplied.

## Draft hero

Build a repeatable newsletter practice with workshops, examples and peer feedback.

The Newsletter Lab brings the supplied resources into one membership at the proposed $49/month. Review the exact benefits and confirmed billing terms before joining.

## What is included

- Weekly Tuesday workshop at the confirmed time.
- A library of 200 examples, subject to the creator's rights to share them.
- A database of 80 sponsor prospects. Their current activity and details must be maintained and verified; a listing does not guarantee a deal.
- Peer feedback groups, with matching process and timing to confirm.
- Monthly AMA and open DMs within limits the creator confirms.

## Evidence section

The creator reports 340 members, with 67 reaching the stated subscriber milestone and 23 obtaining a first sponsor. Before publication, confirm the reporting period, measurement method, consent and relevant context. These observations do not prove the community caused the outcomes or promise similar results for a new member. No quote or personal testimonial is invented.

## Fit

For newsletter writers who want structured practice and are willing to participate. It is not an offer of guaranteed growth, sponsor bookings or unlimited individual coaching. Do not promise 100 hours saved, 3–5 pitches in an inbox or a sponsor database that pays for itself without supporting evidence.

## FAQ drafts

**What does it cost?** The proposed price is $49/month. Confirm the actual checkout, billing period and applicable terms before publishing.

**Is there a trial?** [Insert only the verified configured trial and its actual charge/cancellation terms.] A proposed trial does not prove no charge until a specific day.

**What happens if I cancel?** [Insert verified access, cancellation and refund rules.] Cancel-anytime wording alone does not answer those questions.

**How available is the creator?** The supplied offer includes a monthly AMA and open DMs. Response times, daily attendance and extra sessions remain unconfirmed.

**Will my niche have a matching group?** [Confirm actual member mix and matching policy.] Do not promise a match or invent member categories.

## Call to action

Review the confirmed benefits, schedule and billing terms at [verified signup link]. This is a draft sales page; nothing has been published or charged.
