# Paid Community Membership Sales Page Writer

Give it your community name, price, platform, and the transformation you promise members. Get back every section of a high-converting membership sales page — hero headline, belonging hook, community pillars, creator access promise, social proof structure, FAQ, and CTAs — written with community-specific psychology, not course-selling language.

## What This Skill Does

Community sales pages fail when creators copy course-page templates. Courses sell curriculum. Communities sell belonging, ongoing access to a person, and transformation through connection. This skill writes every section of a high-converting membership sales page using the right psychological levers — so you stop describing features and start selling the feeling of being inside.

## What You Need Before Starting

Gather these details about your community:

1. **Community name** — What are you calling it?
2. **Platform** — Skool, Discord, Circle, Mighty Networks, Slack, or other?
3. **Price** — Monthly, annual, or lifetime? What's the cost?
4. **Target member** — Who is this for? Be specific ("freelance designers earning under $3K/month" not "creative entrepreneurs")
5. **The transformation** — What changes for members in 90 days? (Use before/after framing)
6. **Community pillars** — What are the 3-5 core things members get? (Courses, live calls, accountability, direct access, templates, job board, etc.)
7. **Creator access** — What kind of access do you give? (AMA threads, DM access, live office hours, personal feedback?)
8. **Social proof** — Any member wins, testimonials, or enrollment numbers?
9. **Free alternative** — What are members giving up by staying on the free path?
10. **Guarantee** — Do you offer a trial period or money-back guarantee?

## The Psychology Behind Community Sales Pages

This skill uses community-specific conversion levers — not the course frameworks that kill community conversions:

**Belonging > Curriculum** — People join communities to stop feeling alone. Lead with isolation, not information.

**Access > Content** — The most powerful community differentiator is how close you get to the creator. Sell access before curriculum.

**Transformation through Connection** — Course pages promise skills. Community pages promise who you'll become when surrounded by the right people.

**Ongoing Value > One-Time Consumption** — Communities justify recurring payment through live interaction, fresh content, and peer relationships that compound. Hammer the "still paying off in year 2" angle.

**Churn Prevention in the Copy** — Great community sales pages pre-answer "why would I stay?" Questions like "how active is the creator?" and "will this be a ghost town?" are churn objections disguised as sales objections. Address them on the page.

**Social Proof of Transformation, Not Just Outcomes** — "I made $X" is weak community proof. "I had 12 people in my corner when I almost quit, and now I'm at $X" converts.

## Common Mistakes Community Pages Make

❌ **Listing features instead of feelings** — "Weekly live calls" sells less than "Every Tuesday you have 47 people in your corner who know exactly where you're stuck"

❌ **Copying course sales page structure** — Curriculum breakdowns don't sell memberships; transformation stories do

❌ **Underselling creator access** — Access to you is your #1 differentiator. Most community pages bury it in bullet 4 of a feature list

❌ **No specifics in social proof** — "Members love it" vs. "67 members crossed 1,000 subscribers in their first 60 days"

❌ **Vague cancellation policy** — Hiding your cancellation terms creates distrust. State them clearly and early — it increases conversions

❌ **Not addressing churn objections** — "Will this community be active in 6 months?" is the real question. Answer it explicitly
