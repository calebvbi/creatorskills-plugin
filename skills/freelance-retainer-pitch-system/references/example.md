# Freelance Retainer Pitch System — Worked Examples

Two complete examples showing the system in action for different freelance service types.

## Example 1: Social Media Manager — Converting a 3-Month Client

### Creator Input

```
I'm a social media manager. I've been working with a wellness brand (supplement company,
~40K Instagram followers) for 3 months. Each month they hire me for a "content batch":
20 Instagram posts (graphics + captions), 1 round of revisions, delivered in 7 days.
I charge $1,200 per batch. They always pay on time, the briefs are usually decent,
and last week they said they're planning to "keep the momentum going" for the rest of the year.

I want to pitch them a retainer. Can you score them as a candidate, scope it,
and write me the email?
```

### Step 1: Candidate Assessment Output

---

**Retainer Candidate Score: Strong (7/8 signals)**

Based on what you've described, this client is a strong retainer candidate. Here's the breakdown:

**High-fit signals present:**
- Hired you 3 consecutive months — the pattern is already a retainer in practice
- Explicitly said they plan to "keep the momentum going" — directional commitment stated
- Consistent content need (social media is never one-and-done)
- Pays on time, every time
- Briefs are usable (not perfect, but not chaotic)
- The business is growth-focused (building Instagram presence)

**Signals not assessed (don't have data):**
- Referrals to others

**Caution flags:**
- None present

**Recommendation:** Make the pitch. This is exactly the client type retainers are built for. They've already bought into the recurring model — you're just formalizing it.

---

### Step 2: Retainer Scope Output

---

**Recommended Scope: Core Option**

Based on your current arrangement ($1,200/month for 20 posts), here's how to structure the retainer with a 15% discount:

**Monthly Retainer: $1,020/month**

What's included:
- 20 Instagram posts per month (custom graphics + on-brand captions)
- 2 rounds of revisions per post (up from your current 1 — adds value without significant extra work)
- Content calendar delivered by the 25th of each month for the following month
- Delivery window: all graphics and captions within 7 business days of approved brief

Not included:
- Instagram Story design (separate rate: $50/story set)
- Video editing or Reels production
- Paid ad creative
- Work on platforms other than Instagram
- Rush content with less than 3 business days notice (rush rate: $95/hour)

**Minimum commitment:** 3 months\
**After initial term:** Month-to-month, 30 days written notice to cancel from either party\
**Rate escalation:** Standard 10% at 12-month mark\
**Billing:** Invoiced on the 1st of each month, due within 5 business days

**Optional Starter tier (if they push back on commitment):**
- 12 posts/month (reduced from 20)
- 1 round of revisions
- Same turnaround and exclusions
- $680/month, 3-month minimum

## Example 2: Video Editor — Premium Retainer Pitch to a SaaS Brand

### Creator Input

```
I'm a freelance video editor. A B2B SaaS company (project management tool) has hired me
for 4 YouTube video edits over the past 6 weeks — 2 long-form product tutorials (15–20 min)
and 2 Shorts per month at $450 per long-form and $150 per Short. So about $1,200/month
in project fees currently.

They're consistently happy with the work, always have more videos in the pipeline,
and their head of marketing mentioned they're doubling their YouTube output next quarter.
I want to pitch a premium retainer that captures that expansion. I'm thinking 3 tiers.
```

### Candidate Assessment

---

**Retainer Candidate Score: Strong (6/7 assessed signals)**

This client checks all the boxes you'd want. They've hired you repeatedly, the content need is growing (not stable, growing), they've expressed satisfaction, and the head of marketing is thinking about expansion — which means there's budget appetite and strategic intent behind the YouTube channel.

The fact that they're doubling output next quarter is a time-sensitive signal: if you don't formalize the retainer before that expansion, they may onboard another editor to handle the volume.

**Recommendation:** Pitch the premium tier as your lead option, but present all three. They're in growth mode — the Core and Premium will be more relevant than Starter.

---

### Tiered Scope Output

---

**Option A — Starter**\
$900/month

- 4 YouTube Shorts per month (up to 60 seconds each)
- 2 rounds of revisions per Short
- Delivery: 3 business days from raw footage
- Includes: captions, branded end screen
- Not included: long-form editing, thumbnails, music licensing

Best for: Getting started on the retainer before they ramp up.

---

**Option B — Core** ⭐ Recommended\
$1,700/month

- 2 long-form tutorials per month (up to 25 minutes)
- 4 YouTube Shorts per month
- 2 rounds of revisions per video
- Delivery: long-form in 5 business days, Shorts in 3 business days
- Includes: color correction, audio cleanup, branded intro/outro, captions, end screens
- Not included: thumbnail design, motion graphics packages, raw footage back-up hosting

Best for: Matching their current volume with the consistency of a retained relationship.

---

**Option C — Premium**\
$2,800/month

- 4 long-form tutorials per month (up to 25 minutes each)
- 6 YouTube Shorts per month
- 3 rounds of revisions per video
- Delivery: long-form in 5 business days, Shorts in 2 business days
- Priority scheduling: your projects jump the queue
- Monthly content review call (30 min) — what's working, what to adjust in next month's production
- Includes: everything in Core, plus custom chapter markers + timestamps + YouTube description drafts
- Not included: thumbnail design, paid promotion assets, motion graphics beyond standard package

Best for: The doubled YouTube output they're planning next quarter.

---

**All options:** 3-month minimum, then month-to-month with 30 days written notice. Rush work (under 2 business days notice): $125/hour. Invoiced on the 1st, due within 7 business days. Rate subject to 10% increase at 12-month renewal.

**Client response:** "The Premium is more than we'd planned to spend. Can we do the Core but with faster turnaround on the long-form?"

**Suggested reply:**

Hi [Name],

Totally understand. A faster turnaround on long-form is possible — here's how I'd frame it:

Core at $1,700/month already includes 5-business-day delivery on tutorials, which gets you a finished edit within a week of receiving footage. If you need 3-business-day turnaround on long-form (to match the Shorts timeline), that's meaningful additional capacity I'd need to hold — so I'd add $200/month to the Core rate for that, bringing it to $1,900/month.

So your options at this point:
- **Core at $1,700/month** — 5-day long-form, 3-day Shorts (standard)
- **Core + Fast Delivery at $1,900/month** — 3-day turnaround on everything

If the 5-day window works for your production calendar, the standard Core is the right pick. Most of my clients find it does.

Want me to send over the agreement for whichever option you'd like to start with?

[Your name]
