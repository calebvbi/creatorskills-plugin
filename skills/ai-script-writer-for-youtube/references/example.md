# AI Script Writer for YouTube: no-history example

## Creator input

> Write a short YouTube script about planning a small Twitch channel. Audience: new streamers. My tips are to pick one game or topic, keep a schedule I can sustain, and review moments when chat joins in. I have no case studies or income data. Give me three hooks and one draft.

## Hooks

1. Before you add another streaming day, decide what your next stream is for.
2. A small channel plan can fit on one page: topic, schedule and one thing to learn.
3. If your stream plan is just “go live more,” try making it more specific.

## Draft

**Hook**
Before you add another streaming day, decide what your next stream is for.

**Introduction**
If you're starting a Twitch channel, you need a plan you can actually use. Here are three decisions to make before your next stream.

**One: choose a clear topic**
Pick one game or subject for the next few streams. Write down what a viewer will find there. For example, “beginner puzzle-game attempts with room for chat suggestions” gives you a more concrete plan than “gaming.” That example is a possible format, not a claim about anyone's channel.

**Two: choose a schedule you can sustain**
Look at the time you have for setup, streaming and reviewing the session. Choose a schedule that fits that time. Put the next dates where viewers can find them once you've confirmed them.

**Three: notice when conversation starts**
After the stream, review a moment when chat joined in. What was happening? Was there a clear question, a surprising result or a choice viewers could help with? Write down one idea to try next time. If nobody chatted yet, prepare one simple invitation and see what happens.

**Action**
Before your next stream, fill in these three lines: my topic is __; my next session is __; the interaction I want to try is __.

**Close**
Which decision is hardest for you right now: topic, schedule or starting a conversation?
