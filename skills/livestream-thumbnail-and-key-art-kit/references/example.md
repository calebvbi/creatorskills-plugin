## Pre-Stream Thumbnail: Concept 1 (Recommended)

**Why this works:** Ranked grinds live or die on stakes. "Going for X rank" is a story with a clear finish line — the thumbnail should sell the climb, not a generic gameplay screenshot you don't have yet.

### Composition
- **Layout:** Face cam frame (webcam-style crop, not full body) positioned left-third, intense focused expression. Right two-thirds: bold rank badge graphic (Immortal icon silhouette) with a magenta glow.
- **Live signal (pre-stream only):** Pulsing "LIVE" tag top-left in brand magenta, plus a lightning-bolt icon animation cue (static frame shows the bolt mid-strike) reinforcing the brand motif as the urgency signal — not just a platform badge.

### Text Overlay
- **Copy:** "IMMORTAL OR BUST"
- **Placement & style:** Bottom third, bold condensed sans, white with magenta outline, angled 3-5 degrees for energy.

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Near-black (#0D0D12)
- **Secondary:** Magenta (#E0299D)
- **Accent:** Electric white (#F5F5F7) for the rank badge glow

### Image Generation Prompt
```
16:9 Twitch livestream thumbnail, competitive gaming energy. Left third:
stylized webcam-style portrait of a focused female gamer, intense
expression, dramatic side lighting in magenta (#E0299D) against a
near-black (#0D0D12) background. Right two-thirds: bold glowing
"Immortal" rank badge silhouette with a lightning bolt motif striking
through it. Pulsing red-magenta "LIVE" tag in the top-left corner.
Bold condensed white text reading "IMMORTAL OR BUST" angled slightly,
outlined in magenta, positioned in the bottom third. High contrast,
esports broadcast aesthetic, no watermarks.
```

## Pre-Stream Thumbnail: Concept 2

**Why this works:** Some viewers scroll past intensity — a countdown/progress framing appeals to viewers who like watching a grind unfold in real time.

### Composition
- **Layout:** Rank ladder graphic (Diamond → Immortal) running diagonally across the frame, current position marked with the lightning-bolt motif. No face required.
- **Live signal (pre-stream only):** Animated-style "LIVE NOW" ribbon across the top edge in magenta, distinct from a static platform badge.

### Text Overlay
- **Copy:** "THE FINAL PUSH"

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Near-black (#0D0D12) / **Secondary:** Magenta (#E0299D) / **Accent:** Muted gray-white for the ladder rungs

### Image Generation Prompt
```
16:9 Twitch thumbnail, dramatic rank-up progression graphic. Diagonal
ladder of rank icons rising from Diamond to Immortal against a
near-black (#0D0D12) background, current rung glowing in magenta
(#E0299D) with a lightning-bolt accent mark. Bold white stacked text
"THE FINAL PUSH" center-right with subtle magenta drop shadow. Magenta
"LIVE NOW" ribbon across the top edge. Clean esports broadcast style,
high contrast, no gameplay screenshot needed.
```

## My Pick: Concept 1

**Why:** The face-forward composition with an explicit rank goal outperforms in Twitch's live category browse, where viewers decide in under a second whether a real person is behind the stream. Concept 2 works as a strong alternate for viewers who've already followed the grind across multiple streams.

## Channel Key Art System

### Visual Identity
- **Primary color:** Cream (#F4F1EA)
- **Secondary color:** Sage green (#8FA98C)
- **Recurring motif:** A simple potted-plant line icon, used as a corner accent across all three pieces
- **Font pairing:** Header — soft rounded serif (editorial, calm); Body — clean geometric sans for schedule/labels

### Banner (1920x480, safe zone center 1200x480)
Cream background with a subtle sage-green desk illustration (laptop, plant, coffee cup) anchored bottom-center, kept inside the safe zone. Channel name in the rounded serif, sage green, centered top of the safe zone. Small potted-plant icon motif in the bottom-right corner, outside the safe zone so it survives mobile cropping without losing the name.

```
Generation prompt: Minimal YouTube channel banner, 1920x480, cream
(#F4F1EA) background, soft sage green (#8FA98C) flat-illustration desk
scene — laptop, small plant, coffee cup — anchored bottom-center within
a 1200x480 safe zone. Channel name "StudyWithMattias" in a soft rounded
serif font, sage green, centered in the upper safe zone. Small
potted-plant line icon in the bottom-right corner. Calm, minimal,
editorial aesthetic, no clutter.
```

### Offline Screen (1920x1080)
Same cream/sage palette. Center message: "Back for the next session" plus the streaming schedule (days/times) in the geometric sans below it. Potted-plant motif repeated as a small corner accent, tying it to the banner.

```
Generation prompt: YouTube offline screen, 1920x1080, cream (#F4F1EA)
background, centered sage green (#8FA98C) rounded serif headline
"Back for the next session," geometric sans subtext showing a weekly
schedule (Mon/Wed/Fri 6-10PM EST), small potted-plant line icon in the
bottom-right corner matching the banner. Calm, minimal, plenty of
negative space.
```

### Panel Header Style (recurring template for About/Rules/Schedule panels)
Every panel header uses the same cream background with a thin sage-green rule line beneath the header text, header text in the rounded serif, and the potted-plant icon shrunk to a small favicon-size mark in the top-left of each header — consistent enough that the panel row reads as one designed set rather than three separate images.

**System check:** All three pieces share the cream/sage palette, the rounded serif for headlines, the geometric sans for supporting text, and the potted-plant motif as the one recurring visual anchor — the channel reads as one deliberate brand across banner, offline screen, and panels.
