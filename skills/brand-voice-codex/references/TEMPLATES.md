## 2. Quick Voice Audit Checklist

Don't have time for a full codex? Answer these 10 questions to get a rough voice profile in 5 minutes. Your answers can also serve as input for the AI analysis.

### The 10 Questions

1. **If your content were a person at a party, how would they act?** (The wallflower with the sharpest one-liners? The loud storyteller everyone gathers around? The person giving genuinely useful advice in the kitchen?)

2. **What three words would your most loyal followers use to describe your style?** (Not what you want them to say — what they'd actually say.)

3. **Finish this sentence: "I'd never say _____ in my content."** (What words, phrases, or tones are off-limits for you?)

4. **Finish this sentence: "You can always tell it's me because I _____ ."** (What's your tell? A catchphrase? A structural habit? A type of joke?)

5. **When you're writing and something sounds "too AI," what specifically feels wrong about it?** (Be concrete — is it word choice? Sentence length? Tone? All of the above?)

6. **Do you swear in your content? If yes, which words and how often?** (This is a real part of voice. Own it or note its absence.)

7. **How do you start most of your videos/posts/emails?** (Jump straight in? A greeting? A question? A bold statement?)

8. **How do you handle disagreement or criticism in your content?** (Direct confrontation? Diplomatic reframing? Humor? Ignore it?)

9. **Who do you sound NOTHING like?** (Name a creator or brand whose tone is the opposite of yours.)

10. **Read your last 5 posts out loud. What patterns do you hear?** (Sentence rhythm, repeated phrases, energy level, anything.)

## 4. AI Instructions Template (Portable Snippet Format)

This is the template for the portable AI prompt snippet — the block of text a creator pastes into any AI tool to get voice-matched output. Fill in the brackets and the result is ready to copy-paste.

```
[START VOICE INSTRUCTIONS]

Write in the voice of [creator name/handle]. Here are the key parameters:

TONE: [2-3 sentence tone description. Be specific — not "casual and friendly"
but the exact flavor of casual and friendly this person embodies.]

VOCABULARY:
- Use words/phrases like: [list 8-12 signature words, comma-separated]
- Never use: [list 8-12 banned words, comma-separated]
- Jargon level: [none / light / moderate / heavy — with context]
- Profanity: [none / rare / occasional / frequent — with specifics if applicable]

SENTENCE STYLE:
- Average sentence length: [X words], ranging from [X] to [X]
- Fragments: [frequent / occasional / rare]
- Questions: [frequent / occasional / rare — rhetorical or genuine]
- Paragraph length: [short (1-2 sentences) / medium (3-4) / long (5+) / mixed]
- Transitions: [formal connectors / conversational bridges / hard cuts]

PERSONALITY:
- Humor: [type and frequency]
- Storytelling: [yes/no — if yes, what kind]
- Emoji: [none / minimal / moderate / heavy]
- Emphasis style: [caps / bold / italics / word choice / punctuation]

WRITE LIKE THIS:
"[One example sentence that nails their voice — pulled from actual content or
faithfully constructed from the analysis.]"

NOT LIKE THIS:
"[The same idea written in generic AI voice — to show the contrast.]"

When in doubt, [one sentence capturing the creator's default mode — the
voice they return to when they're not thinking about it].

[END VOICE INSTRUCTIONS]
```

**Usage notes:**
- Keep the snippet between 200 and 400 words. Longer instructions get ignored or diluted by most AI tools.
- The "Write like this / Not like this" pair is the single most effective part. Make it count.
- The final "when in doubt" sentence acts as a tiebreaker. It should capture the creator's center of gravity.
- Test the snippet by pasting it into a fresh AI conversation and asking it to write a short post. If the output doesn't feel different from un-instructed output, the snippet is too vague.
