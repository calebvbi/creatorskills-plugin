## Interview questions

### Warm-up

1. How would you describe the product and the people it serves?
2. What were you working on when you first started it?

### Core conversation

3. What did building alongside full-time work look like in practice?
4. Which early decision would you make differently now?
5. What does “solo” mean in your business: which work do you do and which support do you use?
6. How did you decide what to charge, and how has that changed?
7. What did customers ask for that surprised you?
8. Which operational task has required the most attention?
9. Is there an experience you can share that illustrates that challenge?

### Reflection

10. What part of your story might not transfer to another founder's situation?
11. What are you working on now?
12. Which resources or links should accompany the episode?

No question assumes a childhood background, job title, quit threshold, controversial position or investment activity that was not supplied.
