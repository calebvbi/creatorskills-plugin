## 1. Course Topic Validation Worksheet

Complete this before building a single module. If you can't fill in every field, the course idea isn't ready yet.

```
COURSE TOPIC VALIDATION

Course working title: [Your course name]
Creator: [Your name / channel name]

THE TRANSFORMATION
My student currently: [Their starting point — what they can't do, don't
know, or struggle with]
After this course, my student will: [Specific outcome — what they can
now do, know, or have built]
Transformation in one sentence: [Target student] who [starting point]
will be able to [outcome] by the end of this course.

THE STUDENT
Who is this for (be specific): [Age range, profession, experience level,
situation — "beginner photographers who own a DSLR but shoot in auto"]
What have they already tried: [YouTube tutorials, free guides, other
courses, self-teaching, etc.]
Why haven't those worked: [Too scattered, no structure, no accountability,
too advanced, too basic, etc.]
What would they pay to solve this: $[amount] — and why? [What's the
outcome worth to them?]

THE DIFFERENTIATION
What can students get for free on YouTube right now: [Be honest — what
free content already covers this topic?]
What does this course provide that free content doesn't:
  [ ] Structured path from A to B (not random videos)
  [ ] Hands-on activities with specific outputs
  [ ] Community and accountability
  [ ] Downloadable templates, worksheets, or tools
  [ ] Personal feedback or coaching access
  [ ] A proprietary framework or system
  [ ] [Other: _______________]

If fewer than 2 boxes are checked, the course needs more value
before it's worth charging for.

THE MARKET
Similar courses that exist (list 2-3 with prices):
  1. [Course name] — $[price] — [what it covers]
  2. [Course name] — $[price] — [what it covers]
  3. [Course name] — $[price] — [what it covers]
What's different about mine: [Your unique angle, audience, or approach]

EXISTING CONTENT TO REPURPOSE
YouTube videos on this topic: [count, and which ones are most relevant]
Blog posts or articles: [count]
Newsletter issues: [count]
Templates or downloads you've already made: [list them]
Estimated % of course content you already have: [X]%

READINESS CHECK
  [ ] I can state the transformation in one clear sentence
  [ ] I know exactly who the student is
  [ ] I have at least 2 differentiators from free content
  [ ] I have existing content I can repurpose
  [ ] My target price matches my audience's ability to pay
  [ ] I have an audience to sell to (email list, subscribers, followers)
```

## 4. Pricing Calculator Framework

Work through this framework to set your course price. Fill in the numbers, then compare the three approaches to find your pricing sweet spot.

```
COURSE PRICING CALCULATOR

COURSE: [Title]
Target student: [Who]
Course format: [Self-paced / Cohort / Hybrid]

===================================
APPROACH 1: VALUE-BASED PRICING
===================================

What outcome does the student get?
[Describe the result — new skill, new income, saved time, etc.]

What is that outcome worth in dollars?
- If the outcome makes them money: $[amount] per [month/year/project]
- If the outcome saves them time: [hours saved] x $[their hourly value]
- If the outcome replaces a service: $[cost of alternative]

Suggested price range (10-20% of outcome value):
$[low end] — $[high end]

===================================
APPROACH 2: COMPETITOR BENCHMARKING
===================================

Similar courses on the market:
1. [Course name] — $[price] — [what's included]
2. [Course name] — $[price] — [what's included]
3. [Course name] — $[price] — [what's included]

Average competitor price: $[amount]
Your differentiator: [What you offer that they don't]

Suggested price relative to competitors:
  [ ] Below market (if less support or shorter) — $[amount]
  [ ] At market (if comparable) — $[amount]
  [ ] Above market (if more support, better community,
      or unique framework) — $[amount]

===================================
APPROACH 3: AUDIENCE ABILITY TO PAY
===================================

My audience is primarily: [profession/demographic]
Their typical income range: $[low]-$[high] per year
What they currently spend on learning/courses: $[amount] per year
Their spending mindset:
  [ ] Hobbyist (discretionary spending, price-sensitive)
  [ ] Career-oriented (investment spending, ROI-focused)
  [ ] Business owner (expense spending, outcome-focused)

Maximum price for this audience without significant friction:
$[amount]

===================================
FINAL PRICING DECISION
===================================

Value-based range: $[low] — $[high]
Competitor range: $[low] — $[high]
Audience ceiling: $[amount]

Selected price: $[amount]
Rationale: [1-2 sentences explaining your choice]

TIER STRUCTURE (if applicable):

Tier 1 — [Name]: $[price]
  Includes: [list]

Tier 2 — [Name]: $[price]
  Includes: [everything in Tier 1 + ...]

Tier 3 — [Name]: $[price]
  Includes: [everything in Tier 2 + ...]

REVENUE PROJECTIONS (first launch):
Audience size: [email list + social following]
Conservative conversion rate: [0.5-1% of email list]
Expected sales: [number]
Projected revenue: $[amount]
```

## 5. Launch Sequence Template

Adapt this timeline based on your audience size and course format. The specific dates don't matter — the sequence and spacing do.

```
COURSE LAUNCH SEQUENCE

COURSE: [Title]
Launch date: [Date]
Audience channels: [YouTube / Email / Instagram / TikTok / etc.]
Email list size: [count]
Social following: [count per platform]

===================================
PHASE 1: SEED (Days -21 to -14)
===================================

Goal: Build awareness about the TOPIC (not the course yet)

Content to publish:
- [Platform]: [Content piece about the course topic — blog, video,
  or social post that hints at your expertise without selling]
- [Platform]: [Second content piece — different angle on the topic]
- [Platform]: [Third content piece — audience engagement post:
  poll, question, or "what's your biggest struggle with [topic]?"]

Email (Day -18):
Subject: [Topic-related subject line, NOT about the course]
Content: Share a personal story or insight about the topic.
End with a question that invites replies.

===================================
PHASE 2: ANNOUNCE + WAITLIST (Days -14 to -7)
===================================

Goal: Announce the course exists and collect waitlist signups

Waitlist page includes:
- Course name and one-sentence description
- What they'll learn (3-4 bullet points)
- "Join the waitlist for early access + early-bird pricing"
- Email capture form

Email (Day -14): The Announcement
Subject: [Something personal — "I built the thing I wish existed
when I started [topic]"]
Content: Announce the course. Share the transformation promise.
Link to the waitlist. Mention early-bird pricing for waitlist only.

Social posts (Days -14 to -7):
- Behind-the-scenes content creation posts
- Sneak peeks of course content or templates
- Testimonials from beta students (if applicable)
- Waitlist reminder with deadline

Email (Day -8): The Waitlist Reminder
Subject: [Urgency angle — "Early bird pricing goes live in 7 days"]
Content: Remind them about early-bird pricing. Share one piece of
valuable course content as a preview.

===================================
PHASE 3: CART OPEN (Days 0 to 5-7)
===================================

Goal: Drive sales during the open enrollment window

Email (Day 0): Cart Open — Early Bird
Subject: [Direct — "It's here: [Course Name]"]
Content: Full course details. Early-bird pricing (waitlist only or
first 48 hours). Clear CTA to enroll.

Email (Day 1): The Deep Dive
Subject: [What's inside — "Here's exactly what you'll learn"]
Content: Module-by-module breakdown with outcomes. Address the
question "is this for me?" with specific student profiles.

Email (Day 3): The Objection Buster
Subject: [Address the top hesitation — "Is this worth it if I
already know [basic version of the topic]?"]
Content: Handle 2-3 common objections. Include testimonials
or beta student results.

Email (Day 5): The Deadline Warning
Subject: [Urgency — "[Price] is ending tomorrow"]
Content: Remind them enrollment closes or price increases.
Recap the transformation. Social proof.

Email (Day 6): Final Call
Subject: [Last chance — "Closing tonight at midnight"]
Content: Short. Direct. This is it. Enroll or wait for the next
cohort (if cohort-based) / miss the launch price (if evergreen).

Social posts throughout cart open:
- FAQ answers as individual posts
- Student testimonials or beta results
- "Day X of enrollment — here's what people are saying"
- Countdown to deadline

===================================
PHASE 4: POST-LAUNCH (Days 7-14)
===================================

Goal: Welcome students, close the launch, and set up for next round

Email to BUYERS (Day 7): Welcome + Getting Started
Subject: [Warm welcome — "Your course is ready — here's where
to start"]
Content: Login instructions, community link, what to do first,
schedule for live calls (if applicable).

Email to NON-BUYERS (Day 7): The Soft Close
Subject: [No pressure — "You didn't join — and that's okay"]
Content: Acknowledge they chose not to enroll. Offer a free
resource related to the course topic (builds goodwill for next
launch). Mention when enrollment reopens.

Post-launch debrief (for yourself):
- Total sales: [#]
- Revenue: $[amount]
- Conversion rate: [email list to sales %]
- Top-performing email: [which one drove the most sales]
- What to improve for next launch: [notes]
```
