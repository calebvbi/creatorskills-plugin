# Short-Form Video Producer: a three-video plan without invented results

## Creator brief

A beginner baking creator wants three short videos per week for Reels and Shorts. They can record on a phone and edit simple cuts. Their supplied material: keep notes on room temperature, flour and proofing time; change one variable at a time; a warm spot alone does not guarantee a successful loaf. No personal transformation, measured result, existing footage or music license is supplied.

## Weekly draft calendar

| Slot | Format | Topic | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| First available slot | Checklist | Three notes for your next bake | Give a useful starting point |
| Second slot | Demonstration plan | Set up a one-variable comparison | Explain the process |
| Third slot | Question | What do your notes miss? | Invite relevant discussion |

Dates and posting times depend on creator availability and audience information. No posts have been scheduled.

## Video 1: three notes

**Spoken script:** “For your next bake, start with three notes: the flour you use, the room temperature and the proofing time. If several things change at once, it gets harder to interpret the difference. You don't need a complicated system. Write those details on one page, then add what you observed.”

**Visual plan:** Overhead shot of a blank notebook. Write each label as it is mentioned. Use the creator's actual ingredients if shown; no stock image implies a measured result.

**On-screen copy:** “Flour / Room temperature / Proofing time.”

**Audio:** Recorded narration. Music is optional; use only a track the creator has rights to use on the chosen platform. No soundtrack was generated or licensed here.

**Caption:** “Start with a few notes you can compare next time.”

## Video 2: change one variable

**Spoken script:** “Want to compare two bakes? Decide what you'll change before you start. Try to keep the other conditions similar and record what actually happens. If you change the flour and the proofing location at the same time, you won't know which difference mattered. One variable at a time gives you a clearer question to investigate.”

**Visual plan:** A simple planning card with columns for “keep similar,” “change” and “observe.” This is a proposed demonstration, not a completed experiment.

**On-screen copy:** “Plan the comparison before the bake.”

**Caption:** “What variable would you choose to examine?”

## Video 3: preserve the caveat

**Spoken script:** “A warmer spot doesn't guarantee a better loaf. It is one condition among the things worth recording. If you're troubleshooting a bake, look at your notes before deciding what caused the result. What detail do you wish you'd written down last time?”

**Visual plan:** Talking head or notebook close-up. No unsupported before-and-after montage.

**On-screen copy:** “A condition to record, not a guaranteed fix.”

**Caption:** “Keep the uncertainty in your notes too.”
