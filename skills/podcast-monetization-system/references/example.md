# Podcast Monetization System — Worked Examples

## Example 1: First Sponsor Deal — 4,200-Download Finance Podcast

### Creator Context

- **Show:** "Debt Free in Your 30s" — biweekly personal finance podcast
- **Niche:** Debt payoff strategies for millennial professionals
- **Downloads:** 4,200 per episode (8,400/month)
- **Episode frequency:** Biweekly (2/month)
- **Show history:** 18 months, 38 published episodes, 4.8-star average (212 ratings)
- **Newsletter:** 1,100 subscribers
- **Current monetization:** None. A budgeting app just emailed asking about sponsorship.
- **Goal:** "I have no idea what to charge. I don't want to under-price myself but I don't want to lose the deal."

### Host-Read Ad Script Output

*Request: 90-second mid-roll for a budgeting app. App helps users track spending, set savings goals, and visualize their net worth over time. Promo: 30-day free trial.*

```
HOST-READ AD SCRIPT — BUDGETING APP — 90-SECOND MID-ROLL

[LEAD-IN]
"Before I get into the next section — quick word from today's sponsor..."

[HOOK — 10 seconds]
"If you've ever gone to check your bank account and immediately wished you
hadn't — I get it. Most of us have a rough sense of where the money goes.

[PRODUCT INTRO — 15 seconds]
"[Brand] is a budgeting app that actually shows you where you stand. Not just
your spending — your full financial picture. Checking accounts, savings, debt

[ENDORSEMENT — 20 seconds]
"I've been messing around with it for the last few weeks. What I appreciate
is it doesn't just track — it flags stuff. I got a notification that my
grocery spending was up 24% from last month. I had no idea. That's exactly
the kind of thing that sneaks up on you when you're focused on paying off debt."

[CTA — 15 seconds]
"They're offering a 30-day free trial — no credit card required.
Go to [URL]. That's [URL]. See your full financial picture before
you make your next budget call.

[URL]. And now back to the episode."

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

- "I've been messing around with it" should feel casual; don't read it formally
- Slow down slightly on the URL, especially the repeat
```

## Example 2: Revenue Diversification Plan — 12,000-Download True Crime Podcast

### Creator Context

- **Show:** "Cold Files" — weekly true crime podcast covering unsolved cases
- **Niche:** True crime, focus on regional US cases with documentary depth
- **Downloads:** 12,000 per episode (48,000/month)
- **Episodes:** Weekly, 4/month, 90–120 minutes each
- **History:** 3.5 years, 170+ episodes, 4.7 stars (980 ratings)
- **Newsletter:** 4,200 subscribers, 42% open rate
- **Current monetization:** $600/month from two direct sponsors (renewable quarterly). Inconsistent.
- **Goal:** "I want to reach $3,000/month reliably. Right now it's feast or famine — one sponsor pulls out and I'm at zero."

---
