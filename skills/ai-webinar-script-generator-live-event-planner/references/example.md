# AI Webinar Script Generator — Worked Example

## Input

> "I'm running my first webinar to sell my course: 'YouTube Scripting System' — $497. I have 1,800 email subscribers and expect 80–120 live attendees. Past objections: 'I don't have time,' 'my niche is too small,' 'is this just about prompts?' Platform: Zoom. Using slides. Second webinar ever."

## Output

### Webinar Recommendation

- **Topic fit:** Strong — scripting is a specific, high-pain problem with a clear transformation
- **Biggest opportunity:** Attendees have likely tried generic prompts and failed; positioning around a system vs. a prompt is the key differentiator
- **Biggest risk:** Course price ($497) from a second webinar may feel high; the authority segment needs to work hard
- **Recommended length:** 60 minutes

### Script Structure Overview

| Segment | Time | Format |
|---------|------|--------|
| Hook | 0:00–5:00 | Bold promise + self-selection |
| Authority | 5:00–10:00 | Personal result story |
| Teaching | 10:00–40:00 | 4 frameworks + case studies |
| Bridge | 40:00–45:00 | "What I just showed you works — but most people get stuck at [specific step]" |
| Offer | 45:00–55:00 | Full presentation + objection handling |
| Q&A + Close | 55:00–60:00 | 3 planned questions + final CTA |

### Live Event Planner

**Tech Checklist (24 hours before):**
- Audio tested with backup phone dial-in
- Screen share confirmed on Zoom
- Slides exported as PDF locally
- Recording enabled and moderator briefed

**Attendee Warmup Sequence:**
- 24 hours: "Tomorrow at [time]. Here's your link + a quick prep question."
- 1 hour: "Going live in 60 minutes. Link inside."
- 15 minutes: "Starting now. Join early — we're doing a live poll at minute one."

### Post-Webinar Follow-Up

**Same Day — Replay:** "Replay + the slide deck from today"
**Day 2 — FAQ:** "The 3 questions everyone asked after yesterday"
**Day 3 — Deadline:** "Fast-action bonus closes tomorrow"
**Day 4–5 — Last chance:** "Doors close at [time]"

### Next 72 Hours

1. Send the replay email within 2 hours
2. Review chat log for unaddressed objections
3. Follow up with no-shows using the "one framework" email
