# Outreach & Collab Planning Templates

## Outreach Message Templates

These are fill-in-the-blank templates. Replace everything in [brackets] with your real information. Each template is designed for a specific situation — pick the one that matches and customize it.

---

### Template 1: Cold DM (Social Media)

Use when reaching out to a creator you've never talked to before, via Instagram DM, Twitter DM, or YouTube message.

```
Hey [First Name] — [specific reference to one of their recent
videos/posts, including what you liked or how it affected you.
Be genuine and concrete, not generic praise].

I make [content type] about [your niche] ([platform], [subscriber
count]). I've got an idea for a collab: [one-sentence concept
that clearly describes the format and why it works for both
audiences].

Would you be up for chatting about it?
```

**Keep it under 100 words.** DMs are casual. Don't over-explain.

---

### Template 2: Cold Email

Use when reaching out to a creator for the first time via email. Slightly more detailed than a DM but still concise.

**Subject line:** [Specific reference to their content] — collab idea

```
Hi [First Name],

[One sentence connecting you to their content — a specific video
you liked, a series you follow, something you've shared with
your own audience. Be specific enough that they know you actually
watch their stuff.]

I'm [Your Name] — I run [channel/account name] on [Platform]
([subscriber count], [avg views per video], [engagement rate]%).
My audience is [one sentence describing your viewers in terms
the other creator would care about].

Here's the idea: [2-3 sentences describing the collab concept.
Be specific about the format, what each person contributes, and
why it benefits both audiences. The other creator should be able
to picture the finished content after reading this.]

[One sentence about logistics — e.g., "I can handle my own
filming and editing" or "Happy to work around your schedule" or
"I'm based in [city] if in-person works, but this also works
great remote."]

Worth a conversation?

[Your Name]
[Channel/Handle] | [Key metric]
[Business email]
```

**Keep it under 150 words.** Respect their inbox.

---

### Template 3: Warm Intro (Mutual Connection)

Use when a mutual friend, fellow creator, or community member can introduce you, or when you've interacted before (comments, replies, same event).

**Subject line:** [Mutual connection's name] suggested we chat

```
Hi [First Name],

[Mutual connection] mentioned that a collab between us could be
a good fit — and after binging your [specific series or recent
content], I agree.

Quick background: I'm [Your Name], I make [content type] for
[audience description] on [Platform] ([subscriber count],
[avg views]). [Mutual connection] and I [how you know them —
past collab, same community, met at event, etc.].

The idea I had: [2-3 sentences with the specific collab concept.
Format, what each person does, why it works for both audiences.]

[Mutual connection] said you might be into this kind of thing.
Would you want to hop on a quick call to talk it through?

[Your Name]
[Channel/Handle]
[Business email]
```

---

### Template 4: Follow-Up Message

Use when you sent a pitch 5-7 days ago and haven't heard back. Only follow up once. If they don't respond to this, move on.

**DM version:**
```
Hey [First Name] — just floating this back up in case it got
buried. No pressure at all — I know how busy things get.

[One sentence restating the core idea, even shorter than the
original pitch.]

If the timing isn't right, totally get it. Just wanted to make
sure it didn't slip past you.
```

**Email version:**

**Subject line:** Re: [Original subject line]

```
Hi [First Name],

Following up on my note from last week about [one-line collab
concept]. I know your inbox is probably a war zone, so no
worries if now isn't the right time.

If you're interested, I'm flexible on timing and format. If not,
no hard feelings — I'll keep watching your stuff either way.

[Your Name]
```

**Rules for follow-ups:**
- Wait at least 5 days before following up
- Only follow up once — a second follow-up crosses into pestering
- Keep it shorter than the original pitch
- Give them an easy out ("no worries if now isn't the right time")
- Don't guilt-trip ("I noticed you haven't responded")
- Don't re-send the entire original pitch

### Template 5: Collab Proposal Document

Use once a creator says "yes, I'm interested" and you need to formalize the plan. Send this as a shared doc or clean email. This is not the initial pitch — this is the planning document after they've agreed to talk.

**Subject line:** [Your Channel] x [Their Channel] — collab plan

```
Hey [First Name],

Great chatting with you — here's a quick outline so we're on the
same page. Let me know what you'd change.

CONCEPT
[2-3 sentences describing the collab content idea]

FORMAT
- Type: [Guest appearance / Joint video / Challenge / Series swap / etc.]
- Length: [Target duration for final content]
- Recording: [In-person / Remote via Zoom/Riverside / Async]

WHAT EACH SIDE DOES
[Your Name]:
- [Deliverable 1]
- [Deliverable 2]
- [Any prep work you'll handle]

[Their Name]:
- [Deliverable 1]
- [Deliverable 2]
- [Any prep work they'd handle]

TIMELINE
- [Date]: Prep work due (outlines, talking points, tool picks, etc.)
- [Date]: Film / record
- [Date]: Editing done, share rough cuts for review
- [Date]: Both publish (aim for same day if possible)

PROMOTION
- Both [pin a comment / post a Story / mention verbally] linking
  to the other's version
- [Any other promotion commitments]

REVENUE
- [Each keeps own ad revenue / Split sponsored deal X% / etc.]

CREDIT
- [Both named in titles and thumbnails / Guest credit in description / etc.]

Let me know if anything needs adjusting. Happy to hop on another
quick call if it's easier to hash out the details that way.

[Your Name]
```

## Collab Planning Worksheet

Use this worksheet to organize your thinking before you pitch anyone. Fill it out, then use the templates above for the actual outreach.

---

### Part 1: Know Your Channel

```
MY CHANNEL SNAPSHOT

Platform: [Primary platform]
Niche: [Be specific — not "tech" but "budget Android phone reviews"]
Subscribers/Followers: [Count]
Average views per video/post: [Count]
Engagement rate: [%]
Upload frequency: [How often]

Past collabs: [List any, or "none yet"]
What worked: [If applicable]
What didn't: [If applicable]

My collab goal: [More subscribers? New audience? Content variety?
A specific format I want to try?]

What I bring to a collab: [Your unique angle, expertise, or
audience demographic that a partner would value]
```

---

```
Audience relevance (1-10): [Score]
  Why: [One sentence]

Size compatibility (1-10): [Score]
  Why: [One sentence]

Content style match (1-10): [Score]
  Why: [One sentence]

Engagement quality (1-10): [Score]
  Why: [One sentence]

Collab history (1-10): [Score]
  Why: [One sentence]

OVERALL SCORE: [Average / 10]

Recent video I'd reference in my pitch: [Title and what I liked]

My collab idea for them: [One sentence — the concept]

Why this benefits THEIR audience: [One sentence — what their
viewers get out of this]
```

---

### Part 3: Pre-Filming Checklist

Use this after a creator says yes, before anyone starts recording.

```
PRE-FILMING AGREEMENT

Both sides have agreed on:

[ ] Concept and format
[ ] Who films what
[ ] Who edits what
[ ] Recording date/time
[ ] Edit review deadline
[ ] Publish date (same day for both if applicable)
[ ] Thumbnail coordination (both thumbnails reference each other)
[ ] Title coordination (both titles name both creators)
[ ] Promotion plan (pinned comments, Stories, verbal mentions)
[ ] Revenue arrangement (each keeps own / split / other)
[ ] What happens if one side isn't happy with the final content
[ ] Follow-up plan (one-off or potential series?)
```

---

### Part 4: Post-Collab Review

Fill this out after the collab publishes. Use it to decide whether to do a follow-up.

```
POST-COLLAB RESULTS

Collab partner: [Name]
Publish date: [Date]

My video performance:
  Views (7 days): [Count]
  Views vs. my average: [Above / Below / About average]
  New subscribers attributed: [If trackable]
  Engagement rate on collab video: [%]
  Top comment themes: [What did viewers say?]

Partner's video performance (if visible):
  Views (7 days): [Count]
  My channel mentioned in their comments? [Yes / No]

What went well: [1-2 sentences]
What I'd do differently: [1-2 sentences]
Would I collab with them again? [Yes / No / Maybe — why]
Should I propose a Part 2? [Yes — here's the concept / No — here's why]
```
