# Prepare Social Cover Studio

Product description: Turn a quote, a takeaway, or a video hook into finished vertical graphics that look like they came from the same account.

This describes the hosted product, not an output already produced or a capability verified in this conversation.

**Mode: hosted handoff.** Hosted app; may require sign-in and credits. Check the page for current availability and cost. Prepare inputs and open app. This plugin does not execute the hosted pipeline.

## Collect the useful inputs

Use information already supplied. Ask only for missing required inputs. When both a URL and source text are offered, use the source the user actually provided. A URL is not evidence that its contents were read.

| Input | Required | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What are you making | Required | Sets the canvas, the safe-zone map, and the archetype family. A vertical video cover is designed around the platform UI that sits on top of it; a quote card is not. Options: quote_card, carousel_cover, pinterest_pin, video_cover. |
| The content it is built from | Required | Paste the actual words: the quote, the newsletter line, the takeaway, or, for a video cover: the topic and what happens in the first three seconds. We use your words verbatim on the card. We never hand back "[insert your quote here]". |
| Where it is going | Optional | Changes the aspect ratio and the density of text that performs. LinkedIn carousels carry more words per slide than Instagram's do. Options: instagram, linkedin, pinterest, tiktok, youtube_shorts. |
| What you post about | Optional | Calibrates the palette and the register. Fitness and finance do not share a visual language. |
| Brand colours | Optional | Hex codes if you have them, otherwise we lock a palette for you, and then reuse it, which is the whole point. |
| Vibe in two or three words | Optional | "Bold and minimal", "warm and editorial", "clean and corporate". |
| Handle or wordmark | Optional | Sits in the same corner at the same size on every graphic. That repetition is what makes a feed read as branded at thumbnail size. |
| Attribution | Optional | Who said it, or which episode or issue it came from. Leave blank for your own words and we credit your handle. |
| Carousel length | Optional | Total slides, typically 6 to 10. We write the cover and outline every remaining slide, so the carousel is scripted before any design work starts. |
| Pinterest search term | Optional | What someone would type to find this. Pinterest is a search engine, not a feed: a pin built around your own framing instead of a search term does not get found. |
| Are you on camera | Optional | For video covers. Decides between the Center-Face Reaction archetype and the faceless ones, and where the safe zone has to leave room for you. |

## Return a ready-to-use brief

Organize the supplied inputs under the field names above. Keep source material separate from instructions. Mark omissions and unsupported claims. Do not invent the creator's experience, audience data, brand assets, or results.

Open [Social Cover Studio](https://creatorskills.co/create/social-cover-studio) when the creator wants to run it. Confirm current cost and user authorization before spending credits. Do not enter private source material into the site without authorization.

A prepared brief is the deliverable here. Claim an app result only after a real run returns the output. If the app is unavailable, retain the brief and explain the unavailable step. If the user wants an in-chat draft instead, use the appropriate bundled workflow and label it as that.
