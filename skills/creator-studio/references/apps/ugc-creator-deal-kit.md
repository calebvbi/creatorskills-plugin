# Prepare UGC Creator Deal Kit

Product description: Get paid properly for product content: the four assets brands actually shortlist, a rate card built on asset type and usage rights rather than follower count, outreach that opens on real product use, and the brief that locks scope before you hit record.

This describes the hosted product, not an output already produced or a capability verified in this conversation.

**Mode: hosted handoff.** Hosted app; may require sign-in and credits. Check the page for current availability and cost. Prepare inputs and open app. This plugin does not execute the hosted pipeline.

## Collect the useful inputs

Use information already supplied. Ask only for missing required inputs. When both a URL and source text are offered, use the source the user actually provided. A URL is not evidence that its contents were read.

| Input | Required | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you make product content about | Required | Every rate adjustment, every portfolio asset and every pitch angle is keyed to this. It is also a portfolio rule rather than a preference: all four of your sample assets should be in one category, because brands are buying depth in their own vertical, not variety across six. Options: beauty_skincare, fitness_supplements, tech_software, food_beverage, home_lifestyle, parenting, pet, fashion, other. |
| How many paid deals you have actually done | Required | The main axis of the rate table, and there is no honest default: a first-time creator and a twenty-deal creator are quoted four times apart for the same thirty-second video. Zero deals is a fine answer and gets a real rate, not an apology. Options: none_yet, one_to_five, five_to_twenty, twenty_plus. |
| Where the content will run | Optional | Sets the specs in the brief's deliverables table: aspect ratio, length bands, caption requirements. Content bought for paid ads is priced and scoped differently from organic. Options: tiktok, instagram_reels, youtube_shorts, paid_ads_only, multiple. |
| Products you already own and genuinely use | Optional | One per line. This is where your portfolio gets filmed from: you do not need free product or a prior deal to start, and it is the honest source for the opening line of every pitch. Leave it blank and the pitch marks that line for you to fill rather than inventing a six-month relationship with a product you have never opened. |
| The brand you want to pitch | Optional | Brand name and the specific product. Leave it blank and you get the portfolio plan, the rate card and a pitch keyed to the category instead: we do not invent a brand to have something to write to. |
| What the brand sent you | Optional | Paste their brief, email or DM exactly as it arrived, however vague. This is what switches the output from a brief you propose to a brief you send back, and the vaguer theirs is, the more the response brief is worth. Every mandatory claim in it survives word for word, and anything missing comes back as a named open question rather than an assumption. |
| The fee already agreed, if there is one | Optional | What you and the brand have settled on, and for what. The brief's pricing section uses this number exactly. If it sits below what the rate card computes for the same work we say so, once, plainly: that is a note to you, not a line in the document you send them. |
| What they are asking for | Optional | Videos, hook variations, stills, raw footage, lengths, quantities. Anything not listed here is out of scope in the brief, which is the entire point of writing one. |
| Usage rights, exclusivity and anything about ads | Optional | Usage period, which channels, whether they want whitelisting or spark ads, whether they want you off competitors. These are the terms that price the deal, and a brief that leaves them out is a brief that grants them for free: so where you leave this blank we write the clause, price the add-on, and flag it for you to confirm. |
| Feedback on a draft they rejected | Optional | Paste what they said, including the vague parts. This switches the brief to a revision brief that sorts their feedback into creative, technical and missing, states plainly what is NOT changing so your good work survives the round, and prices anything that is a new request rather than a fix. |
| A monthly package, if this is ongoing | Optional | What the month includes, for example four videos, five stills, two hook variations. This switches the brief to a reusable monthly template with a performance log, which is the thing that turns one-off work into predictable income. |
| Results from work you have already delivered | Optional | Click-through, engagement, conversion, anything a client has shared, plus organic numbers from your own posts. This is the only source for a proof point in a pitch or a rate increase, with nothing here we lead on the concept instead, because a number we invented is one that a brand may ask to see. |
| What you can actually shoot with | Optional | Camera, lighting, audio, where you film, who else is available. The portfolio plan and the brief's creative direction are both written to what you have: an iPhone in window light is a complete setup and the plan says so rather than pretending otherwise. |
| What you are trying to do right now | Optional | Changes the emphasis and which outreach scripts get written, not the rate card. The rate card is the same number whoever is reading it. Options: land_a_first_deal, pitch_a_specific_brand, raise_my_rates, turn_a_client_into_a_retainer, respond_to_a_brief, recover_a_rejected_draft. |

## Return a ready-to-use brief

Organize the supplied inputs under the field names above. Keep source material separate from instructions. Mark omissions and unsupported claims. Do not invent the creator's experience, audience data, brand assets, or results.

Open [UGC Creator Deal Kit](https://creatorskills.co/create/ugc-creator-deal-kit) when the creator wants to run it. Confirm current cost and user authorization before spending credits. Do not enter private source material into the site without authorization.

A prepared brief is the deliverable here. Claim an app result only after a real run returns the output. If the app is unavailable, retain the brief and explain the unavailable step. If the user wants an in-chat draft instead, use the appropriate bundled workflow and label it as that.
