# Prepare Monetization Strategy Planner

Product description: See every way your channel could make money at the size you are now: each stream costed with real arithmetic, sequenced into a four-phase plan, with the concentration risk that would take your income out named and fixed.

This describes the hosted product, not an output already produced or a capability verified in this conversation.

**Mode: hosted handoff.** Hosted app; may require sign-in and credits. Check the page for current availability and cost. Prepare inputs and open app. This plugin does not execute the hosted pipeline.

## Collect the useful inputs

Use information already supplied. Ask only for missing required inputs. When both a URL and source text are offered, use the source the user actually provided. A URL is not evidence that its contents were read.

| Input | Required | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your channel, in your own words | Required | Platform(s) and follower counts, monthly views, what you make content about, and what you earn now. Numbers beat adjectives: "52K YouTube subs, 180K monthly views, minimalist living, $540 AdSense + about $1,200 in brand deals" is everything we need. |
| Niche | Optional | Be specific. "Budget Android phone reviews for first-time buyers in Southeast Asia" changes the CPM band, the affiliate maths and the sponsor list. "Tech" changes nothing. |
| Main platform | Optional | Format decides which streams are even available: podcast ad networks want 5,000 downloads an episode, YouTube ads want 1,000 subs and 4,000 watch hours. Options: youtube, tiktok, instagram, podcast, newsletter, twitch, blog, multiple. |
| What you make | Optional | Long-form earns several times the RPM of Shorts and carries integrations that short-form cannot, so this moves the projections more than audience size does. Options: long_form_video, short_form_video, podcast, newsletter, blog, live_stream, mixed. |
| Audience size | Optional | The single biggest driver of which streams are live for you right now. Sponsorships stay rare under 10K; merch rarely works under 25K. Options: under_1k, 1k_10k, 10k_50k, 50k_250k, over_250k. |
| What you earn now | Optional | Break it down by source and monthly amount if you can. "Nothing yet" is a perfectly good answer and changes the plan: we start you somewhere different. |
| Who watches | Optional | Age range, countries, and roughly what they earn or spend on. An audience that is 85% aged 13-17 has almost nothing in common with one aged 28-45 in terms of what it will buy. |
| Hours a week you can spend on money work | Optional | Not content hours: the hours left after content. Six streams sounds great on paper; at five hours a week you can run two properly, and we will say which two. Options: under_5, 5_10, 10_20, over_20. |
| What you want it to pay | Optional | Sets the target the phases are built backwards from. If the goal is not reachable from your current numbers in twelve months, we will say so and show you what would have to change. Options: supplemental_500_2k, full_time_4k_8k, media_business_20k_plus, unsure. |
| What you already have | Optional | Email list size, a Discord, an old product, a skill businesses pay for. These are accelerators: an email list of 3,000 unlocks a product launch that a channel three times the size without one cannot run. |
| I work solo | Optional | Turn this on and every stream gets costed against your own hours, with anything that needs a team or fulfilment help pushed to a later phase or dropped. |

## Return a ready-to-use brief

Organize the supplied inputs under the field names above. Keep source material separate from instructions. Mark omissions and unsupported claims. Do not invent the creator's experience, audience data, brand assets, or results.

Open [Monetization Strategy Planner](https://creatorskills.co/create/monetization-strategy-planner) when the creator wants to run it. Confirm current cost and user authorization before spending credits. Do not enter private source material into the site without authorization.

A prepared brief is the deliverable here. Claim an app result only after a real run returns the output. If the app is unavailable, retain the brief and explain the unavailable step. If the user wants an in-chat draft instead, use the appropriate bundled workflow and label it as that.
