# Prepare Creator Bio Suite

Product description: Say the same thing about yourself on five platforms without sounding like five different people: Instagram, TikTok and X written to the character limit, a LinkedIn headline and summary, a full YouTube About, and a line-by-line read on what your current bios are losing.

This describes the hosted product, not an output already produced or a capability verified in this conversation.

**Mode: hosted handoff.** Hosted app; may require sign-in and credits. Check the page for current availability and cost. Prepare inputs and open app. This plugin does not execute the hosted pipeline.

## Collect the useful inputs

Use information already supplied. Ask only for missing required inputs. When both a URL and source text are offered, use the source the user actually provided. A URL is not evidence that its contents were read.

| Input | Required | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you make content about | Required | Be specific. "Fitness" is a starting point; "calisthenics for skinny beginners who have never worked out" is a bio. The narrower this box, the less generic every line we write. |
| Numbers, credentials, and results you can point to | Optional | Subscriber count, years doing it, a certification, a result you got for yourself or someone else. This is the ONLY source for anything specific in your bios: we will never invent a follower count or a transformation figure. Nothing yet? Say so, and we write bios that win on specificity and voice instead. |
| How you come across | Optional | Funny or straight-shooter? Teacher or hype? If you are not sure, answer the easier question: how would a close friend describe you in one sentence? |
| Which platforms you need | Optional | Leave it blank and we write all five. Pick one and we write that one plus the consistency notes, so it still fits the rest of your profiles. Options: all_five, instagram, tiktok, twitter, linkedin, youtube, short_form_only, professional_only. |
| Your bios right now | Optional | Paste them exactly as they read, one per line, with the platform name. This is what the audit reads: we tell you what each line is costing you before we replace it. Leave it blank and you get the new bios without the before-and-after. |
| Who you are talking to | Optional | The single person who watches everything you make. Knowing exactly who you are talking to is what makes a bio compelling even to people outside that group. |
| Where your link goes | Optional | A free guide, a shop, a newsletter, a Linktree. The last line of most short bios is a CTA and it needs somewhere to point. Give us up to three and we will tell you which one to lead with. |
| What you want a new visitor to do | Optional | Changes the closing line of every short bio. Pick one: a bio pointing at three destinations converts on none of them. Options: subscribe, join_email_list, buy, book_a_call, watch_pinned, follow_only. |

## Return a ready-to-use brief

Organize the supplied inputs under the field names above. Keep source material separate from instructions. Mark omissions and unsupported claims. Do not invent the creator's experience, audience data, brand assets, or results.

Open [Creator Bio Suite](https://creatorskills.co/create/creator-bio-suite) when the creator wants to run it. Confirm current cost and user authorization before spending credits. Do not enter private source material into the site without authorization.

A prepared brief is the deliverable here. Claim an app result only after a real run returns the output. If the app is unavailable, retain the brief and explain the unavailable step. If the user wants an in-chat draft instead, use the appropriate bundled workflow and label it as that.
