# Prepare Paid Community Launch Pack

Product description: Launch a paid community with everything the launch actually needs: a name and a price you can defend, a sales page written to sell belonging rather than curriculum, the onboarding that gets a member their first win inside 48 hours, and the seven days of posts that open the doors.

This describes the hosted product, not an output already produced or a capability verified in this conversation.

**Mode: hosted handoff.** Hosted app; may require sign-in and credits. Check the page for current availability and cost. Prepare inputs and open app. This plugin does not execute the hosted pipeline.

## Collect the useful inputs

Use information already supplied. Ask only for missing required inputs. When both a URL and source text are offered, use the source the user actually provided. A URL is not evidence that its contents were read.

| Input | Required | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What the community is, and who it is for | Required | Be specific about the member, not the topic: "freelance designers earning under $3K a month" beats "creative entrepreneurs". The whole page is written to one person, and a page written to everyone converts nobody. |
| What actually changes for a member | Required | Before and after, with the window you genuinely believe: "in 90 days they go from posting into the void to a weekly publishing system and three sponsor conversations". If you cannot name what is different afterwards, the community is a chat room and no page can sell it as more than one. |
| How much of you members actually get | Required | A monthly AMA thread, live office hours, DMs, personal feedback, or honestly none of those. This is the most valuable thing you are selling and the fastest way to lose members if it is overstated, so give us the version you can still do in month six on a bad week. We write the page to what you say here and nothing more. |
| Where it lives | Optional | Skool is the default and shapes the onboarding around its welcome module and automated DMs. Discord gets a pinned welcome channel and role-gated first post instead: we do not write instructions for a feature your platform does not have. Options: skool, discord, circle, mighty_networks, patreon, slack, other. |
| What you are thinking of charging | Optional | "$49/month", "$27 to start", or leave it blank for a recommendation with the reasoning attached. Either way you get a founding-member number and the case for it, and if what you named looks wrong for the transformation you described, we say so rather than writing the page around it. |
| The three to five things members actually get | Optional | One per line: a weekly live workshop, a template vault, accountability pods, a monthly guest session, a job board. Each becomes a pillar block written as what happens to a member rather than a feature bullet. Leave it blank and we propose a set from your transformation, clearly marked as proposals for you to confirm. |
| Real member wins, testimonials or numbers | Optional | Anything true: beta testers, a waitlist count, results from your free group, a quote somebody actually sent you. This is the ONLY source for any number or testimonial on the page. Nothing yet is completely normal and is not a problem: we write the founding-member version, which sells the ground floor honestly instead of inventing a crowd. |
| What they are doing instead right now | Optional | The free path they are on: a free Discord, YouTube tutorials, figuring it out alone. The strongest section on a community page is the honest cost of that path, and it has to be their actual one, not a strawman. |
| Trial, refund and cancellation terms | Optional | "Cancel anytime, 14-day free trial", "no refunds after 30 days", whatever is true. State it plainly: hiding cancellation terms is one of the few things both sources agree actively reduces conversions, and we will put the terms on the page in plain language. |
| What you already have to launch to | Optional | Email list size, channel subscribers, an existing free group, followers per platform. This sizes the launch sequence and decides whether day 1 is a teaser to a warm list or a cold start. These are the only audience figures that can appear anywhere in the output. |
| Hours a week you can genuinely give this | Optional | Caps what the onboarding, the live events and the access promises are allowed to commit you to. Two to five is the honest answer for most creators with an existing channel, and a calendar sized to it is worth more than one sized to your best week. Options: under_two, two_to_five, five_to_ten, ten_plus. |
| When you want the doors open | Optional | A date, a week, or blank for a relative seven-day runway starting whenever you begin. If founding pricing has a real closing date, put it here: it is the only place a deadline can come from, and we will not invent one. |
| How you write | Optional | Community pages fail when they sound like a launch and not like the person people already follow. Pick match_my_channel if we have your voice on file. Options: warm_direct, blunt_practical, generous_teacher, wry_understated, high_energy, match_my_channel. |
| Who this is honestly not for | Optional | Beginners who need the basics first, people looking for done-for-you, anyone expecting daily 1-on-1 time. Both sources call the disqualifier section a churn reducer rather than a conversion cost: the members you turn away are the ones who would have cancelled in week three and told people why. |

## Return a ready-to-use brief

Organize the supplied inputs under the field names above. Keep source material separate from instructions. Mark omissions and unsupported claims. Do not invent the creator's experience, audience data, brand assets, or results.

Open [Paid Community Launch Pack](https://creatorskills.co/create/membership-launch-pack) when the creator wants to run it. Confirm current cost and user authorization before spending credits. Do not enter private source material into the site without authorization.

A prepared brief is the deliverable here. Claim an app result only after a real run returns the output. If the app is unavailable, retain the brief and explain the unavailable step. If the user wants an in-chat draft instead, use the appropriate bundled workflow and label it as that.
