# Prepare Newsletter Conversion Engine

Product description: Turn something you already published into a newsletter issue you can send today, with five subject-line options, a five-email welcome sequence that runs on autopilot, a lead magnet with its landing page copy, and a four-issue plan.

This describes the hosted product, not an output already produced or a capability verified in this conversation.

**Mode: hosted handoff.** Hosted app; may require sign-in and credits. Check the page for current availability and cost. Prepare inputs and open app. This plugin does not execute the hosted pipeline.

## Collect the useful inputs

Use information already supplied. Ask only for missing required inputs. When both a URL and source text are offered, use the source the user actually provided. A URL is not evidence that its contents were read.

| Input | Required | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you make content about | Required | "Tech reviews and productivity for people who work from a laptop" beats "tech". The narrower you are, the sharper every subject line gets. |
| The content this issue comes from | Required | Paste a video transcript, a blog post, a podcast summary, a social post that did well, or just the topic you want to cover this week. This is where every fact, number and story in your issue comes from. We never invent one, so a thin paste gets a thinner email. |
| What you sell, or want to sell | Optional | Course, coaching, digital product, affiliate links, sponsorships, with the price if you have one. Welcome emails 4 and 5 build toward this. "Nothing yet" is a real answer; turn on the toggle below and we'll write value emails instead. |
| How big your list is now | Optional | Zero is completely fine and changes the advice rather than limiting it: a list of zero gets a lead magnet plan, a list of ten thousand gets a segmentation and re-engagement plan. Options: zero, under_500, 500_to_2k, 2k_to_10k, over_10k. |
| Your email platform | Optional | Pick "undecided" and we'll recommend one based on your list size and what you need it to do, instead of assuming. Options: convertkit, beehiiv, substack, mailchimp, ghost, other, undecided. |
| How often you want to send | Optional | Weekly is the default for most creators and the plan assumes it if you leave this blank. Missing a week beats sending something empty to stay on schedule. Options: weekly, twice_weekly, every_two_weeks, monthly, undecided. |
| Format for this issue | Optional | Leave it on "pick for me" and we'll choose the format that fits what you pasted: a transcript becomes a video-to-newsletter, a strong opinion becomes a deep dive. Options: video_to_newsletter, curated_roundup, deep_dive, behind_the_scenes, promotion_launch, pick_for_me. |
| Something you wrote that sounds like you | Optional | A past newsletter, a caption, a long DM. This is the single biggest difference between "generic email" and "sounds exactly like me". Two paragraphs is enough. |
| Your three best pieces of content | Optional | Titles and links to your most popular or most useful work. Welcome email 3 sends these: "if you only watch three things I've made, make it these", and we only ever link to what you list here. |
| Why you started doing this | Optional | The real version, not the About page version. The moment, the frustration, the thing that made you start. Welcome email 2 is built from this: leave it blank and we'll write the email's shape and tell you exactly which sentence to fill in yourself, rather than making a story up for you. |
| Subject lines you've sent recently | Optional | One per line. We use them two ways: to avoid repeating you, and to tell you what's wrong with them if your open rate is the reason you're here. |
| Your current open and click rates | Optional | Straight from your dashboard, e.g. "18% open, 1.2% click, 2,000 subscribers, weekly". Every number in your diagnosis comes from this box. Leave it blank and we'll tell you which screen to pull it from instead of guessing at your numbers. |
| How you sign off | Optional | The name that goes at the bottom of every email. Emails from a person outperform emails from a brand, so "Sam" beats "The Sam Newsletter Team". |
| I don't have anything to sell yet | Optional | Turn this on and welcome emails 4 and 5 become a second value email and a "what should I make next?" question that gets replies, which is the fastest way to find out what to build. |

## Return a ready-to-use brief

Organize the supplied inputs under the field names above. Keep source material separate from instructions. Mark omissions and unsupported claims. Do not invent the creator's experience, audience data, brand assets, or results.

Open [Newsletter Conversion Engine](https://creatorskills.co/create/newsletter-conversion-engine) when the creator wants to run it. Confirm current cost and user authorization before spending credits. Do not enter private source material into the site without authorization.

A prepared brief is the deliverable here. Claim an app result only after a real run returns the output. If the app is unavailable, retain the brief and explain the unavailable step. If the user wants an in-chat draft instead, use the appropriate bundled workflow and label it as that.
