# Prepare Content Idea Engine

Product description: Ten specific, filmable ideas for your niche: each with a working title, the angle that makes it worth watching, and why it will land: plus the one to make first and where the rest sit against your evergreen pillars over the next month.

This describes the hosted product, not an output already produced or a capability verified in this conversation.

**Mode: hosted handoff.** Hosted app; may require sign-in and credits. Check the page for current availability and cost. Prepare inputs and open app. This plugin does not execute the hosted pipeline.

## Collect the useful inputs

Use information already supplied. Ask only for missing required inputs. When both a URL and source text are offered, use the source the user actually provided. A URL is not evidence that its contents were read.

| Input | Required | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your niche | Required | The more specific, the sharper the ideas. "Fitness" works. "Calisthenics for skinny guys aged 18-25 building muscle without a gym" produces ideas that could only be yours. |
| Where you publish | Optional | Format and length norms differ enough that a good TikTok idea is often a bad long-form one. Leave it blank for a platform-neutral mix. Options: youtube, youtube_shorts, tiktok, instagram, podcast, newsletter, multiple. |
| Audience size | Optional | This changes strategy, not just tone. A small channel needs searchable, evergreen-adjacent ideas; a large one can carry a pure-timing play. It also stops us suggesting "I asked 1,000 subscribers…" to someone with 300. Options: under_1k, 1k_10k, 10k_50k, 50k_250k, over_250k. |
| What you have actually noticed | Optional | Optional, and read literally. Paste what you have seen yourself: a Google Trends line that is climbing, a hashtag count you looked up, three competitors who all posted the same thing this week, a question your comments keep asking. We score what you paste and attribute it back to you. We do not have live access to Google, YouTube or TikTok, so we will never produce a trend figure you did not give us, and with this box empty you get evergreen ideas and we say so. |
| Your evergreen pillars | Optional | One per line. Used for the 60/30/10 placement, and to avoid handing you an idea you have already covered three times. |
| What has worked best for you | Optional | Your best-performing pieces, with numbers if you have them. "My 30-day challenge video did 4x my average" is the single most useful thing you can tell us: it names the format to press on. |
| Weight the list towards | Optional | If your last two months have been all tutorials, ask for story and behind-the-scenes and the mix shifts. Options: balanced, evergreen_tutorials, personal_story, deep_dives, hot_takes, behind_the_scenes, community, timely_angles. |
| How often you publish | Optional | This decides the calendar length and whether a fast-turn angle is realistic for you at all. A five-day window is useless advice if you need two weeks per piece. Options: monthly, twice_monthly, weekly, twice_weekly, three_plus_weekly. |
| How long one piece takes you start to finish | Optional | We will not put a time-sensitive angle in front of you if the window closes before you could publish it. Options: same_day, two_to_three_days, about_a_week, two_weeks_or_more. |

## Return a ready-to-use brief

Organize the supplied inputs under the field names above. Keep source material separate from instructions. Mark omissions and unsupported claims. Do not invent the creator's experience, audience data, brand assets, or results.

Open [Content Idea Engine](https://creatorskills.co/create/content-idea-engine) when the creator wants to run it. Confirm current cost and user authorization before spending credits. Do not enter private source material into the site without authorization.

A prepared brief is the deliverable here. Claim an app result only after a real run returns the output. If the app is unavailable, retain the brief and explain the unavailable step. If the user wants an in-chat draft instead, use the appropriate bundled workflow and label it as that.
