# Prepare Digital Product Ladder

Product description: Turn an audience into four products people actually climb: a free thing that proves the method, a cheap thing that makes them a customer, a flagship that does the real work, and a premium tier, with the one you should build first blueprinted in full and the emails that move people up written out.

This describes the hosted product, not an output already produced or a capability verified in this conversation.

**Mode: hosted handoff.** Hosted app; may require sign-in and credits. Check the page for current availability and cost. Prepare inputs and open app. This plugin does not execute the hosted pipeline.

## Collect the useful inputs

Use information already supplied. Ask only for missing required inputs. When both a URL and source text are offered, use the source the user actually provided. A URL is not evidence that its contents were read.

| Input | Required | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you make, and who for | Required | Format, topic and audience in a paragraph: "a YouTube channel about freelance motion design, mostly people one to three years in, about 14K subs and a 1,900-person list". Numbers help: audience size changes which tiers are worth building at all. |
| The one problem your audience keeps asking you to solve | Required | The question in your comments and DMs every week, in their words. This becomes the spine of all four tiers: every rung solves a slice of this one problem, which is what makes the ladder something people climb rather than four unrelated things you happen to sell. |
| What you already sell or give away | Optional | One per line with the price, including free things: "free: 12-page lighting PDF; $47: preset pack, maybe 4 sales a month". Fill this in and you get an audit of the ladder you already have instead of a plan from zero, which is almost always the more useful answer. Sponsorships do not count; this is about products you own. |
| What you can actually produce | Optional | Writing, templates, spreadsheets, video, live coaching, community moderation, and what you cannot or will not do. A ladder built on formats you dread is a ladder with three unbuilt rungs, so we scope every tier to what you named here. |
| Content or IP you could repurpose | Optional | Scripts, docs, frameworks, processes, a popular video, a checklist you already use privately. Roughly how many and what they cover. The fastest tier to ship is almost always the one made of something you already wrote. |
| Audience size | Optional | Decides which rungs are live now and which are a later phase. A premium tier needs enough people at the top of the ladder to fill it; under 1,000 the honest answer is usually the first two rungs and nothing else yet. Options: under_1k, 1k_10k, 10k_50k, 50k_250k, over_250k. |
| How fast you need something shipping | Optional | Changes which rung we blueprint first. "This week" gets you the tier you can build from existing material; "this quarter" lets us prioritise the tier that earns most rather than the one that is quickest. Options: this_week, this_month, this_quarter, no_rush. |
| The most your audience has ever paid you | Optional | "$27 for a preset pack", "nothing yet", "$300 for a workshop". This anchors the whole ladder honestly: a premium tier at ten times anything they have ever bought from you is a tier that sells to nobody, and we would rather say that than price a fantasy. |
| Do you already have a community | Optional | Turn this on if you already run a Discord, a Circle or an active comment section. Premium tiers built on an existing community ship in weeks; premium tiers that have to build one from scratch take months, and we sequence them differently. |
| How you write | Optional | The bridge emails and the sales copy get sent from your address to people who already know your voice. Pick match_my_channel if we have your voice on file. Options: warm_direct, blunt_practical, generous_teacher, wry_understated, high_energy, match_my_channel. |

## Return a ready-to-use brief

Organize the supplied inputs under the field names above. Keep source material separate from instructions. Mark omissions and unsupported claims. Do not invent the creator's experience, audience data, brand assets, or results.

Open [Digital Product Ladder](https://creatorskills.co/create/digital-product-ladder) when the creator wants to run it. Confirm current cost and user authorization before spending credits. Do not enter private source material into the site without authorization.

A prepared brief is the deliverable here. Claim an app result only after a real run returns the output. If the app is unavailable, retain the brief and explain the unavailable step. If the user wants an in-chat draft instead, use the appropriate bundled workflow and label it as that.
