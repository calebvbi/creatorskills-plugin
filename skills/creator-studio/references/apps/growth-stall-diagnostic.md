# Prepare Growth Stall Diagnostic

Product description: Your channel stopped growing and you have been guessing at why. Paste 90 days of numbers and get one named verdict with the evidence behind it, a week-by-week 30-day recovery protocol, the two or three numbers that will tell you it is working, and the overreactions that would make it worse.

This describes the hosted product, not an output already produced or a capability verified in this conversation.

**Mode: hosted handoff.** Hosted app; may require sign-in and credits. Check the page for current availability and cost. Prepare inputs and open app. This plugin does not execute the hosted pipeline.

## Collect the useful inputs

Use information already supplied. Ask only for missing required inputs. When both a URL and source text are offered, use the source the user actually provided. A URL is not evidence that its contents were read.

| Input | Required | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform | Required | The benchmarks and even the metric names change completely between these four, so this one is required. Pick the platform where growth stalled. Options: youtube, tiktok, podcast, newsletter. |
| Your channel | Required | Subscriber or follower count, niche, how long you have been active, and who the audience is. Be specific: "home espresso for people who bought a machine and got bad shots, 18K subs, 14 months" produces a different diagnosis from "coffee". |
| Last 90 days | Required | Net new subscribers or followers per month, impressions or reach, CTR if you have it, and average view duration or completion rate. Estimates are fine. If a number does not exist on your platform, skip it: we will say what we could not check rather than guess. |
| Your last 10 videos, episodes or issues | Optional | Title plus view or download count, one per line. This is what separates a real diagnosis from generic advice: the titles alone reveal whether a new viewer could tell what your channel is about. |
| Exported analytics file | Optional | Optional: upload the YouTube Studio, TikTok, podcast host or email platform CSV export instead of pasting it. |
| What changed when growth slowed | Optional | Posting frequency, niche, video length, thumbnail style, a hiatus, a new intro: anything that lines up with the slowdown. The source of this audit calls the timing of the stall relative to a change the single most important input, because it usually names the leak on its own. |
| How often you publish, and whether you have been consistent | Optional | "Weekly, but I missed three weeks in April" is far more useful than "weekly". Missed uploads are the first thing the algorithm-signal check looks at. |
| How the stall arrived | Optional | A gradual plateau and a cliff after a specific change get different diagnoses. Leave it blank and we will work it out from your numbers. Options: gradual_no_obvious_cause, coincided_with_something, not_sure. |
| What you most want answered | Optional | "Did switching thumbnail styles do this?" We lead the verdict with your question instead of making you go looking for it. |
