# Prepare Creator Collab Engine

Product description: Work out which creators are actually worth pitching, write the message they will answer instead of the one they delete, and land with a plan both sides can execute: including who publishes when and how the audience gets handed over.

This describes the hosted product, not an output already produced or a capability verified in this conversation.

**Mode: hosted handoff.** Hosted app; may require sign-in and credits. Check the page for current availability and cost. Prepare inputs and open app. This plugin does not execute the hosted pipeline.

## Collect the useful inputs

Use information already supplied. Ask only for missing required inputs. When both a URL and source text are offered, use the source the user actually provided. A URL is not evidence that its contents were read.

| Input | Required | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your platform and what you actually make | Required | Be specific: "fitness" is too broad, "home workouts for busy parents" is useful. The overlap judgement is the whole engine here, and it cannot be made against a category label. |
| Your size, and what a video actually does | Required | Subscriber or follower count AND average views per piece. Average views matter more than subscriber count, and both are needed: the rule about not pitching someone more than three times your size is arithmetic on these two numbers. |
| The creator you want to pitch, if you have one | Optional | Their handle, their niche, and their size. Leave it blank and you get a partner profile instead: the type of channel, the size band and the overlap shape worth targeting: rather than a list of names. We do not invent creators, because a name we made up is one search away from being obviously made up. |
| What you have actually watched of theirs | Optional | A video title, a running series, a specific moment that stuck with you. This is the single line that decides whether the message gets a reply, and it is the one line we will not write for you: leave it blank and the draft marks the spot and tells you exactly what to go and find. We will never put a video they never made into your mouth. |
| The idea, if you have one | Optional | One sentence. "We each cook the other's signature recipe and film the reaction" is a pitch; "we should collab" is not. With nothing here we propose formats from the overlap and you pick one. |
| How you would reach them | Optional | All by default, each written natively rather than one message reformatted: a DM under a hundred words and a cold email are different artifacts, and the video pitch is a spoken script. Options: all, instagram_or_tiktok_dm, email, twitter_dm, youtube_comment_then_dm, video_pitch. |
| Anyone you both know | Optional | A shared friend, an event you both attended, a Discord you are both in, a previous exchange. A warm intro converts far better than a cold one, which is exactly why we never claim one you did not tell us about. |
| Collabs you have done before | Optional | What you have made with other creators, what worked, and anything that went badly. A bad past collab changes the plan: the safeguards get written into the agreement rather than learned twice. |
| What you would actually enjoy making | Optional | The format changes the effort, the growth ceiling and the money split all at once. Any by default and we recommend from the size gap and the overlap. Options: any, guest_appearance, joint_video, challenge_or_competition, series_swap, live_stream, cross_promotion, co_created_product. |
| What you can and cannot do | Optional | Remote only, travel budget, filming windows, publishing deadlines, gear. These go straight into the plan's timeline and division of work, and a plan that ignores them is a plan neither of you can run. |
| What you want out of it | Optional | Changes which formats get recommended and how the audience handoff is designed, not who is worth pitching. Options: reach_a_new_audience, subscribers, content_variety, build_the_relationship, revenue. |
| Anything you will not put your name to | Optional | Products, positions, formats or partners you will not be associated with. A collab ties two reputations together, so these are honoured absolutely and flagged where a proposed partner or format collides with one. |

## Return a ready-to-use brief

Organize the supplied inputs under the field names above. Keep source material separate from instructions. Mark omissions and unsupported claims. Do not invent the creator's experience, audience data, brand assets, or results.

Open [Creator Collab Engine](https://creatorskills.co/create/creator-collab-engine) when the creator wants to run it. Confirm current cost and user authorization before spending credits. Do not enter private source material into the site without authorization.

A prepared brief is the deliverable here. Claim an app result only after a real run returns the output. If the app is unavailable, retain the brief and explain the unavailable step. If the user wants an in-chat draft instead, use the appropriate bundled workflow and label it as that.
