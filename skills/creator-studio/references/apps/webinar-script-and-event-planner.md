# Prepare Webinar Script and Event Planner

Product description: Get the whole webinar written out: every word of the sixty minutes, the eight objections answered before anyone types them, the tech runbook and contingency lines for when something breaks live, and the five emails that close the people who watched and did not buy.

This describes the hosted product, not an output already produced or a capability verified in this conversation.

**Mode: hosted handoff.** Hosted app; may require sign-in and credits. Check the page for current availability and cost. Prepare inputs and open app. This plugin does not execute the hosted pipeline.

## Collect the useful inputs

Use information already supplied. Ask only for missing required inputs. When both a URL and source text are offered, use the source the user actually provided. A URL is not evidence that its contents were read.

| Input | Required | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you are teaching on the webinar | Required | The promise, in the words an attendee would use. "How to get your first five coaching clients without an audience" is a webinar; "coaching business growth" is a category. The narrower this is, the less the teaching block wanders. |
| What you are selling, and to whom | Required | Name it, say what it includes, and say who it is for in one sentence. The bridge from teaching to offer only works when the offer is the obvious next step from what you just taught: if it is not, we will say so rather than writing a transition that papers over it. |
| Price | Optional | The number and any payment plan. Decides how much objection handling the offer segment needs: a $97 product and a $3,000 cohort do not get the same close. Leave it blank and we mark every price slot so you can fill them in one pass. |
| How long the webinar runs | Optional | Minutes. Sets the word count and rebalances all six segments: a 45-minute version cuts teaching frameworks, it does not just talk faster. Defaults to 60. Options: 45, 60, 90. |
| Where you are running it | Optional | Changes the tech checklist and which engagement markers are even possible: a platform without native polls gets chat prompts instead of [POLL] cues rather than cues you cannot run. Options: zoom, livestorm, webinarjam, youtube_live, streamyard, other. |
| Who is registering | Optional | What they already know, what they have already tried, and what they are afraid of. This is where the objection bank gets its specificity: generic objections get generic answers, and generic answers do not close anyone. |
| Have you taught this before | Optional | A first-timer gets more scaffolding in the script and more contingency lines. Someone who has taught it live gets tighter copy and more room for their own examples. Options: first_time, taught_informally, taught_this_exact_material. |
| Results, case studies and stories you can actually cite | Optional | Client outcomes, your own numbers, screenshots you can show, credentials. Every number and every story in the script comes from here: we never invent one. Leave it blank and the authority and offer segments arrive as shapes with a marked line telling you exactly what to drop in. |
| Attendees and sales you are aiming for | Optional | Used to size the contingency scripts and the follow-up sequence, never to predict a result. We will not write a projection you would have to defend. |
| Your refund or guarantee policy | Optional | "14-day refund, no questions", "30 days if you have done the first two modules", or "all sales are final": whichever is true. It is spoken on the pitch and it answers the refund objection, so it has to be your words. Leave it blank and the script says the offer is covered by your stated terms and the blueprint asks you to decide, rather than a guarantee we made up that a buyer could claim against. |

## Return a ready-to-use brief

Organize the supplied inputs under the field names above. Keep source material separate from instructions. Mark omissions and unsupported claims. Do not invent the creator's experience, audience data, brand assets, or results.

Open [Webinar Script and Event Planner](https://creatorskills.co/create/webinar-script-and-event-planner) when the creator wants to run it. Confirm current cost and user authorization before spending credits. Do not enter private source material into the site without authorization.

A prepared brief is the deliverable here. Claim an app result only after a real run returns the output. If the app is unavailable, retain the brief and explain the unavailable step. If the user wants an in-chat draft instead, use the appropriate bundled workflow and label it as that.
