# Prepare Audience Persona Builder

Product description: Turn what you already half-know about your viewers into one named, specific person you can plan against: their day, what frustrates them, what makes them click, what they buy, and a marked list of everything you are still guessing about.

This describes the hosted product, not an output already produced or a capability verified in this conversation.

**Mode: hosted handoff.** Hosted app; may require sign-in and credits. Check the page for current availability and cost. Prepare inputs and open app. This plugin does not execute the hosted pipeline.

## Collect the useful inputs

Use information already supplied. Ask only for missing required inputs. When both a URL and source text are offered, use the source the user actually provided. A URL is not evidence that its contents were read.

| Input | Required | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What your content is about | Required | The elevator pitch: what you cover and the angle you bring to it. Include your main platform and roughly how big your audience is if you know it. |
| What you actually see about who watches | Required | Everything you know but never wrote down. Who shows up in the comments, what they say, what they ask for, what your analytics show, who DMs you. Paste real comments if you have them. This box is the entire evidence base: anything not in here or the boxes below gets marked as a gap rather than guessed at. |
| Primary platform | Optional | Changes how the consumption section is written: a YouTube audience in a research session behaves nothing like a TikTok audience in a scroll. Options: youtube, tiktok, instagram, podcast, newsletter, twitch, linkedin, blog. |
| Demographics from your analytics | Optional | Age split, gender split, top countries, watch time by segment. YouTube Studio → Audience, Instagram Insights → Audience, TikTok Analytics → Followers. Leave it blank and we build from behaviour signals instead, and say so, rather than inventing percentages. |
| What of yours gets the most engagement | Optional | Titles or topics, plus what people actually say in the comments on them. This is the single most useful thing you can give us: what an audience rewards tells you more about them than what they say they want. |
| What of yours they ignore | Optional | The topics and formats that flop. A persona built only on the hits is a persona missing half its shape. |
| What frustrates them | Optional | The complaints, struggles and stuck points that come up in comments, DMs and community posts. In their words if you can: the phrasing is what makes the persona usable when you sit down to write a title. |
| Who else is in their feed | Optional | Other creators, brands, subreddits, newsletters. It is the fastest way to place your audience in a landscape you can actually reason about. |
| What they spend money on in your niche | Optional | Courses, gear, subscriptions, tools: what they buy, roughly what they pay, and how they decide. Affiliate click-through data counts. Leave it blank and the purchasing section says "unknown" and tells you how to find out, which is more useful than a confident guess. |
| What they keep asking you for | Optional | The thing that comes up again and again that you have not made yet. It usually becomes the first line of the content-opportunities section. |
| How many personas | Optional | Most channels need one. Ask for two or three only if you genuinely serve distinct groups: a YouTube audience unlike your TikTok one, or tutorial viewers unlike entertainment viewers. More than three and the document stops getting used. Options: 1, 2, 3. |
| My audience is small or brand new | Optional | Turn this on under about a thousand followers and we frame the persona as who you are building for rather than who is already here: clearly labelled, so you never mistake an aspiration for a measurement. |

## Return a ready-to-use brief

Organize the supplied inputs under the field names above. Keep source material separate from instructions. Mark omissions and unsupported claims. Do not invent the creator's experience, audience data, brand assets, or results.

Open [Audience Persona Builder](https://creatorskills.co/create/audience-persona-builder) when the creator wants to run it. Confirm current cost and user authorization before spending credits. Do not enter private source material into the site without authorization.

A prepared brief is the deliverable here. Claim an app result only after a real run returns the output. If the app is unavailable, retain the brief and explain the unavailable step. If the user wants an in-chat draft instead, use the appropriate bundled workflow and label it as that.
