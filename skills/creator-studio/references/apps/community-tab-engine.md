# Prepare YouTube Community Tab Engine

Product description: Fill your Community tab for a whole quarter in one sitting: every post written out and ready to paste, a mix that never repeats itself two days running, and the warm-up and follow-up posts that bracket each upload.

This describes the hosted product, not an output already produced or a capability verified in this conversation.

**Mode: hosted handoff.** Hosted app; may require sign-in and credits. Check the page for current availability and cost. Prepare inputs and open app. This plugin does not execute the hosted pipeline.

## Collect the useful inputs

Use information already supplied. Ask only for missing required inputs. When both a URL and source text are offered, use the source the user actually provided. A URL is not evidence that its contents were read.

| Input | Required | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your channel, in a couple of sentences | Required | What the channel is about, who watches it, and your usual format: tutorials, vlogs, reviews, commentary. "Budget home-studio gear reviews for people recording their first podcast" beats "a tech channel": every poll has to be specific enough that it could not be pasted onto another channel in the same niche. |
| What is actually coming up | Required | Videos in production, launches, collabs or real decisions you are weighing in the next few weeks: one per line, with the rough week if you know it. Teasers and audience-input posts are built from these and from nothing else. "Not much, just the usual weekly upload" is a perfectly good answer and we will build the calendar without teasers rather than inventing a video. |
| How far ahead to plan | Optional | Ninety days is the default and is the point of doing this in one sitting. Thirty is the honest answer if your upload schedule is likely to change. Options: thirty_days, sixty_days, ninety_days. |
| Posts a week you will actually keep up | Optional | Three is the default and the number most channels hold for a full quarter. Pick the number you will still be hitting in week nine, not the one you want to be hitting in week one: a calendar you abandon is worse than a shorter one you finish. Options: two, three, four, five. |
| When you upload | Optional | "Thursdays", "Tuesday and Saturday", "whenever it's ready". This is what places the warm-up 48 hours before and the follow-up 24 hours after. Leave it blank and those two posts come as an unattached pair you can slot in yourself. |
| Roughly how many subscribers | Optional | Changes the mix rather than the copy: under 10K leans on questions and personal updates because relationships are the priority, over 100K leans on polls and challenges. This is also the only subscriber figure that can appear anywhere in the output; we never invent one. |
| Real milestones, wins or numbers you can reference | Optional | One per line: "hit 10K last Tuesday", "my first video just passed 100K", "took two weeks off". Throwback and milestone posts are built only from these. Leave it blank and those post types are dropped from the mix rather than filled with a number we made up. |
| How you sound | Optional | Community posts are the most casual thing you write: closer to a text than a caption. Pick match_my_channel if you described your voice above. Options: casual_friend, dry_deadpan, high_energy, educational_warm, irreverent_funny, match_my_channel. |
| Topics you cover | Optional | Three to five, one per line. These are what the polls, hot takes and tips are built from, so a specific list gets you posts nobody else in your niche could run. |
| How much you want to poke the bear | Optional | Occasional is the default and caps hot takes at one a fortnight. Choosing none removes the type entirely: a debate post you regret is a worse outcome than a quieter tab. Options: none, occasional, regularly. |
| Community posts you already ran | Optional | Paste or paraphrase the last handful. Used to keep this calendar off polls you already asked and angles you already used, which is the thing that makes a Community tab feel automated. |
| Anything off limits | Optional | Topics, running jokes that have worn out, a competitor you would rather not name, anything personal you keep off the channel. One per line. |

## Return a ready-to-use brief

Organize the supplied inputs under the field names above. Keep source material separate from instructions. Mark omissions and unsupported claims. Do not invent the creator's experience, audience data, brand assets, or results.

Open [YouTube Community Tab Engine](https://creatorskills.co/create/community-tab-engine) when the creator wants to run it. Confirm current cost and user authorization before spending credits. Do not enter private source material into the site without authorization.

A prepared brief is the deliverable here. Claim an app result only after a real run returns the output. If the app is unavailable, retain the brief and explain the unavailable step. If the user wants an in-chat draft instead, use the appropriate bundled workflow and label it as that.
