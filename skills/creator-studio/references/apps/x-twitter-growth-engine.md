# Prepare Twitter/X Growth Engine

Product description: Build the X account you keep meaning to build: a bio and pinned thread rewritten to stop losing the people who click through, four pillars with a week's distribution, eighteen posts already written in your niche, a fifteen-minute reply routine, and the funnel that moves followers onto a list you own.

This describes the hosted product, not an output already produced or a capability verified in this conversation.

**Mode: hosted handoff.** Hosted app; may require sign-in and credits. Check the page for current availability and cost. Prepare inputs and open app. This plugin does not execute the hosted pipeline.

## Collect the useful inputs

Use information already supplied. Ask only for missing required inputs. When both a URL and source text are offered, use the source the user actually provided. A URL is not evidence that its contents were read.

| Input | Required | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you are known for, and who you want reading | Required | The more specific the better: "I help freelance designers price projects, my audience is people two years in who still charge hourly" gives four real pillars. "Business" gives four fake ones. |
| Your bio right now | Optional | Paste it exactly as it stands, including the link. We rewrite against what is there rather than guessing, and if you leave this blank we write three from scratch and say so. |
| Your handle and display name | Optional | Used for the display-name keyword call. We cannot read your account: nothing here is checked against the live profile, so paste anything you want us to work from. |
| Roughly where you are | Optional | Changes which module does the work. Under 500 the profile and the pillars matter most; between 1K and 5K the reply routine is usually the unlock; past 5K the funnel is. Options: under_500, 500_2k, 2k_10k, 10k_50k, 50k_plus. |
| What you want X to do for you | Optional | One goal. It decides the pinned tweet strategy, the CTA on every post format, and whether the funnel section is the main event or a footnote. Options: followers, newsletter_signups, product_sales, authority, community. |
| What you would send people to | Optional | Your newsletter, your product, your channel, with what it actually gives someone. This is the only source for the funnel CTAs; leave it blank and we write the lead magnet concept but leave the link and the promise for you. |
| Minutes a day you can spend | Optional | The reply routine is built to fit this exactly. Fifteen minutes gets three to five replies against a shorter target list, not a shortened version of the sixty-minute plan. Options: 15, 30, 60. |
| Numbers or credentials you can actually claim | Optional | Subscriber counts, revenue, years doing it, a result you got someone. One per line. Everything specific in your bio and posts comes from here: we will not invent a number to make a line land. |
| What you have already made | Optional | Video titles, newsletter subjects, post topics: anything. The pillars get mined from this rather than guessed, and the eighteen example posts sound like you instead of like the niche. |

## Return a ready-to-use brief

Organize the supplied inputs under the field names above. Keep source material separate from instructions. Mark omissions and unsupported claims. Do not invent the creator's experience, audience data, brand assets, or results.

Open [Twitter/X Growth Engine](https://creatorskills.co/create/x-twitter-growth-engine) when the creator wants to run it. Confirm current cost and user authorization before spending credits. Do not enter private source material into the site without authorization.

A prepared brief is the deliverable here. Claim an app result only after a real run returns the output. If the app is unavailable, retain the brief and explain the unavailable step. If the user wants an in-chat draft instead, use the appropriate bundled workflow and label it as that.
