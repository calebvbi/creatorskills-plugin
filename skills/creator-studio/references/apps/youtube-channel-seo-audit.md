# Prepare YouTube Channel SEO & Competitor Gap Audit

Product description: Paste your channel's numbers and your competitors' top videos and get back the audit: what your packaging actually does, where your search traffic leaks, which topics your niche proves demand for that you have never made, and next month's four videos with their target keywords.

This describes the hosted product, not an output already produced or a capability verified in this conversation.

**Mode: hosted handoff.** Hosted app; may require sign-in and credits. Check the page for current availability and cost. Prepare inputs and open app. This plugin does not execute the hosted pipeline.

## Collect the useful inputs

Use information already supplied. Ask only for missing required inputs. When both a URL and source text are offered, use the source the user actually provided. A URL is not evidence that its contents were read.

| Input | Required | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your channel | Required | Niche, subscriber count, typical view range, how often you upload, and what format you make. Be specific: "budget audio gear reviews for home studio creators, 8K subs, 1 video a week, usually 12-minute talking-head reviews" produces a different audit from "tech". |
| Your own video numbers | Optional | Paste your last 10-15 videos however you have them: titles with CTR, average view duration, and traffic sources, straight out of YouTube Studio. This is what turns generic advice into an audit. Without it we read packaging from your titles alone and say so. |
| Exported analytics file | Optional | Optional: upload the YouTube Studio CSV export instead of pasting it. |
| Competitor channels and their top videos | Optional | Paste exact titles, not descriptions of them: patterns only emerge from literal text. Top 20 by views per channel is ideal, with subscriber counts and view counts where you have them. Stay inside about 2x your own size; studying a 10M channel from 5K subs distorts every conclusion. |
| What you are optimising for | Optional | A channel chasing sponsorship revenue gets a different plan from one building an email list. Defaults to a mixed read if omitted. Options: subscribers, views, revenue, break_into_a_topic, mixed. |
| What brought you here | Optional | "Views stuck at 200 for three months" or "CTR fell off a cliff in June". We lead the audit with your question instead of making you find it. |
| Keywords or topics you already target | Optional | One per line. Used to spot cannibalisation: two of your own videos competing for the same query, and to avoid recommending a topic you already own. |
| How many videos you can realistically publish a month | Optional | The plan is sized to what you can actually ship. A four-video plan handed to someone who publishes monthly is a plan they abandon in week two. Options: one, two, three_or_four, weekly_plus. |

## Return a ready-to-use brief

Organize the supplied inputs under the field names above. Keep source material separate from instructions. Mark omissions and unsupported claims. Do not invent the creator's experience, audience data, brand assets, or results.

Open [YouTube Channel SEO & Competitor Gap Audit](https://creatorskills.co/create/youtube-channel-seo-audit) when the creator wants to run it. Confirm current cost and user authorization before spending credits. Do not enter private source material into the site without authorization.

A prepared brief is the deliverable here. Claim an app result only after a real run returns the output. If the app is unavailable, retain the brief and explain the unavailable step. If the user wants an in-chat draft instead, use the appropriate bundled workflow and label it as that.
