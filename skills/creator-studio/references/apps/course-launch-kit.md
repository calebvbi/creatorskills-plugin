# Prepare Course Launch Kit

Product description: Everything a course launch actually needs, written out: an honest readiness verdict, a price with the reasoning attached, the full sales page, every launch email day by day, and the debrief that makes the next cohort better.

This describes the hosted product, not an output already produced or a capability verified in this conversation.

**Mode: hosted handoff.** Hosted app; may require sign-in and credits. Check the page for current availability and cost. Prepare inputs and open app. This plugin does not execute the hosted pipeline.

## Collect the useful inputs

Use information already supplied. Ask only for missing required inputs. When both a URL and source text are offered, use the source the user actually provided. A URL is not evidence that its contents were read.

| Input | Required | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The course, and who it is for | Required | Title, topic, and the student in one paragraph. Be specific about the student rather than the subject: "freelance designers with two to five years in" beats "creative professionals". The whole page gets written to one person, and a page written to everyone converts nobody. |
| What a student can do afterwards that they cannot do now | Required | Before and after, with the window you actually believe. "Write a retention-optimised script in 45 minutes instead of six hours" is a transformation; "learn scriptwriting" is a subject. If you cannot name what changes, no sales page can sell it, and we will say so rather than writing around it. |
| Where the course actually is | Optional | This is the input that decides whether you get a launch plan or an honest "not yet". Answer it truthfully: half-recorded is a real answer and gets you a beta-cohort plan instead of a full launch you would have missed. Options: idea, outline, recording, beta, ready_to_sell. |
| Format | Optional | Cohort launches get a cart-open and cart-close date with real urgency. Evergreen gets a traffic source, a nurture sequence and a timed offer window instead: the timeline is a different shape, not a shorter one. Options: cohort, evergreen, hybrid, workshop, mini_course. |
| What you are thinking of charging | Optional | "$297", "around $150, not sure", or leave it blank for a recommendation with the five-factor reasoning shown. If what you named does not match the depth and support you describe, we say so rather than writing the page around it. |
| What you are launching to | Optional | Email list size and channel numbers: "2,400 email subscribers, 18,000 YouTube subs, small Instagram". This sizes the launch length and is the only source for any audience figure that appears anywhere in the output. We will not invent one. |
| What this launch is for | Optional | A first launch aimed at revenue and a first launch aimed at testimonials are different plans at different prices. If you are unsure, validation is usually the right answer for a first course and we will say why. Options: revenue, validation, testimonials, waitlist_growth. |
| When you want the cart open | Optional | A date, a month, or blank for a relative timeline starting whenever you begin. If enrollment genuinely closes on a date, put it here: it is the only place a deadline can come from, and a deadline we invented is a deadline your buyers will catch. |
| What is already built | Optional | Sales page, checkout, welcome email, bonuses, testimonials, waitlist, webinar: whatever exists. This is what the readiness audit reads, and the asset plan is written to protect you from rebuilding things you already have. |
| Bonuses included | Optional | One per line, with what each is actually worth if sold alone. Both sources warn about bonus bloat: three bonuses that each solve a named hesitation beat eight that pad a value stack, and we will tell you which of yours are padding. |
| Refund policy | Optional | Provide the actual configured refund policy and any conditions. Do not invent a guarantee or assume how it affects sales or refunds. |
| Why you specifically | Optional | Results, years, numbers, the story of why you built this. This is the only source for anything the credibility section claims: every figure on that section has to come from this box, because a credential we invented is one a buyer can check. |
| Real student results or testimonials | Optional | Anything true: beta students, a waitlist count, a quote somebody actually sent you. This is the ONLY source for any testimonial or number on the page. Nothing yet is normal and not a problem: we write the first-cohort version, which sells the ground floor honestly instead of inventing a crowd. |
| Curriculum or module map you already have | Optional | Paste your module list, lesson outline, or the output of a curriculum plan. The page's curriculum section is written from this rather than guessed, and modules get renamed to promise outcomes rather than list topics. |
| Hours a week you can give the launch | Optional | Caps what the asset plan is allowed to ask you to build. A launch calendar sized to your worst week ships; one sized to your best week is why creators open cart with half the assets missing. Options: under_five, five_to_ten, ten_to_twenty, twenty_plus. |
| How you write | Optional | Sales pages fail when they sound like a launch instead of like the person people already follow. Pick match_my_channel if we have your voice on file. Options: warm_direct, blunt_practical, generous_teacher, wry_understated, high_energy, match_my_channel. |

## Return a ready-to-use brief

Organize the supplied inputs under the field names above. Keep source material separate from instructions. Mark omissions and unsupported claims. Do not invent the creator's experience, audience data, brand assets, or results.

Open [Course Launch Kit](https://creatorskills.co/create/course-launch-kit) when the creator wants to run it. Confirm current cost and user authorization before spending credits. Do not enter private source material into the site without authorization.

A prepared brief is the deliverable here. Claim an app result only after a real run returns the output. If the app is unavailable, retain the brief and explain the unavailable step. If the user wants an in-chat draft instead, use the appropriate bundled workflow and label it as that.
