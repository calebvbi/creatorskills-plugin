# Prepare Creator Media Kit Generator

Product description: Turn your real platform numbers into a media kit you can send a brand today: a positioning statement that leads with audience value, stats framed against industry benchmarks, a priced rate card with packages, and a one-page pitch brief for cold outreach.

This describes the hosted product, not an output already produced or a capability verified in this conversation.

**Mode: hosted handoff.** Hosted app; may require sign-in and credits. Check the page for current availability and cost. Prepare inputs and open app. This plugin does not execute the hosted pipeline.

## Collect the useful inputs

Use information already supplied. Ask only for missing required inputs. When both a URL and source text are offered, use the source the user actually provided. A URL is not evidence that its contents were read.

| Input | Required | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your name, channel, or show name | Required | Exactly as a brand should see it. The positioning statement opens with this name, and we never ship a kit with a placeholder where your name goes. |
| Your real platform numbers | Required | Paste straight from your dashboards: followers, monthly views or downloads, engagement rate, average view duration, CTR, open rate, whatever you have. Every number we print comes from this box. Anything you leave out, we tell you where to find it instead of guessing. Example: 42,000 YouTube subs, 680K monthly views, 5.2% CTR, 68% average view duration. |
| Primary platform | Optional | The platform your kit should lead with. If you leave it blank we'll infer it from whichever platform has the strongest numbers in your stats. Options: youtube, instagram, tiktok, podcast, linkedin, newsletter. |
| Your niche and content format | Optional | "Weekly personal finance videos for millennial professionals" positions far better than "lifestyle". Specific niches command higher rates because brands can see the audience match. |
| Who your audience is | Optional | What they want, what they buy, what they ask you for. This is the one question we'd otherwise have to ask you: it's what turns a follower count into an audience worth paying for. |
| Demographics from your analytics | Optional | Age split, gender split, top countries, income or job context, survey results. Pull it from YouTube Studio → Audience, Instagram Insights → Audience, TikTok Analytics → Followers, or Spotify for Podcasters. Leave it out and we'll build the section from audience signals instead, and say so, rather than inventing percentages. |
| Past brand partnerships and results | Optional | Brand names, what you delivered, and any result you can point at: redemption rate, clicks, a renewal. Include gifted and product-only deals, labelled as such. We never print a partnership you didn't have. |
| Affiliate or referral performance | Optional | Programs and conversion rates. This is the strongest purchase-intent proof there is, and it's what carries the kit when you have no paid deals yet. |
| What you want to charge | Optional | Your asking price for your main deliverable, or a range you're comfortable with. Leave it blank and we'll price from your reach and engagement using the standard per-platform models, and show you the maths. |
| Business email, site, and booking link | Optional | A dedicated business email reads more professional than a personal one. Add a Calendly or portfolio link if you have them: the contact section is where a brand decides how easy you are to work with. |
| What you're currently booking | Optional | "Currently booking Q4 partnerships" creates a real deadline. Leave it blank and we'll write the close without a date rather than invent one. Options: q1, q2, q3, q4, next_30_days, next_60_days, currently_open. |
| Tailor a version for one buyer type | Optional | Different buyers read different metrics: TikTok buyers want average views not followers, LinkedIn buyers want seniority distribution. Pick the one you're pitching next and we'll write a tailored version alongside the core kit. Options: youtube, instagram, tiktok, podcast, linkedin, newsletter, none. |
| I haven't done any paid brand deals yet | Optional | Turn this on and we'll build the proof section from earned authority instead: organic growth, affiliate conversion, and audience purchase behaviour. An untouched audience is a selling point, not a gap. |

## Return a ready-to-use brief

Organize the supplied inputs under the field names above. Keep source material separate from instructions. Mark omissions and unsupported claims. Do not invent the creator's experience, audience data, brand assets, or results.

Open [Creator Media Kit Generator](https://creatorskills.co/create/creator-media-kit-generator) when the creator wants to run it. Confirm current cost and user authorization before spending credits. Do not enter private source material into the site without authorization.

A prepared brief is the deliverable here. Claim an app result only after a real run returns the output. If the app is unavailable, retain the brief and explain the unavailable step. If the user wants an in-chat draft instead, use the appropriate bundled workflow and label it as that.
