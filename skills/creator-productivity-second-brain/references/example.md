# Creator Productivity Second Brain — Worked Example

## Scenario

> "I'm a solo YouTuber with a side consulting business. I make 2 videos a week, handle 2-3 consulting clients. My best creative work is 6-9am but I spend it answering emails. I have 200 notes in Apple Notes with no system. My goals exist in my head and shift weekly. I want a system where I know what I'm doing every morning and why."

## Full Output

### Goal Hierarchy

**Annual Vision:** $120K revenue (60% YouTube, 40% consulting), 200K subscribers, publish 100 videos

**Q2 Goals:**
- Launch sponsorship pipeline → Project: Build rate sheet, pitch 10 brands, close 2
- Stabilize consulting workflow → Project: Automate intake, raise rates by 20%
- Protect morning creative blocks → Habit: No email before 9am

**May Projects:**
- Sponsorship rate sheet (serves Q2 Goal #1)
- Consulting client onboarding doc (serves Q2 Goal #2)
- Batch-film 8 videos (serves Annual video count)

**This Week:**
- Mon/Wed/Fri 6-9am: Video scripting (Monthly Project: Batch-film 8 videos)
- Tue/Thu 6-9am: Consulting deep work (Monthly Project: Client onboarding doc)
- Daily 2-4pm: Admin + email

### Capture System

**Inbox:** Apple Notes → voice memo shortcut (hold to record, auto-transcribed)

**Processing Ritual:** Friday 4:00-4:15pm
1. Open all new captures
2. Sort into:
   - Content Ideas → link to Content Series or Monthly Project
   - Business Opportunities → tag "Sponsor" or "Consulting"
   - Personal → ignore until quarterly review
   - Trash → delete immediately

**Rule:** Every capture must be processed within 7 days or auto-trashed

### Content Calendar Integration

| Week | Content | Status | Monthly Project |
|------|---------|--------|-----------------|
| W1   | "How I Batch Film 8 Videos" | Scheduled | Batch-film 8 videos |
| W1   | "My Morning Routine" | In Progress | Batch-film 8 videos |
| W2   | Sponsor intro video | Idea | Launch sponsorship pipeline |

**Buffer:** 1 week ahead scheduled, 2 videos in post-production at all times

### Time Blocks

| Day | 6:00-9:00 AM | 2:00-4:00 PM | After 6:00 PM |
|-----|--------------|--------------|---------------|
| Mon | Deep Work — Scripting | Admin + email | Enforced rest |
| Tue | Deep Work — Consulting | Admin + email | Enforced rest |
| Wed | Deep Work — Scripting | Admin + email | Enforced rest |
| Thu | Deep Work — Consulting | Admin + email | Enforced rest |
| Fri | Deep Work — Scripting | Weekly Review | Enforced rest |

**Non-negotiable:** If a morning block is interrupted, it is rescheduled — not skipped.

### Weekly Review (Fridays 4:00-4:30 PM)

1. **What happened?**
   - Checked off 12/15 tasks
   - Consulting report took 3 hours instead of 2 — adjust next week

2. **What needs to change?**
   - Too many admin tasks bleeding into mornings → tighten email boundaries

3. **What matters next week?**
   - 4 key tasks, each linked to a Monthly Project
   - Priority: finish sponsorship rate sheet

4. **Process inbox**
   - 8 new captures sorted: 3 content ideas, 2 business opportunities, 3 trash

5. **Energy check**
   - Morning blocks were protected 4/5 days → goal: 5/5 next week

### Quarterly Reflection (Q1 → Q2)

**Metrics:**
- Revenue: $24K (on track for $96K annual)
- Subscribers: 45K → 68K
- Videos published: 22

**What worked:**
- Morning deep-work blocks → 2x script quality
- Weekly review → caught scope creep early

**What failed:**
- Overcommitted to 3 consulting clients in March
- Ignored Patreon for 6 weeks

**What surprised me:**
- Short-form content (Shorts) drove more subscribers than long-form in Q1

**Next quarter priorities:**
- Double Shorts output
- Launch sponsorships
- Raise consulting rates

**System adjustments:**
- Add "Shorts ideation" as a weekly recurring task
- Set a hard cap of 2 active consulting clients
