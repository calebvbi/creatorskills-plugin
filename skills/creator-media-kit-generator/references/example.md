# Worked Example: A Media Kit With Verified Inputs and Open Fields

## Synthetic creator input

A personal-finance creator supplies 42,000 YouTube subscribers, 680,000 monthly views, 5.2% impression CTR and 68% average percentage viewed. Instagram has 8,400 followers and 4.1% engagement under the creator's existing definition. The audience is described as women aged 25–38, primarily in the US, Canada and UK, with household income $45K–$90K. Affiliate conversion figures are Acorns 3.2% and Rocket Money 2.8%, without denominators or reporting periods. There have been no paid brand deals. No identity, growth history, surveys or industry benchmarks are supplied.

## Positioning draft

[Creator name] makes personal-finance content for millennial professionals, including budgeting, debt payoff and investing basics. The channel has 42,000 YouTube subscribers and 680,000 monthly views in the supplied reporting period. The creator is seeking a first paid brand partnership.

## Metrics page

| Metric | Value | Detail to confirm before sharing |
|---|---:|---|
| YouTube subscribers | 42,000 | Snapshot date |
| Monthly views | 680,000 | Exact dates and view definition |
| Impression CTR | 5.2% | Period and source mix |
| Average percentage viewed | 68% | Format/length mix; does not mean all viewers finish |
| Instagram followers | 8,400 | Snapshot date |
| Instagram engagement | 4.1% | Numerator, denominator and period |

Do not add growth, demographic percentages, traffic-source shares or top-percentile claims. Describe the supplied audience profile as the creator's account of their audience, pending source confirmation. Leave country percentages and survey claims blank.

## Prior work and affiliate context

Paid sponsorships: none reported. Affiliate conversion figures: 3.2% and 2.8%, with attribution windows and denominators to confirm. Do not compare them with an invented category average or derive a total activation count. Request permission before using brand logos or sharing private dashboard evidence.

## Proposed partnership menu

Offer a draft scope such as one integration, a dedicated demonstration or a cross-platform package. Rates, rights, revisions, deadlines, exclusivity and measurement are negotiation fields. A package proposal is not proof of availability or expected sales. Include only deliverables the creator approves.

## Pitch excerpt

Hi [contact],

I make [confirmed topic] content for [confirmed audience]. My channel has 42,000 YouTube subscribers and 680,000 monthly views during [confirmed period]. I'd like to discuss a [proposed deliverable] about [relevant, verified product angle]. I have not run a paid sponsorship yet; I can share approved examples of my existing work.

Would this be relevant to your current plans?

[Creator name and confirmed contact]

## Final review

Verify all reporting periods and definitions, add actual contact details and approved links, remove unknown fields that are unnecessary, and check any attachments exist before saying they are attached. This example creates a draft, not a sent pitch or exported media-kit file.
