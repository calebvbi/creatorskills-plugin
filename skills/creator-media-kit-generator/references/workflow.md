# Creator Media Kit Generator

Create a concise, truthful media kit and optional outreach draft from the creator's supplied evidence. A polished kit must not add achievements, demographics, results, product use or industry comparisons that the creator cannot substantiate.

## 1. Gather the minimum evidence

Ask for creator name/handle, niche, audience description, active platforms, reporting dates, metric definitions, approved work samples, prior brand work, actual offer scope and contact details. Accept redacted aggregates. Ask permission before using private analytics or third-party logos. If something is missing, omit it or mark a visible placeholder; do not manufacture a plausible profile.

## 2. Write positioning

State who the creator serves and what they make using supplied facts. Use actual credentials or experience only. A subscriber count does not establish trust, purchase intent or a professional credential. Do not invent years of work, organic growth history or personal product usage.

## 3. Assemble metrics

Use platform-specific definitions and consistent windows. For video, distinguish subscribers, views, eligible impressions, CTR and viewing measures. For social, state whether engagement uses views, followers or reach. For newsletters, separate delivered messages, measured opens and unique clicks; tracking can be affected by privacy features. For podcasts, include the actual download/listener definition and observation window.

Show counts and denominators, not just flattering rates. Do not divide average duration by an unrelated average video length. An external comparison requires a dated source, compatible metric and relevant population; otherwise omit it. No universal open-rate or engagement benchmark is supplied by this skill.

## 4. Describe the audience

Separate observed analytics, voluntary survey findings and the creator's qualitative impression. Give sample size and period for survey percentages. Leave income, purchase intent, country shares and age/gender distribution unknown unless supplied. Saves and views do not prove purchase-research intent or conversions.

## 5. Show work and propose packages

Use approved links and label self-initiated work accurately. A previous brand relationship is not proof of a successful campaign. Attribute results to the actual source and window, and preserve causal limitations.

Draft packages with deliverables, rights, revisions, deadlines and asking prices approved by the creator. Optional add-ons are proposals. Do not silently grant perpetual use or promise results, access or availability. Confirm current legal/disclosure and platform requirements when relevant.

## 6. Format and handoff

A useful two-page structure is positioning and audience evidence on page one; work samples, proposed packages and contact on page two. Use readable labels and a last-updated date. Adapt length to the user's request. If no export tool was used, describe the output as copy/layout rather than a generated PDF.

Before sharing, verify numbers, definitions, dates, links, permissions and actual attachments. An email may say a kit is attached only if it is. Return the kit, missing-evidence checklist and optional unsent pitch. Sending, exporting or publishing requires the relevant authorization and observed tool result.
