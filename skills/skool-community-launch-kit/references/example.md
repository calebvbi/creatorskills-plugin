# Worked Example: A Fitness Community Launch Plan

## Synthetic creator input

A fitness coach focused on sustainable activity for women over 35 reports 12,000 Instagram followers, 2,400 email subscribers and two years of one-to-one coaching. They are exploring a $47/month Skool community with a supportive tone. No medical qualification, hormone assessment, health outcomes, trial configuration or member demand data is supplied.

## Positioning options

- Strong After 35: practical fitness habits and peer support.
- Everyday Movement Circle: build a routine that fits your life.
- Consistent Together: a supportive place to practice and learn.

Avoid promises about balancing hormones, fixing metabolism or diagnosing why someone struggles. The platform and community plan do not establish medical efficacy.

## Proposed offer

A weekly activity-planning discussion, optional progress reflection, a resource library using material the coach is qualified and authorized to share, and a monthly group Q&A within confirmed scope. Establish participation and privacy rules. Do not require members to post medical details, body measurements or diagnostic self-assessments.

The proposed $47/month price is a testable offer, not proof of demand. Any founding price, capacity cap, trial, refund policy or cancellation term requires the creator's explicit decision and verification of the actual configured platform behavior. No false scarcity is added.

## Welcome sequence

1. Welcome and clear explanation of the confirmed benefits and support limits.
2. Optional introduction with a personal goal the member chooses to share.
3. A simple activity-planning worksheet; members can keep it private.
4. Directions to the next scheduled community session once confirmed.

## First-month draft calendar

Week 1: discuss routines and realistic scheduling.
Week 2: share optional ways to notice progress without medical claims.
Week 3: review obstacles and adapt the schedule.
Week 4: collect feedback on which community activities were useful.

The coach must review any exercise instruction for qualifications, audience suitability and appropriate scope. Do not invent perimenopause modules or claim member demand for them.

## Launch drafts

"I'm exploring a community for women over 35 who want support building consistent fitness habits. The proposed format is [confirmed benefits]. If that would help you, [optional verified interest route]. I'll share the final scope and terms before anyone joins."

Send only to an authorized audience under confirmed recipient permissions. No post, email, billing setup or community has been created by this example.

## Review plan

Measure voluntary interest, attendance, useful feedback and fulfillment time. Follower count is not expected member count. Choose a small test and review actual demand and workload before expanding. Existing coaching commitments remain unchanged.
