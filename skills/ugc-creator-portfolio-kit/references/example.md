# Worked Examples: Portfolio and Retainer Drafts

## Example 1: New creator

Synthetic input: a creator has three self-initiated UGC samples, no paid deals and wants a portfolio for skincare brands. Product experience and usage permissions are not yet documented.

Output: build a portfolio with a short positioning statement, the three approved sample links, a clear label "self-initiated sample, not a paid brand campaign," and a proposed services menu. For each sample describe the actual brief, creative choices, format and rights status. Do not invent client endorsements, ad performance or paid partnerships. Product claims require supporting material and honest experience. Contact details and availability remain placeholders until supplied.

A rate proposal should name scope, revision limits, usage term, raw footage and add-ons. Prices are proposed terms, not market-certified values. Do not imply that a high follower count is required or that a template guarantees a deal.

## Example 2: Existing client retainer

Synthetic input: a fitness creator has completed 12 paid UGC deals at $300–$600 per video. A fictional client, FitFuel, bought five videos at $400 each. The client reported that the latest video's ad CTR was 3.2%, its highest that quarter. The creator wants to ask $750 per standalone video or propose four videos per month for $2,800. Current turnaround is four days.

Draft email:

Hi [contact],

I'd like to discuss a monthly arrangement for our next videos. We've completed five projects together. You shared a 3.2% ad CTR for the latest video and described it as your highest this quarter; I'd like to confirm the reporting window before including that in a public case study.

My proposed standalone rate is $750 per video. Alternatively, four videos per month would be $2,800, or $700 each. We'd agree the formats, usage rights, revision limit, schedule and payment terms before starting. The current four-day turnaround is a starting point for that discussion.

Would a monthly plan fit your needs?

[Name]

## Response if asked for proof

"The performance result I currently have is your report of 3.2% CTR on the latest video. I don't have other campaign-level figures to share. If useful, we can review the relevant dashboard together and agree what can be quoted."

No 2.8%/2.6% historical CTRs, brand averages, 180K views, 45K views or saves are fabricated. A client-reported result is labelled as such and does not guarantee future performance.

## Negotiation options

Three videos at $700 each would be $2,100. Two standalone videos at $750 each would be $1,500. These are proposed scopes; rights, revisions and scheduling must be agreed rather than silently included. Do not invent a full calendar, impending scarcity or a claim that current rates are unsustainable without cost evidence.
