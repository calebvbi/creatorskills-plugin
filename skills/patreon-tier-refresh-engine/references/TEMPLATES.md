## Existing-member transition checklist

- Confirm all existing prices, benefits and promises.
- Verify provider support and current requirements.
- Preserve existing arrangements unless an explicitly agreed change is authorized.
- Require explicit opt-in for a proposed migration; no automatic price increase from silence.
- Explain any choice, price and benefit change clearly before consent.
- Document a reviewed remedy for benefits that cannot continue.
- Verify a sandbox or limited authorized transition before broader application.
