# Worked Example: An Opt-In Membership Proposal

## Synthetic creator input

A creator reports 120 members: 90 at $3, 28 at $10 and 2 at $25. The current benefits are early access, wallpapers plus Discord, and feedback sessions respectively. Monthly churn is reported as 8%; the definition and window need confirmation. The top tier has had no new member in six months. Feedback takes two hours per month. No member survey or competitor research is supplied.

## Current-state audit

Gross monthly tier receipts are 90×$3 + 28×$10 + 2×$25 = $600 before fees/refunds. The $3 tier represents 75% of members but 45% of gross receipts. The feedback tier contributes $50 gross for the stated two hours, before other work or costs. Whether that is acceptable depends on the creator's goals and costs.

Hypotheses: the middle-tier benefits may need clearer value; the top-tier format may be costly to maintain. Do not claim members' motives or that a competitor has a specific tier or membership count without evidence. Ask existing members which benefits they actually use.

## Proposed tiers

Keep a $3 support/early-access option. Explore a $12 process-content option and a $20 group-critique option, subject to the creator's real capacity and member interest. These are proposed offers, not changes already configured. Document benefit scope, scheduling and service limits before launch.

## Existing-member transition

Preserve existing price and benefit commitments unless the creator and member explicitly agree to a supported change. Verify current platform tools and requirements. Do not auto-migrate existing members or increase their charges from silence. Provide an opt-in invitation and a clear way to stay on the existing arrangement. Any inability to honor an old benefit requires a specific, reviewed customer remedy, not an invented default.

Draft announcement:

"I'm exploring two new membership options, [confirmed descriptions]. Your existing arrangement will remain in place unless we agree to a change. If you'd like to review the new options, [verified opt-in route]. I'll confirm the exact benefits and terms before you choose."

No post has been published, message sent or membership changed.

## Scenario math

| Hypothetical member distribution | Members | Gross monthly receipts |
|---|---:|---:|
| Current: 90×$3, 28×$10, 2×$25 | 120 | $600 |
| Scenario A: 80×$3, 35×$12, 8×$20 | 123 | $820 |
| Scenario B: 75×$3, 40×$12, 12×$20 | 127 | $945 |

These scenarios assume specific voluntary participation; they are not forecasts. Scenario B is $345 above the current gross, or 57.5%, before costs. It is not 130 members or $1,020. Also model no opt-ins, cancellations and changed fulfillment costs. Do not call an upside scenario conservative without evidence.

## Review plan

Collect member feedback, estimate actual fulfillment time, verify platform transition behavior and propose a small opt-in trial. Track benefit use, cancellations with defined denominators, service workload and contribution. Honor existing commitments throughout.
