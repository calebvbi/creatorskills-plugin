# KDP Self-Publishing Launch Kit

Apply the Grok Bot compatibility rules in the parent SKILL.md. This is an adaptation of the MIT CreatorSkills workflow for planning a book launch; it does not publish a book or purchase advertising.

## 1. Validate the topic

Reuse the creator's audience, proposed topic, format, goals and budget. Ask for the missing constraint that most affects the plan. Separate supplied facts, observed market research and untested hypotheses.

When browsing is available, inspect relevant Amazon listings and current official KDP guidance. Record source URLs and observation dates for price, review count, format and page count. Search suggestions show wording, not monthly search volume. Do not invent competitor books, keyword volumes or an empty market. Without research tools, return a research worksheet and mark validation pending.

Choose a defensible reader problem and differentiation hypothesis. Sales rank is not units sold and cannot be multiplied by price to estimate revenue. Only calculate a labeled scenario from stated unit-sales assumptions and verified applicable royalty/cost rules. Show assumptions, delivery or printing costs, taxes where applicable, and advertising costs separately. Never call a scenario a forecast.

## 2. Draft metadata

Produce a benefit-led title, subtitle, draft keywords, short and long descriptions, and author bio using supplied facts. Validate the current official limits and rules before declaring them upload-ready. Any book contents, credentials, endorsements or results not supplied must remain placeholders or proposed manuscript features. Avoid unsupported superlatives and keyword stuffing.

## 3. Outline the manuscript

Give chapter purpose, reader takeaway and proposed length, with totals checked. Use a clear progression from the reader's problem through the proposed solution. Label word and page counts as planning assumptions; layout and illustrations change page counts. Do not imply the manuscript already exists.

## 4. Prepare a cover brief

Include title hierarchy, genre references, visual concept, back-cover blurb and required assets. Choose trim size with the creator; derive print dimensions, bleed and spine width from current KDP templates for the actual page count, paper and binding. Do not use a universal print specification. Endorsements and author photography require real, authorized assets.

## 5. Build a launch calendar

Use a requested date or relative dates. A plan spanning Day -14 through Day +21 covers 36 calendar days including launch day. Name the actual range instead of calling it a 21-day plan. Include preparation, launch and follow-up tasks, owners, inputs and dependencies. Review requests must follow current platform policies. Draft outreach; do not send it or promise reviews.

## 6. Design an optional advertising experiment

Only include advertising if it fits the creator's budget. Propose a campaign type, a small keyword hypothesis list, test duration, user-approved spending cap and stop rule. Explain how to measure impressions, clicks, orders, spend and contribution after costs. Do not prescribe universal bids, conversion targets or profitable ACoS. No campaign creation or spending occurs without separate authorization.

## 7. Compare pricing scenarios

Use current official KDP eligibility and pricing rules for the chosen format, marketplace and territory. Royalty eligibility varies with conditions; never apply a blanket rate to all prices. Compare plausible prices using stated unit-sales assumptions, applicable per-unit proceeds and costs. Show what is unknown. Do not promise that a discount will improve rank, reviews or revenue.

Deliver the requested launch plan, usable draft copy and one prioritized next action. Keep research-pending items visible beside the affected decision.
