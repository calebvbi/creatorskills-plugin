# Worked example — illustrative planning, not market research

## Supplied brief

“I have a fitness YouTube channel and 15,000 newsletter subscribers. I want to write my first short ebook about home gyms for beginners in small apartments. I have not researched competitors or priced equipment.”

## Example output

**Positioning hypothesis:** a beginner's guide to choosing a small set of equipment that fits an apartment. Market differentiation remains unverified.

**Research worksheet:** inspect comparable listings; record title, URL, observation date, format, price, review count and reader complaints. Current values are unknown. Search suggestions can suggest language but do not establish search volume. Price the proposed equipment before using a budget promise in the title.

**Working title:** Your First Apartment Gym. **Subtitle:** A beginner's guide to choosing equipment, planning your space, and getting started. Both are draft copy, subject to manuscript fit and current KDP metadata rules.

**Outline:** define goals and space; set a budget; compare equipment choices; plan storage; explain a beginner routine with appropriate expertise; build a maintenance checklist. Proposed manuscript target: 20,000 words. Page count is unknown until layout.

**Cover brief:** show a small, practical setup with room to move; clear title at thumbnail size. Trim, bleed and spine specifications await format and page-count decisions. No endorsement has been supplied, so omit testimonials.

**Calendar:** Day -14 to -1, validate manuscript and production files; Day 0 to +7, publish through the creator's own approved account steps and share prepared announcement drafts; Day +8 to +21, collect permitted feedback and review observed outcomes. This is a 36-day planning window, not completed or scheduled work. Confirm current platform review policies before outreach.

**Advertising:** no budget was supplied. Prepare keyword hypotheses and a measurement worksheet; do not launch or spend. Set the budget, duration and stop rule with the creator if they choose to test ads.

**Pricing:** no royalty quote or earnings forecast yet. Verify current official KDP eligibility and costs for the chosen format and territory. A later scenario can use assumed units × verified net proceeds per unit − advertising and other costs, with each assumption labeled. Sales rank cannot supply a verified unit-sales count.

**Next action:** research three relevant listings and price a proposed equipment list before choosing a numerical budget promise.
