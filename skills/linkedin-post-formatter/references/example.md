# LinkedIn Post Formatter - Worked Example

## Creator Input

```text
Idea: I stopped chasing viral content and doubled qualified leads.
Audience: Freelancers and solo consultants
Goal: Comments

```

## Generated Output

### Post Version A - Story Format
I stopped chasing viral content.

And my qualified leads doubled in 60 days.

For months, I optimized for reach.
More views. Worse inquiries.

Then I changed one thing:
I wrote for my ideal client, not the feed.

Less vanity.
More intent.
Better pipeline.

What changed your lead quality most this year?

### Post Version B - Tactical Format
Your content can grow reach and still miss revenue.

Framework that improved my lead quality:

1) Pick one audience with one urgent problem
2) Write posts that diagnose that problem
3) End with one conversation CTA
4) Track qualified replies, not impressions

Which metric are you optimizing right now?

### Hook Variants
1. Viral reach does not guarantee better leads.
2. I traded reach for relevance and won.
3. More views, fewer buyers? Fix this.
4. Your content KPI might be wrong.
5. Reach went down. Revenue went up.

### Comment Starter
1. I can share the exact template if useful.
2. Want my qualified-lead scoring criteria?

### Formatting Check
- Hook leads with tension
- Short lines for mobile readability
- Clear paragraph spacing
- One core idea per post
- One CTA question only
- No jargon-heavy filler
