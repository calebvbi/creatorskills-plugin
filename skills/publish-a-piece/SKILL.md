# Publish a piece

Deliver the requested piece at the requested depth. Use the topic, audience, platform, intended takeaway, and evidence already supplied. Ask for the missing central topic or source when necessary; do not make the creator fill out a long intake form.

## Build around one honest promise

Write one sentence stating what the audience will understand or be able to do. Separate confirmed facts from the creator's opinions and examples still needed. Match the opening to that promise. Avoid a title that promises a result the body cannot support.

Verify demonstration-critical factual claims against an authoritative source, particularly exact temperatures, safety instructions, and causal explanations. If verification is unavailable, mark the claim for checking outside the spoken copy.

Offer three meaningfully different title-and-opening pairs unless the user requests another count. Explain their tradeoffs briefly: curiosity, specificity, or emotional relevance. Do not score them as predicted click-through rates.

Choose the most defensible pair for the stated audience. Draft an outline with a concrete scene, demonstration, evidence, or example in each section. For a requested full script, write the script rather than returning an outline. Estimate spoken duration from word count and label it an estimate.

## Prepare production

Include a short shot or visual list and a thumbnail concept when relevant. Each concept must reinforce the title without repeating every word. Describe composition, focal subject, and any necessary text. Label it a concept until an image has actually been generated.

End with an exact recording or writing action and a short pre-publication check: factual gaps, rights for third-party assets, links to verify, and whether the opening pays off. Apply only checks relevant to this piece.

Never turn invented personal experience into the creator's testimony. Keep missing proof in a clearly marked note outside the spoken copy. Do not publish, schedule, send, or buy assets unless that action is authorized. Respect any narrower scope the user requested.
