# Worked Example: Community Posts Grounded in a Video Brief

## Creator input

A budget-tech creator reports 18,000 subscribers, an Android-focused audience and Tuesday/Thursday uploads. Their next Thursday video reviews the Motorola Edge 50 Neo. They believe it is a strong option under $250. No test duration, exact current price, specification findings or personal anecdote is supplied.

## Monday poll draft

What matters most when you choose a budget phone?
- Battery life
- Camera
- Software support
- Price

Verify the current platform's poll options before publishing.

## Tuesday preview draft

Thursday's video is my review of the Motorola Edge 50 Neo. I'll explain why it made my shortlist and where it might not fit. What would you want answered before buying a budget Android phone?

Do not say it was tested for three weeks or give a confirmed $200 price without evidence.

## Wednesday discussion draft

Which budget-phone tradeoff is hardest for you to accept? I'd like to compare your questions with what the review actually covers.

## Thursday launch draft

The review is [live at verified link / planned for confirmed time]. It covers [points verified from the finished video].

Choose the correct state after verification; a planned upload is not a live video. No link or content detail is invented.

## Follow-up draft

Which question still needs a clearer answer after the review? I can use those replies to plan a follow-up.

## Measurement

Compare actual participation and clicks using the creator's normal post baseline. These drafts do not guarantee early distribution, sales or an algorithm boost. No post has been published.
