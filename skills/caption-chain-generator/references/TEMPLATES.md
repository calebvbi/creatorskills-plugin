# Caption Templates — Fill-in-the-Blank Frameworks

Use these templates when you need a quick caption without generating a full calendar. Fill in the brackets, adjust the tone to match your voice, and post.

Each template includes: opening hook formula, body structure, call to action, and hashtag guidance.

---

## 1. Value Post Template

**Hook:**
```
The [fastest/simplest/most overlooked] way to [desired result] is [surprising method].
```

**Body:**
```
Here's how it works:

- [Tip 1 — one sentence, specific and actionable]
- [Tip 2 — one sentence, specific and actionable]
- [Tip 3 — one sentence, specific and actionable]

[Optional: 1-2 sentences explaining WHY this approach works better than the obvious one]

Most people [common mistake]. Instead, [what to do differently].
```

**CTA:**
```
Save this for the next time you [situation where they'd need this advice].
```

**Hashtag guidance:** Use niche-specific educational tags. Include one broad tag (#tips, #howto) and the rest specific to your topic.

---

## 2. Personal Story Template

**Hook:**
```
[Specific time reference] I [unexpected or vulnerable moment].
```
Examples: "Last Wednesday I opened my DMs and almost cried." / "Three years ago I was [past situation] making $[amount]."

**Body:**
```
[Set the scene in 1-2 sentences — where you were, what was happening]

[What went wrong or what changed — 2-3 sentences]

[The turning point — what you did differently]

[The result — be specific with numbers or outcomes]

Here's what I took from it: [One clear lesson in 1-2 sentences]
```

**CTA:**
```
When was the last time [related experience]? Tell me your story — I read every comment.
```

**Hashtag guidance:** Mix personal/authentic tags (#myjourney, #realstory) with niche tags. Keep it warm, not promotional.

---

## 3. Hot Take Template

**Hook:**
```
Unpopular opinion: [thing most people in your niche believe] is [why it's wrong/overrated/counterproductive].
```

**Body:**
```
Here's what I mean.

[Describe what most people do — 1-2 sentences]

The problem with that:
[Reason 1 — backed by your experience]
[Reason 2 — with a specific example or data point]

What actually works instead:
[Your alternative approach — 2-3 sentences with proof]
```

**CTA:**
```
Agree or disagree? I want to hear your take. Don't hold back.
```

**Hashtag guidance:** Include 1-2 conversation-driving tags (#unpopularopinion, #hottake) alongside your niche tags.

---

## 4. Behind-the-Scenes Template

**Hook:**
```
Here's what [the thing your audience sees] actually looks like behind the scenes.
```
Examples: "Here's what making a 60-second reel actually takes." / "The real process behind every client proposal I send."

**Body:**
```
[Time/Step 1]: [What actually happens — honest, specific]
[Time/Step 2]: [What actually happens — include a messy or funny detail]
[Time/Step 3]: [What actually happens]
[Time/Step 4]: [What actually happens]

It's not glamorous. But it [works/gets results/keeps me sane].

[1 sentence reflecting on what this process has taught you]
```

**CTA:**
```
What does your version of this look like? Messy answers encouraged.
```

**Hashtag guidance:** Use behind-the-scenes and process tags (#bts #behindthescenes #myprocess) mixed with your niche tags.

---

## 5. Carousel Lead-In Template

**Hook:**
```
[Number] [things/mistakes/steps/secrets] that [desirable outcome].
```
Examples: "5 changes that doubled my engagement this month." / "The 7 mistakes killing your reach (swipe to check)."

**Body:**
```
[1-2 sentences of context — what problem this carousel solves or why you made it]

Swipe through to [see them all / grab the full breakdown / find the one that applies to you].
```

**CTA:**
```
Save this and come back to it when you [relevant situation]. Which slide hit hardest? Tell me below.
```

**Hashtag guidance:** Include saveable/shareable tags (#savethis, #tipsandtricks) alongside your niche. Carousels get discovered through saves — hashtag for discoverability.

---

## 6. Question/Poll Template

**Hook:**
```
[Direct question — no preamble].
```
Examples: "What's the one tool you couldn't run your business without?" / "Real talk: how many hours do you actually spend on content per week?"

**Body (optional):**
```
[Your answer in 1-2 sentences to prime the conversation]

OR

Here are the options:
A) [Option]
B) [Option]
C) [Option]
D) [Something unexpected or funny]
```

**CTA:**
```
Drop yours below. [Optional reason: "I'm building a resource list" / "I'm genuinely curious" / "Best answer gets featured in my next post"]
```

**Hashtag guidance:** Light on hashtags for pure engagement posts. The goal is comments, not discovery. 8-10 relevant tags max on Instagram. 2-3 on LinkedIn.

## 7. Testimonial Feature Template

**Hook:**
```
[Name] went from [starting point] to [impressive result] in [timeframe].
```

**Body:**
```
When [they] started, [they] were [specific situation — paint the before picture].

What changed:
- [Change 1 — what they did differently]
- [Change 2 — the key shift]

[Timeframe] later: [specific result with numbers].

[1-2 sentences in their own words if you have a quote]

[1 sentence acknowledging their effort — this is about them, not you]
```

**CTA:**
```
Drop a [emoji/word] to celebrate [Name].
```
OR (if promoting):
```
If you want results like this, [specific next step — link, DM trigger word, etc.].
```

**Hashtag guidance:** Mix celebration tags (#clientwins, #transformation, #results) with your niche. Don't use overly promotional tags — it undermines the authenticity.

## 8. Product Promotion Template

**Hook:**
```
[Describe the problem your product solves — use "you" language].
```
Examples: "Tired of spending Sunday writing captions that get 12 likes?" / "You know that feeling when you have 30 tabs open trying to figure out your SEO?"

**Body:**
```
[Describe the "before" state — what life/work looks like without your solution. 2-3 sentences.]

[Transition: "That's why I built [product name]." or "Here's what [product] does differently:"]

[Describe the "after" state — what changes when they use your product. Focus on transformation, not features. 2-3 sentences.]

[One proof point: a number, a testimonial quote, a specific result]
```

**CTA:**
```
[Direct action — "Link in bio" / "DM me [word] for the link" / "Grab it at [URL]"]
[Optional urgency — only if genuine: "Price goes up Friday" / "Only 20 spots this round"]
```

**Hashtag guidance:** Use your normal niche hashtags. Don't add #ad or #sponsored unless legally required (paid partnerships). Keep it feeling like a natural post, not a commercial break.

## 9. Milestone Celebration Template

**Hook:**
```
[Milestone number]. [Short emotional reaction].
```
Examples: "10,000 followers. I genuinely can't believe it." / "First $10K month. Let me tell you how this felt."

**Body:**
```
[Where you started — 2-3 sentences. Be specific about the early days.]

[What surprised you along the way — 1-2 sentences]

[What you've learned — the honest takeaway, not a humble-brag]

[Thank your audience specifically — not generically. "Every one of you who DM'd me after that post about X" is better than "thanks to my amazing community."]
```

**CTA:**
```
To celebrate, [something valuable — a free resource, a giveaway, sharing your best tip, answering questions in the comments].
```

**Hashtag guidance:** Celebration and gratitude tags (#milestone, #grateful, #thankyou) plus your niche. These posts get high saves and shares — optimize hashtags for reach.

---

## 10. Community Callout Template

**Hook:**
```
This one's for [specific subset of your audience].
```
Examples: "This one's for everyone who started this month and already wants to quit." / "Shoutout to the ones doing this with no support system."

**Body:**
```
[Validate what they're going through — show that you see them. 2-3 sentences.]

[Share something encouraging that's specific, not generic. A real insight or perspective shift.]

[Optional: spotlight a specific community member's win or effort]

[Close with affirmation — but make it earned, not hollow]
```

**CTA:**
```
Tag someone who needs to hear this today.
```
OR:
```
If this is you, drop a [emoji/word] so I know you're here. I see you.
```

**Hashtag guidance:** Community and support tags (#community, #youvegotthis, #support) mixed with niche. These posts build loyalty — optimize for shares and saves over discovery.
