# Content Audit & Cleanup — Templates

Ready-to-use templates for running your own audits. Copy, fill in your data, and feed them to the AI for structured analysis.

---

## Template 1: Content Inventory Spreadsheet

Use this format to prepare your content list before running an audit. Paste it into the conversation as-is — the system knows how to read it.

```
CONTENT INVENTORY — [Your Brand/Site Name]

Format: [Blog / YouTube / Podcast / Newsletter / Mixed]
Total Pieces: [Number]
Date Range: [Oldest publish date] to [Newest]
Primary Goal: [What you want from this audit]

#  | Title                              | URL/ID      | Published  | Views/Mo | Keyword (if known) | Notes
---|--------------------------------------|-------------|------------|----------|--------------------|---------
1  | [Title]                            | [URL]       | [Date]     | [Number] | [Keyword]          | [Any issues you know about]
2  | [Title]                            | [URL]       | [Date]     | [Number] | [Keyword]          |
3  | [Title]                            | [URL]       | [Date]     | [Number] | [Keyword]          |
...
```

### Tips for filling this out

- **Views/Mo:** Use whatever metric you have — monthly pageviews, total video views, average listens. Consistency matters more than precision.
- **Keyword:** Leave blank if you don't know. The audit can evaluate without it.
- **Notes:** Flag anything you already know is wrong — broken links, outdated info, content you're considering removing.
