# Worked Example: A Reviewable Cleanup Plan

## Synthetic input

A developer blog owner supplies six Git articles with a combined 4,200 monthly views and four API articles with 1,900 monthly views. Several topics appear to overlap. The owner asks for an audit. Backlinks, conversions, search queries, contractual retention needs and individual content quality have not been supplied.

## Candidate decisions

| Group | Observation | Proposed next step |
|---|---|---|
| Git articles | Six related pieces, 4,200 combined monthly views | Compare query intent and content before proposing consolidation |
| API articles | Four related pieces, 1,900 combined monthly views | Inspect overlap and distinct reader tasks |

The totals do not establish keyword cannibalization or a traffic lift from merging. Do not forecast 7,000–9,000 or 3,000–4,000 views. Topics can overlap while serving different intents.

## Evidence inventory

For every URL collect actual content, reporting period, query/traffic definitions, conversions, important backlinks, useful unique material, dependencies and retention obligations. Record missing evidence. Score only dimensions supported by observations; a low score is a review flag, not deletion authorization.

## Proposed consolidation plan, if evidence supports it

Identify an exact URL set and the best destination for the shared reader intent. Preserve unique useful material. Back up originals, record the mapping and draft the merged content. Validate that proposed redirects are relevant and do not create loops or break important links. Preserve distinct pages when they answer different questions.

## Approval and verification

The audit returns draft recommendations. Before publishing, redirecting or deleting, obtain scope-specific authorization and a rollback plan. After an authorized change, verify actual URLs, content and redirects, then monitor comparable periods for indexing, traffic and conversion effects. A before/after change still has confounders.

No file, page or redirect was changed in this example. The next action is to complete the evidence inventory for one group.
