## Module 4 Output: Voiceover Brief

**VOICEOVER BRIEF — "The 4% Rule Explained in 12 Minutes"**

**VOICE PROFILE**
- Tone: Authoritative but accessible — sounds like a knowledgeable friend, not a financial advisor
- Gender: Male or neutral
- Accent: American, neutral (no strong regional accent)
- Pace: 145–155 WPM — deliberate enough to feel credible, fast enough to maintain energy
- Emotion baseline: Calm, curious, slightly serious

**DELIVERY NOTES**
- Avoid: Monotone delivery on statistics. Vary pitch slightly on numbers to keep them interesting.
- Emphasis style: Light stress on key terms. Dramatic 0.5s pause on single-word reveals ("Almost." "Four percent.")
- Numbers: Deliver slowly — "four percent" not "4%." Pause after significant numbers.
- Short sentences: Treat single-word/short sentences as their own beat. "Almost." should land with weight, not be rushed.

**PRONUNCIATION FLAGS**
- FIRE: Spell it out as individual letters — "F-I-R-E" on first mention, then "fire" as a word after that
- 4%: Always say "four percent" — never "four point zero percent"
- Trinity Study: "TRIN-ity Study" — first syllable stressed

**SEGMENT-BY-SEGMENT NOTES**
- Hook (0:00–0:45): Slightly more deliberate pace. The hook's power comes from the pause after "Four percent." Let it breathe.
- Study history section: Slightly faster — factual summary that sets up the analysis
- Modern critique section: Slow back down. This is where viewers decide if they trust the channel.
- Outro: Warmer tone, slightly more conversational than the body
