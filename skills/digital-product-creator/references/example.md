### Product Structure

#### Template List

- **Batch Planning Dashboard** — Master calendar view that maps 6 weeks of content from a single session. Shows filming dates, upload dates, and topic slots at a glance.
- **Topic Bank** — Running database of video ideas with columns for hook strength, keyword research status, audience size estimate, and priority rank. Never start a batch session without content.
- **Script Builder Template** — Per-video template with hook, intro, main sections (3-5), outro CTA, and B-roll notes. Structured so you can write a complete script in 45 minutes.
- **Batch Day Planner** — Hour-by-hour schedule for the filming weekend. Includes gear checklist, lighting setup notes, outfit plan, and energy management blocks (built-in breaks, meal times).
- **Post-Production Tracker** — Tracks each video from "filmed" to "scheduled." Columns for edit status, thumbnail status, description written, scheduled time, and publish date.
- **90-Day Content Calendar** — High-level view connecting batch sessions to upload schedule. Shows how 4 batch sessions cover an entire quarter.

**Recommended price: $29**
