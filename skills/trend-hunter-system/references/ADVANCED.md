# Advanced Trend Research

## Cross-platform checks

Compare actual observations from search, video, social and news sources. Record their dates, geography, audience and metric definitions. A search suggestion and social clip are different signals; their combination is not automatically confirmed demand. Look for independent evidence rather than repeatedly citing the same originating story.

## Calendar planning

Ask for the creator's capacity and confirmed events. Use evergreen material, timely candidates and experiments in a mix the creator can sustain; no universal 60/30/10 ratio is required. Mark every trend slot that still needs verification. Calendar entries are drafts, not scheduled posts.

## Seasonal research

Use actual multi-period observations to identify recurring patterns. Check whether dates, geography and query definitions match. Holidays or announced events can anchor planning, but historical peaks do not guarantee a future peak. State what data is missing and when it should be refreshed.

## Collaboration with other skills

Pass the evidence table, source excerpts and uncertainty labels to the script or repurposing workflow. Do not pass a score as proof of a claim. The next workflow must preserve factual limitations and require actual media before claiming a product test or personal result.

## Retrospective

Log the selected hypothesis, evidence date, actual publication and comparable outcomes. Record failed ideas and source limitations as well as successes. A small number of wins cannot validate a general forecasting model.
