# Worked Example: A Negotiation Scenario, Not a Market Valuation

## Synthetic creator input

A tech creator reports 52,000 subscribers, 18,500 average views per video, 740 average likes and 240 average comments. They have completed two sponsorships, at $400 and $250. They want to price a dedicated video. Production cost, target margin, rights and brand budget are not supplied.

## Rate scenario

For illustration only, assume a $40 CPM asking-price model, a discretionary 1.5 scope/niche factor and a 1.15 adjustment. These are editable assumptions, not sourced market benchmarks.

18,500 / 1,000 × $40 = $740.
$740 × 1.5 × 1.15 = $1,276.50.
A discretionary ±15% negotiating band is $1,085.025 to $1,467.975, approximately $1,085–$1,468.

The supplied engagement calculation is (740 + 240) / 18,500 × 100 ≈ 5.30%, using views as the denominator. Do not compare it to a follower-based rate. This scenario does not prove the earlier deals were underpriced or that a buyer will accept the proposed range. A walk-away price requires the creator's actual costs, time, rights and preferences.

## Scope questions

Confirm production hours/costs, deliverable length and format, revisions, deadlines, distribution, usage rights, exclusivity, paid amplification and payment terms. Quote any rights or extra deliverables separately under agreed assumptions. Do not promise views, clicks or sales.

## Draft pitch

Hi [contact],

I run a tech channel with 52,000 subscribers and 18,500 average views per video during [confirmed period]. I would like to discuss a dedicated video about [verified relevant product angle].

My proposed scope is [confirmed deliverables and rights], with an asking price of [creator-approved amount]. I can provide links to approved prior work. Product use, audience demographics and campaign outcomes will be included only where I have evidence.

Would that fit your current campaign plans?

[Name and verified channel link]

## Media-kit outline

Include the supplied audience and engagement metrics with definitions, approved prior-work links, proposed scope and confirmed contact details. Leave demographics and sponsor results unknown. Do not claim the creator used the product for months, attach files that do not exist or describe a package as a guaranteed value for the brand.
