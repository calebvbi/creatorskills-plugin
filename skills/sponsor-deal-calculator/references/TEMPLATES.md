# Pitch Email & Media Kit Templates

## Pitch Email Templates

These are fill-in-the-blank templates. Replace everything in [brackets] with your real information. Each template is designed for a specific situation — pick the one that matches and customize it.

---

### Template 1: Cold Outreach to Dream Brand

Use when you're reaching out to a brand for the first time with no prior relationship.

**Subject line:** Quick idea for [Brand Name] x [Your Channel/Handle]

```
Hi [First Name — find the marketing manager's name on LinkedIn],

I've been [using/wearing/following] [Brand Name]'s [specific product]
for [time period] — [one specific thing you genuinely like about it].
[Optional: mention if your audience has asked about it or if you've
featured it organically before].

I create [content type] on [Platform] for [audience description]
([follower/subscriber count], [avg views], [engagement rate]%).
My audience is [demographic detail that matches the brand's target
customer].

I'd love to create [specific deliverable — e.g., "a dedicated review
video," "a 3-part Reel series," "a before-and-after transformation
post"] showing [specific angle — how you use the product, a use case
relevant to your audience, etc.].

[One proof point: past sponsorship result, high-performing related
post, or relevant audience stat].

My media kit is attached with full stats. Are you open to a quick
call this week?

[Your Name]
[Channel/Handle] | [Key metric]
[Business email]
```

---

### Template 2: Reply to Inbound Brand Inquiry

Use when a brand emails you first. The goal is to establish your value and move to a call before quoting rates.

**Subject line:** Re: [Their original subject line]

```
Hi [First Name],

Thanks for reaching out — I'm glad [Brand Name] is on my radar
because [genuine reason: you use the product, your audience matches,
the product fits your content style].

Before we dive into specifics, I'd love to learn more about what
you're looking for:

- What's the campaign timeline?
- Are you thinking [platform-specific content type] or open to ideas?
- Do you have a target launch date?

A few quick stats on my end: [follower count], [avg views per post],
[engagement rate]%, with an audience that's primarily [key demographic
that matches their brand].

I've attached my media kit with full details. Happy to hop on a
15-minute call to discuss the right format and scope.

What does your schedule look like this week?

[Your Name]
[Channel/Handle]
[Business email]
```

**Important:** Do not quote your rate in this email. Get on a call first — you'll have more context about their budget, goals, and deliverables, which puts you in a stronger negotiation position.

---

### Template 3: Rate Negotiation Counter-Offer

Use when a brand offers below your calculated rate. Stay professional, show the math, and offer alternatives.

**Subject line:** Re: [Their subject line] — Thoughts on pricing

```
Hi [First Name],

Thanks for sending over the details — I'm excited about this
partnership and think [Brand Name] is a great fit for my audience.

For a [specific deliverable they requested], my standard rate is
[your calculated rate]. That's based on my current average of
[avg views] views per [video/post] and a [engagement rate]%
engagement rate, which is [above/well above] the platform average.

I want to make sure I can dedicate the production time to create
something that genuinely performs for [Brand Name]. Here are a few
options that might work within your budget:

1. [Smaller deliverable] — [lower rate]
2. [Original deliverable with adjusted scope] — [mid rate]
3. [Original deliverable + bonus content] — [your full rate, framed
   as added value]

Happy to discuss which option works best. I'm flexible on structure
— I just want to make sure the content quality matches what your
brand deserves.

[Your Name]
```

### Template 4: Partnership Renewal Pitch

Use when you've already completed one campaign with a brand and want to continue the relationship.

**Subject line:** [Brand Name] x [Your Channel] — Round 2?

```
Hi [First Name],

It's been [time period] since our last collaboration and I wanted
to share some results and float an idea for round 2.

Here's how the last campaign performed:
- [Views/impressions on sponsored content]
- [Engagement metrics: likes, comments, saves, shares]
- [Click-throughs or conversions if you have them]
- [Any notable audience feedback — "my most-asked-about product
  this month," etc.]

My channel has grown since then — I'm now at [updated subscriber/
follower count] with [updated avg views] average views per [post/
video]. My engagement rate is [current rate]%.

For the next collaboration, I'd love to [propose specific idea:
sequel video, seasonal campaign, new product launch, etc.]. Based
on the results from last time and my current stats, my rate for
[specific deliverable] is [updated rate].

Want to set up a call to discuss? I have some creative ideas I
think you'll like.

[Your Name]
[Channel/Handle]
[Business email]
```

### Template 5: Multi-Platform Package Proposal

Use when you have presence on multiple platforms and want to increase total deal value by offering a cross-platform bundle.

**Subject line:** [Brand Name] content package — multi-platform idea

```
Hi [First Name],

I have an idea for [Brand Name] that goes beyond a single post.

My audience follows me across [Platform 1] ([count], [avg views])
and [Platform 2] ([count], [avg views]), and I've found that
cross-platform campaigns drive significantly more engagement
because each platform catches a different segment of my audience.

Here's what I'm proposing:

[Platform 1]:
- [Deliverable 1 with detail] — [rate]
- [Deliverable 2 with detail] — [rate]

[Platform 2]:
- [Deliverable 3 with detail] — [rate]

Package total: [total rate]
Bundled rate: [discounted total — typically 10-15% off individual
pricing]

Each piece of content would [tell a cohesive story / show a
different angle of the product / target a different audience
segment — pick the one that applies].

I've attached my media kit with full stats across both platforms.
Would you be open to discussing this over a quick call?

[Your Name]
[Handles for each platform]
[Business email]
```
