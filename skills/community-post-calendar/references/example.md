# Worked Example: A Week of Community Drafts

## Synthetic input

A gaming creator wants three community posts for a week. The only confirmed upcoming topic is monitor settings. No test duration, personal purchase story, completed experiment or release time is supplied.

## Monday poll

Which part of your monitor setup would you most like explained?
- Refresh rate
- Resolution
- Color settings
- How to check the settings I already have

Use the platform's current supported poll format after verifying it. This is a proposed question, not a measured audience preference.

## Midweek topic preview

I'm planning a video about monitor settings. What is one setting you've changed without being sure what it did?

Do not add "I spent 40 hours testing" or a publication time unless the creator confirms it.

## End-of-week discussion

What's one gaming purchase you would research differently next time? Share what you learned, if you're comfortable.

The creator can add a real personal story if they supply one. Do not invent a broken chair or money spent to make the post feel relatable.

## Follow-up plan

Review actual replies for useful questions, with no claim that a poll represents the whole audience. Choose a follow-up draft based on those replies. Nothing is published or scheduled by this example.
